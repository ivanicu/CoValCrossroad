# r14_paraphrase_gauge

**Is the satisfaction judge invariant to semantic-preserving rewording of a criterion?**

A gauge test, in the sense of the frontier protocol. The symmetry group is
paraphrase: rewording a criterion without changing what it asks for.

- the PROPERTY (does this response satisfy the criterion) **is** invariant
- the MEASUREMENT (the judge's Yes/No logit gap) — unknown

If the measurement is not invariant, then "prompt-specific criterion content" is
partly criterion *wording*, and the attribution number in r04/r10 is measuring
lexical coupling between criterion and response, not what the criterion asks.

This cuts world W_C, which r13 left standing.

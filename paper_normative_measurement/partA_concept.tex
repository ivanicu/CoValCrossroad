\section{What normative information is}
\label{sec:concept}

\why{The phrase is used in every alignment paper and defined in none. Six
definitions are in circulation, they disagree by orders of magnitude on the same
data, and five are refutable by construction. This section fixes the object
before anything is measured, because \cref{prop:Q} proves the measurement is
determined by the choice.}

\subsection{The naive picture, and the exact point of failure}

on an infinite set, not a substance with a quantity\\

\subsection{C6, stated}

\begin{definition}[Normative information]
\label{def:normativeinfo}
Fix $Q$, the family of questions the downstream system must answer. Then
$\Nn \sim_Q \Nn'$ iff $q(\Nn,x)=q(\Nn',x)$ for every $q\in Q$ and every $x$; the
normative information in $\Nn$ is the class $[\Nn]_Q$.
\end{definition}

\why{C1--C5 each exercised this freedom silently. C6 makes it an input that must
be published. That is the whole gain: not a better answer, an \emph{answerable}
question.}

\mesotakeaway{Declaring $Q$ is not a preliminary to the measurement. \textbf{It is
most of the result.} A framework reporting one percentage has chosen a $Q$ and
not disclosed it.}

\subsection{Where C6 lives formally, and what that buys}

\begin{table}[H]\centering\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{P{3.0cm}Y}
\toprule
move & take $Q$ to be decision problems rather than arbitrary queries --- no loss
of generality, since any query is posable as a decision\\
consequence & $\Nn\sim_Q\Nn'$ becomes Blackwell indistinguishability on that
family\\
the quantity & \emph{deficiency}
$\delta_Q=\sup_{d\in Q}\big[R_{\Nn'}(d)-R_{\Nn}(d)\big]$, not a percentage\\
\midrule
buys (i) & a \textbf{unit}: deficiency is in the units of the decision loss, so it
is comparable to what the decision is worth\\
buys (ii) & a \textbf{worst case}: a supremum over the declared family, not an
average over whichever queries were tried\\
buys (iii) & an \textbf{order}: Blackwell's relation is partial, so two standards
can be incomparable, and saying so beats forcing a rank\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

\subsection{Three source objects; merging them makes the question unanswerable}

\begin{table}[H]\centering\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{P{1.1cm}P{4.4cm}Y}
\toprule
$Z$ & the individual inputs $(\Nn_1,\dots,\Nn_n)$: who said what, who dissented,
who vetoed & aggregation \emph{necessarily} discards some of this
(\cref{thm:garble})\\
$G$ & $\Agg(Z)$, the collective norm under a rule $\Agg$ & loss here is a
\textbf{social choice}, not an error --- \emph{if} $\Agg$ was declared\\
$S$ & the authorised behaviour set after legitimate expert enrichment & loss here
is either domain necessity or machine distortion\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

\begin{proposition}[Without the layers, ``defect'' is undefined]
A loss between $Z$ and $G$ is a defect only if unlicensed by $\Agg$. If $\Agg$ is
undeclared, no observed loss can be classified, and ``the pipeline lost
information'' is indistinguishable from ``the pipeline did what it said''.
\end{proposition}

\mesoboundary{\textbf{CoVal declares no $\Agg$.} At this site, authorised social
choice and machine defect are \textbf{not separable}. Not a hard measurement
problem --- a missing line of documentation, and the cheapest thing a future
release could fix.}

\subsection{The conservation law: four classes, not one number}

\why{The deliverable of a theory of normative information is not a score. It is a
statement of what may be compressed and what may not --- the content is the
\emph{partition}, not the total.}

\begin{table}[H]\centering\footnotesize
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{P{3.0cm}P{4.6cm}Y}
\toprule
class & distinctions & requirement on the pipeline\\
\midrule
must be causally preserved & polarity; non-compensability; scope; exceptions &
intervening must move behaviour, \emph{in the predicted direction}\\
recoverable is enough & wording; non-load-bearing provenance; restatements &
must be decodable; need not steer\\
licensed to drop & duplicates; entailed criteria; phrasing variance & only if the
licence is declared \emph{at the layer where it happens}\\
type errors when lost & veto $\to$ scalar; uncertainty $\to$ certainty; right
$\to$ preference; expert constraint $\to$ public preference & a category change,
not degradation; \textbf{no continuous metric detects it}\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\caption{Rows 2--3 are assertions. Row 1 is partly measured (§\ref{sec:est}).
Row 4 is where \cref{thm:veto} sits.}
\label{tab:conservation}
\end{table}

\subsection{Why row four is the deepest}

\begin{table}[H]\centering\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{P{3.4cm}Y}
\toprule
the mechanism & a type change can leave the object arbitrarily close to the
source in \emph{every} continuous measure while answering a different question\\
the extreme case & a veto rendered as $-10$ and a strong dispreference rendered as
$-10$ are the \emph{same object}; there is nothing left to measure\\
where the difference lives & only in behaviour on candidate sets not yet seen ---
which is exactly \cref{thm:veto}\\
consequence & similarity metrics are structurally unable to audit this class, and
most alignment evaluation is similarity metrics\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

\mesotakeaway{Normative information is not a quantity flowing down a pipe. It is a
\textbf{partition of distinctions} into: must be causally preserved · must merely
be recoverable · may be dropped under a declared licence · \textbf{cannot be
represented at all in the chosen format}. The last class is invisible to every
similarity measure, and it is where a scalar scale fails.}

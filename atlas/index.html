The audit, as layers over the corpus

Every finding of the campaign, re-expressed as the set of prompts it is about, and painted onto the same cloud. z compares the layer's mean pairwise distance against 60 size-matched random draws — near zero means the finding is spread uniformly and is not a property of any domain; strongly negative means it concentrates somewhere real. Click a layer to paint it; click again to clear.

02

The twelve neighbourhoods, and how they differ

A cluster's name is the terms that most separate it from the rest of the corpus — computed, never assigned. The bars compare each neighbourhood against the corpus mean on seven measured axes, so a domain's character is a shape, not a label.

05

What each annotator did, in order

The order is the finding. Rankings come first; the criteria are written last — so a criterion is not independent evidence, it is the same person's standard, authored after they had already decided. of the people who scored a prompt's criteria also ranked it.

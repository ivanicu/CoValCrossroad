## ⛔ ④'s inertness is a DERIVATION, and I checked rather than reported it

`unique = 0` while a clause removes **57** arms is the shape of the arithmetic trap. Checked:
clause ②'s extension is **strictly contained** in clause ④'s under both comparators, at a minimum
R849 margin of **+0.0714** and **+0.0633** — comfortably clear, not knife-edge.

**So ④ adds nothing *given* ② by set containment**: everything that resolvably beats a prompt-blind

**in different units and are NOT compared**. What is checked is the containment, which is unit-free.
That units mismatch is the reason this round reads committed memberships instead of re-deriving them.

arm on a clause nobody scored it for.

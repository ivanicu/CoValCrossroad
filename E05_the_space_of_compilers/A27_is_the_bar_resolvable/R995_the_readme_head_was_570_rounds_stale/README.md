## ⚠ What was NOT done, and why saying so matters

**The 829 missing summaries were not written.** One round cannot, and promising them would restate
the debt as a plan — which is how a debt survives being noticed. The head now carries:

- the corrected counts, **computed by this round rather than typed**
- the size of the remaining debt, and the gate that names it
- an explicit instruction to treat any count below that is **not** gate-derived as of unknown vintage

That last line is the load-bearing one. The file's other numbers were not audited here, and leaving
them unmarked beside a freshly-corrected head would let the correction launder them.

instrument and its own control. A partial sweep presented as a fix is worse than a stated debt.

as such. Annotating beats deleting — a reader can now see both the number and its vintage.

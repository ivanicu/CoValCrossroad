# R956 · the qualifier rule turns the read into something checkable

**The decision this made safe.** Whether R955's 220 candidates need a read by the document's own
author — self-review, the weakest evidence available — or can be reduced by a rule anyone can check.

**What it rests on.** Three successive filters on 220 disagreeing pairs: 140 EXPLAINED because the
phrases name different qualifiers, 59 UNDECIDABLE kept as its own category, 20 REAL CANDIDATE, 15
surviving the bracket and both-qualifier artifacts, 6 at comparable magnitude. The population was
reproduced against R955's committed counts rather than re-derived.

**The alternatives.** Reading the 220 — rejected, because a verdict I produce by reading my own
document is what this programme has retracted most often.

**What it does not do.** A matching qualifier set does not prove the same quantity: nine of fifteen
survivors are two orders of magnitude apart, a score against a gap.

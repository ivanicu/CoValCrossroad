# R1004 · the formulation, and both of its conditions bind

**THE DECISION THIS MAKES SAFE.** Whether this arc produced a definition or only a pile of refuted
wordings. **It produced a definition** — two conditions, both load-bearing, admitting its own
instance, stable where the release has the prompts for it.

---

## Why not R1003's NEXT as written

R1003 asked what the extension **loses** when ④ is demoted. ⭐ **That is the arithmetic trap.** R1000
measured ④'s unique removals as **0**, and *"removes nothing uniquely"* ⇒ *"dropping it changes
nothing"* **by definition**. The answer is forced by the algebra. It is computed here anyway as a
**bookkeeping control** and labelled a DERIVATION — it could not have come out otherwise.

⭐ **The round is the product instead.** §0.2: a programme that only kills its own claims has a
perfect model of a subject it has produced nothing about.

## The formulation

> An arm is a **CORE** iff
> **②** it **resolvably beats a NAMED prompt-blind comparator** — the 2.5th percentile of the
> bootstrapped paired difference is > 0 — **and**
> **③** it **consumes no prompt-specific human labels.**
>
> **Reported, never required:**
> **①** its **size** (max realised) — R1000: **0** unique removals, so it describes rather than filters;
> **④** its **margin over a declared response-only class**, as a **lower bound with its interval** —
> R1003: as a filter the clause is **vacuous or empty at every setting**.

## ⚠ What this cannot show — the limit of the whole arc

**That the extension is the RIGHT one.** That needs an external standard for what a core is, and
**the release ships none.** Its own card: *"Core is a proof of concept … an invitation for others to
develop and validate better synthesis and aggregation methods."*

So the claim is bounded exactly here: **this formulation is coherent and non-vacuous on this
release.** It is not validated against a ground truth, because none exists to validate against — and
saying so is not modesty, it is the scope.

**Cross-dataset — N/A.** One release; it would require a second carrying human rankings over the same
response sets.

## Alternatives considered

**Keep ① and ④ as conditions and report the definition as four clauses.** Refused on measurement:
R1000 put ①'s unique removals at 0 and R1003 showed ④ has no working setting. A clause that cannot
filter is a description, and calling it a condition overstates what the definition does.

**Run R1003's NEXT literally.** Refused — it is a derivation, and running it as a round would have
produced a number that could not have been otherwise, which is the failure this standard opens by
naming.

# R953 · three near-zeros are three silences until each probe has a positive control

**The decision this made safe.** Whether the ledger's measured emptiness is a fact about the ledger
or about the instruments — a zero from a probe never shown to return non-zero is silence.

**What it rests on.** Nine cells: three probes × {real, synthetic, stripped}. Each probe recovers a
planted structure at a synthetic rate of 0.995000 or better and returns 0.000000 on a stripped twin,
while the real corpus gives 0.007000, 0.093000 and 0.017000.

**The alternatives.** Running the retraction-latency design instead — killed in scoping: 19 of 1,149
entries carry a claim-to-killer pair, so that estimand is unidentified rather than under-powered.

**What it does not do.** The synthetic shows the probes CAN see; it cannot show that what they see is
what the real entries would have said had they been structured.

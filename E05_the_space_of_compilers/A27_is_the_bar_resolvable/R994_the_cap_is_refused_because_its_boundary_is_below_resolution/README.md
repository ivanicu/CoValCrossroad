# R994 · the cap is refused, and the reason is measured rather than tasteful

**THE DECISION THIS MAKES SAFE.** Whether clause ① gains the card's upper bound. **It does not** —
and the reason is not that the card is only an invitation, but that **the cap's boundary sits below
this release's resolution.**

---

## The decision is authorial; only its reason is measured

R993 priced the cap at exactly 2 arms and said what remained was the choice R987 modelled — *decide
it, or record why it stays open, and deciding is not a measurement.* Agreed. So the round measured
the one thing that could make the decision evidenced instead of tasteful: **are the arms the cap
would exclude resolvably different from the instance it would keep?**

| arm | size | margin vs `coval_core` | 95% CI | resolvable? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **`topw_k6`** | 6 | −0.002360 | [−0.00786, +0.00328] | **NO** |
| `topw_k8` | 8 | −0.007166 | [−0.01318, −0.00114] | yes (worse) |

**`topw_k6` is indistinguishable from the instance the cap would keep.** So adopting the cap asserts
a distinction this release cannot support — **the failure §4 records as *a definition that names a
number it cannot resolve*, which is how the number "four" got in here the first time.**

**DECISION: REFUSE the cap.** The departure stays open, deliberately, with the reason recorded.

## Controls

| control | result |
|---|---|
| **POSITIVE** | the statement's own parenthetical reproduces — `topw_k3` vs `topw_k8` unresolvable (margin −0.003865, CI [−0.00997, +0.00219]). **The premise was checked, not assumed** |
| **NEGATIVE** | `random_k4_s0` is resolvably below the instance at −0.073790 — the instrument *can* resolve, so the null on `topw_k6` is a measurement and not silence |
| **PLACEBO** | `coval_core` against itself: margin exactly 0, not resolvable |
| **SEEDS** | 3, unanimity required for "resolvable" |

⭐ The positive control matters most here: **the whole decision rests on the 3–8 claim**, and that
claim lives in the statement. R993 had a committed table refuse a derivation one round ago; checking
this one before leaning on it is the same discipline.

## ⚠ The half of the evidence that points the other way

**`topw_k8` IS resolvably worse than the instance and still clears clause ②.** That is a real
observation and it is reported rather than dropped — but excluding it is **available to the bar on
the metric** and needs no size clause. Reading it as an argument *for* the cap would mean taking the
one arm the evidence separates and generalising from it, which is the shape of most of this
project's retractions.

## What would reopen this

**A release whose design resolves sizes 3 from 8.** That condition is written into the statement,
not the verdict — because the verdict is contingent on it and a later reader needs the trigger, not
the conclusion.

## Alternatives considered

**Adopt the cap anyway, since the card sets one.** Refused on the evidence above: matching the card's
form while asserting a distinction the data cannot carry buys fidelity at the price of the exact
error the card's own "four" is an instance of.

**Leave the departure unrecorded as "open".** Refused: R991 established that an unstated position is
a position by omission. *Open with a measured reason* and *open because nobody decided* are different
states, and only the first is legible to a later reader.

# R233 — the candidate-set test, and why I am not taking its verdict

**Arc E05·A18.** The test everyone said was impossible, on 250 prompts × 4 **freshly generated**
responses cached in this repository since 2026-07-28 (`RETRACTIONS.md` entry 96). 33,320 judgements,
GPU via pueue, both arms judged in the same run.

## What the script printed

```
core preserves Full's class, ORIGINAL : 0.3520   floor 0.4044 [0.3440, 0.4520]
core preserves Full's class, FRESH    : 0.4680   floor 0.4166 [0.3680, 0.4640]
transport delta                       : +0.1160
verdict: SURVIVES -- the compilation transports to a new candidate set
```

## Why that verdict is not admissible

**Three things are wrong with it, and one of them is the point of the round.**

**1 — the positive control missed.** The original arm was re-judged in the same run precisely so the
*only* difference from R231 would be the candidate set. R231 measured **0.3864**; this run gives
**0.3520**, `Δ = −0.0344`. n differs (250 here, 968 there) and the miss sits inside the floor's own
draw spread, but I registered "must reproduce R231" **without a tolerance**, so the control neither
clearly passes nor clearly fails. That is my error in the pre-registration, not a result.

**2 — the margin is 0.0040.** `0.4680` against a floor whose draws reach `0.4640`. The floor's spread
is `0.096` wide. A verdict resting on four thousandths at the edge of a ten-point band is not a
verdict.

**3 — the arms differ in difficulty, and the floors say so.** The random-4 floor is **higher on
fresh** (0.4166) than on original (0.4044). *Random selection also does better on the fresh
responses.* The fresh set was generated by one model at one temperature with one length cap — it is
**more homogeneous**, so fewer criteria suffice to reproduce its ordering.

> **The design conflated "unseen" with "equally hard". They are different, and the floors caught it.**

## What can be said, normalised against each arm's own floor

| arm | core | floor | core − floor |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| original | 0.3520 | 0.4044 | **−0.0524** |
| fresh | 0.4680 | 0.4166 | **+0.0514** |

A difference-in-differences of **+0.104** — the right normalisation, and it still carries the
population confound, because a difference of differences between two populations is only clean if
the *effect of the population* is additive, which nothing here establishes.

**Verdict: `UNVERIFIED`.** Not "the compilation transports" and not "it fails to". The measurement
was run, it is the first of its kind at this site, and its own controls say the arms are not
comparable as built.

## What would settle it

Match the arms on difficulty rather than assuming it. Concretely: **resample fresh responses to match
the original set's spread of Full-scores per prompt**, or generate the fresh set with the same
model/temperature diversity the released four were curated for. Both are runnable here — the
generator, the seed and the parameters are all recorded in `a12_fresh_generations.json`.

## What still cannot be measured

The fresh responses carry **no human rankings**. This measures transport of the **compilation** and
never agreement with **people**, and R12's own artifact says so in its `outcome_variable_scope`
field. That register entry does not move.

## The sentence that can no longer be written

*"Candidate-set generalisation is structurally undetectable here."* It is detectable, it has now
been attempted once, and the attempt's own controls are what tell us the design was not yet right.

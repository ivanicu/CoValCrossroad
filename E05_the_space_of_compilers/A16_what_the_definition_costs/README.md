## Why the arc was opened rather than folded into A13

A13's three arcs all closed on *what the instrument can resolve*. This one closes on **what the
definition is willing to pay**, which is a different kind of question: no measurement settles it,
because it is a commitment about what the word is for. What a measurement *can* settle is the size
of the bill, and that is the only thing this arc tries to do.

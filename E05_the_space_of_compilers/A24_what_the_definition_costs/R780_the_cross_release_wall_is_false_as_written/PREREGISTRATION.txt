--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE ARITHMETIC, DERIVED BEFORE MEASURING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D1  A 2-RESPONSE UNIT CANNOT BE POOLED WITH A 4-RESPONSE UNIT. Its statistic takes values in {0, 1}
    with variance 0.25; a 4-response unit takes values in {0, 1/6, ..., 1} with variance at most
    0.25/6 under independence. Pooling inflates the standard error by a factor set by the mixture,
    not by the effect. **This is why the stratum exists**, and it is a DERIVATION, not a finding.
D2  RELEASE 2's MDE WILL EXCEED RELEASE 1's EVEN AT EQUAL n, because 672 of 7,344 interactions carry
    a score TIE, and a tie contributes a zero to the sign vector that no arm can match. Predicted
    direction only; the magnitude is measured.
D3  E1's two halves are not symmetric evidence. A wall asserted BEFORE R556 is a claim that was true
    as far as its author knew. One asserted AFTER is a correction that did not reach the artifact.
    **Only the second count is about my loop**, and it is the one to report.

by the same producer but on a different corpus, so a failure to transport could be the GENERATOR

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONTROLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OBJECT    `transport_generic` and release-1 `generic` resolve to the same core file; the n=4 stratum
          is non-empty; the two releases' id spaces are disjoint. Exit 2 otherwise.
POSITIVE  plant a known effect by mixing a fraction w of the target's own sign vector into the blind
          arm's predicted scores, sweep w, and require recovery on BOTH releases. BAND, COMPUTED:
          floor at w=0 (must return ~0, the g=0 cell) and ceiling at w=1 (the arm becomes the target
          and the contrast saturates); the threshold is required to lie strictly between two
          COMPUTED ends, never typed.
g=0       the blind arm against a byte-identical copy of its own core: exactly 0.
NEGATIVE  permute the target across units, 200 draws, both releases -- the world excluded is
          "any pair of distinct cores separates this much".
SHAM      ingredient ABSENT, not inverted: the blind reference against ANOTHER prompt-blind arm
          (`transport_randblind_s*`), i.e. the same contrast with prompt-specificity removed rather
          than misdirected. Reported beside the arm's own sham so benefit and harm are separated.
PLACEBO   an arm against itself = exactly 0.
SEARCH    E1's regex is an instrument: a POSITIVE control requires it to flag lines whose status is
          known (R779's own register line, which I wrote), and a NEGATIVE control requires it NOT to
          flag the correctly-scoped lines already in the arc ("the partition and n are this
          release's", "one released core, and its sham is ours").
UNIT      E1 instrument unit = a regex match in a round artifact; claim unit = a line in a round
          artifact. E2 instrument unit = an interaction (r2) / a prompt (r1); claim unit = a RELEASE.

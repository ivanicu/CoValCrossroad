R828 · the controls that were never run
registered 2026-08-06, BEFORE execution. Committed before run.py is executed.

WHY THIS ROUND EXISTS
  assurance/a_control_that_cannot_fail.py found 6 arc rounds whose registered control is
  algebraically constant. R436 was adjudicated last round: its intent was SOUND and the
  implementation collapsed it to `x - x`; running the real check PASSED, so the round shipped an
  assurance it never possessed while its numbers stood. R436 also proved the adjudication cannot be
  inferred -- it is per-round. This round adjudicates the remaining five.

THE DISCRIMINATOR, named before looking at the answers
  A collapsed control is RECOVERABLE iff a SECOND PRODUCER of the same quantity exists in the
  round. R436 was recoverable because two scoring call sites existed and the check named their
  agreement. A control that subtracts a STORED SCALAR from itself has no second path and its
  INTENT is a derivation, not an implementation slip.

ESTIMAND
  For each recoverable round: the value its registered control NAMES, computed through that
  round's own second producer, at full precision. Named before the method.

IDENTIFICATION
  Identified where a second producer exists (3 of 5). Not identified for R361; reported as
  UNRECOVERABLE, never as PASS and never as FAIL.

WORLDS
  W-EMPTY-ASSURANCE  all three recovered checks pass. The rounds' numbers stand; what was absent
                     was the assurance. Predicts: 3/3 pass.
  W-MASKED-DEFECT    at least one fails. The constant control hid a real defect and that round's
                     numbers inherit it. Predicts: >=1 fail, and it localises to one producer.

POSITIVE CONTROL -- and it is the whole point of the round
  For each producer, build a PERTURBED version whose defect the registered property forbids, then
  require BOTH of:
    (a) the RECOVERED check FAILS on the perturbed producer      -- it has power
    (b) the ORIGINAL `x - x` check still PASSES on it            -- the original had none
  (b) is what makes this a measurement of the collapse rather than an assertion about it.
  Perturbations: R332 `e >= 0` for `e > 0` (a row then beats itself); R731 a fresh seed per call;
  R672 a nondeterministic element in the returned set.

NEGATIVE CONTROL / g=0
  Unperturbed producers: the recovered check must PASS. If it fails at g=0 the recovery is
  mis-specified and nothing about the original round may be concluded.

KILL -- a CONDITIONAL, not a threshold (R826's lesson; a kill that can fire on a broken
instrument is an automated way to publish an artifact)
  if all 3 positive controls fire AND all 3 g=0 arms are null:
      evaluate: 3/3 recovered checks pass -> W-EMPTY-ASSURANCE ; else -> W-MASKED-DEFECT
  else:
      UNVERIFIED -- never OVERTURNED, never CONFIRMED.

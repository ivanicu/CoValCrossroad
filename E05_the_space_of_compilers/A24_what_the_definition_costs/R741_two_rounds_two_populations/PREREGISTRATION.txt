   R740's NEXT proposed extrapolating the prompt count needed to resolve a two-hundredth shortfall,

   populations differ by 234 prompts.

   So R740 did not price R738's numbers. It silently corrected the pool and priced DIFFERENT numbers,
   and my report read that difference as a sign flip from sampling noise. That sentence is wrong and
   is retracted in this round regardless of what else it finds.

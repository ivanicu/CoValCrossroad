⛔ THE ARITHMETIC, DERIVED BEFORE MEASURING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FORCED, labelled derivations, not evidence:
  - a pair with an empty joint bin cannot be stratified on that bin. That is definitional, not a
    measurement, and E1 is reported as structure rather than as a result.
  - splitting 118 figures into two arms gives arms near 59, so the MDE will be roughly twice that of

    undefined rate must never be plotted as 0.

citations to rounds that were themselves less rigorous -- so an era effect there could be a property

the effect belongs to the cited rounds and not to any document's practice.

CONTROLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
POSITIVE   the MONOTONE LADDER, as in R752 and R753: rejection must rise across plants 0, m/2, m, 2m.
           Asserting a searched MDE rejects at 0.80 is circular; the ORDER is not. BAND, COMPUTED:
           floor = rejection at zero plant, ceiling = rejection at a maximal plant.
g=0        zero planted difference rejects at ~alpha.
NEGATIVE   shuffle the OLD/NEW label across `DEFINITION.md`'s figures, holding the figures fixed. The
           difference must collapse. World excluded: "any split of this document shows this gap".
SHAM       the same operation with the INGREDIENT ABSENT, not inverted. Ingredient = the era split.
           Split `DEFINITION.md` at a RANDOM cited-round threshold unrelated to R450, over 5 draws,
           and report the spread of differences those give. A real era effect must exceed what an
           arbitrary split produces.
PLACEBO    each arm's rate computed twice -> exactly 0 difference, reported as 0 of N.
NOISE      3 simulation seeds and 5 sham splits; spreads reported, never averaged into one number.
UNIT       instrument unit = a FIGURE; claim unit = an ERA's practice. Figures cluster within lines,
           so the distinct LINE count per arm is reported and the MDE is ALSO computed on lines as
           the conservative reading, exactly as R753 did.

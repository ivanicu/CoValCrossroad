R826 · IS CLAUSE ④ EVALUABLE WITHOUT A STATED MODELLING BUDGET?

CHECK #428. R825's NEXT asked whether any reading of ④ both BINDS and ADMITS the released core.
⛔ THE ARITHMETIC RUNG ANSWERS IT FROM DATA IN HAND: R824's 14-lexical-feature permissive class
excludes 25 of 58 AND admits `coval_core` at +0.0468 [+0.0326, +0.0609]. Such a reading already
exists and I exhibited it two rounds ago. The question was mis-posed -- the FOURTH NEXT line in four
rounds to be answerable without an experiment.

WHAT THE CHECK FOUND INSTEAD. ④'s verdict on the released core is a function of MODELLING EFFORT:
    strict, 30 hand-built rules   bar 0.455679  -> binds on NOTHING
    R824, 14 lexical features     bar 0.519689  -> binds on 25, ADMITS the core
    R825, + char 3-5-grams        bar 0.572335  -> binds on more, EXCLUDES the core
Nothing separates these but how many features the modeller built. If the bar does not saturate,
④ has no verdict at all without a budget nobody has any principled way to set.

ESTIMAND
  E1  the held-out bar as a function of modelling effort k = number of SVD components over char
      3-5-gram TF-IDF, k in {0,5,10,20,40,60,100,150,200}; k=0 is lexical-only
  E2  ⭐ at each k, the PAIRED difference against `coval_core` on the same evaluation halves, with
      a CI -- and the crossing k* where the verdict flips from ADMITS to EXCLUDES
  E3  saturation: is there a k beyond which the bar stops rising by more than the noise floor?
  E4  the decision: is ④ evaluable, and if not, what must replace it

DERIVED FIRST -- BEFORE ANY COMPUTE
  D1  ⛔ IN-SAMPLE monotonicity in k is FORCED (a k-truncation is nested in a k+1-truncation). The
      HELD-OUT curve is NOT forced -- R823 measured a held-out bar FALLING as its class grew from 6
      to 30 rules. Every figure here is held out for exactly that reason.
  D2  ⛔ A CROSSING EXISTS BY CONSTRUCTION. k=0 reproduces R824's 0.5197 which ADMITS the core;
      k=100 reproduces R825's 0.5723 which EXCLUDES it. Given those endpoints a crossing k* must
      lie between them. SO FINDING A CROSSING IS NOT A FINDING. What is measurable is WHERE it
      sits, how sharp it is, and whether the curve saturates on either side of it.
  D3  k* is a property of THIS feature family on THIS release. It is not a property of "core", and
      no number here transfers to another family without being re-measured.
  D4  Truncated-SVD components are NESTED, so one SVD(200) fit per split serves every k by slicing.
      That is an exact identity, not an approximation, and it is what makes the sweep affordable.

WORLDS
  A  SATURATES ABOVE -- the bar plateaus above `coval_core`. ④ has a well-defined limit verdict and
     it EXCLUDES the released core: the clause is decidable and the definition is empty of its own
     instance.
  B  SATURATES BELOW -- plateaus below the core. ④ admits it at the limit, and R825's exclusion was
     an artifact of one particular k.
  C  NO SATURATION -- the bar climbs past every tested k without a plateau. ④ IS NOT EVALUABLE
     without a stated modelling budget, and its verdict is set by where the modeller stops. The
     clause must be replaced rather than repaired.

PREDICTION MATRIX
  A -> bar(200) - bar(150) <= noise floor AND bar(200) - core CI excludes 0 above
  B -> bar(200) - bar(150) <= noise floor AND bar(200) - core CI excludes 0 below
  C -> bar(200) - bar(150) > noise floor, curve still rising at the largest k tested

CONTROLS
  OBJECT     k=0 must reproduce R824's 0.519689 and k=100 R825's ~0.5723, each to within the
             per-split noise floor on this design. Exit 2 otherwise -- 9 new cells are unvalidated
             until the two cells whose answers are already known come back right.
  PLACEBO    at every k, `coval_core` against ITSELF -> exactly 0.0.
  POSITIVE   dose-response scored AGAINST THE PLANT (R824's corrected form): a synthetic target
             that is a known linear function of the features, g in {1.0,0.5,0.2,0.0}, monotone,
             recovering at g=1 and at chance at g=0.
  NEGATIVE   labels shuffled at fit, at the largest k -> must land at chance, not above.
  SHAM ⭐    THE SAME SWEEP ON RANDOM FEATURES, matched in dimension at every k. A max over more
             components rises on noise alone; the real curve is attributable only in EXCESS of the
             sham curve, and a sham measured at one k licenses nothing about the others.
  NOISE FLOOR  per k, across splits.

MULTIPLICITY   9 k-values x 2 (real, sham) = 18 curve cells, plus 9 paired tests against the core.
               BH q=0.05 over the paired family; non-survivors printed.

PRE-REGISTERED KILL -- conditional, gated on the controls
  if placebo == 0 at every k and positive recovers and fails at g=0 and negative at chance:
      if bar(kmax) - bar(k_prev) > noise_floor(kmax):                       WORLD C
      elif paired(kmax) CI excludes 0 and mean > 0:                         WORLD A
      elif paired(kmax) CI excludes 0 and mean < 0:                         WORLD B
      else:                                                                 UNVERIFIED
  else:
      UNVERIFIED   -- never OVERTURNED, never CONFIRMED

STOPPING RULE   WORLD C forces the deliverable to state that ④ is not evaluable as written. Any
outcome lands in DEFINITION.md's head, its clause table, STATEMENT.md and the ledger IN ONE COMMIT.

BUDGET   one SVD(200) fit per split serves all nine k by slicing (D4), so the sweep is ~8 vectoriser
fits, not 72. Under 20 min. Two seeds byte-identical or it does not ship.

COMMIT DISCIPLINE   this preregistration and run.py are committed BEFORE the run, per R825, where an
untracked round survived only because of a manual backup.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
R765's NEXT proposed that the pool tensor's COLUMN ORDER might not be the core JSON's LIST ORDER,
which would make `POOL[0:4]` -- the comparator behind every ② verdict -- the wrong four criteria.

⛔ CHECK #368 KILLED THAT HYPOTHESIS AND TWO MORE, ALL BEFORE ANY ROUND WAS BUILT:
 ① ORDER. `score.py:58` does `o[pid][(int(i), ltr)] = ...` -- the index is an **int**, so `sorted()`
   is numeric and `idxs[0:4] == [0,1,2,3]`. No lexicographic bug. HYPOTHESIS DEAD.
 ② CONTEXT. Maybe satisfaction depends on which OTHER criteria were scored in the same call. The
   `topw_k{1,2,3,4,6,8,12}` family is NESTED (verified on all 968 prompts), and the top-1 criterion's
   satisfaction is **identical to 9 decimals at every k**. HYPOTHESIS DEAD.
 ③ JUDGE IDENTITY. R424/R426's anchor test cannot identify `generic`'s judge: its criteria are the
   generic pool's and appear in NO full table, so the positive control (`topw_k4` vs `sat_full`)
   returns **0.1087**, near the ~0.03 coincidence floor. THE INSTRUMENT IS INADMISSIBLE HERE (P5's
   star rule: a `not found` from an instrument never shown to fire on this class is silence).

WHAT SURVIVES IS THE MEASUREMENT ITSELF, AND IT CONTRADICTS TWO PUBLISHED ZEROS.

  R765 published: "same judge, identical criteria -> |Δ A2| = 0.0000 on 10 pairs, 968/968 exact."

⛔ THE ARITHMETIC, DERIVED BEFORE MEASURING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D1  A PAIR BUILT TO BE IDENTICAL RETURNS ZERO BY CONSTRUCTION. `_detA`/`_detB` are determinism
    controls; that they agree is what their name asserts, not what a measurement found. Counting them
    as evidence about scoring variance is circular. THE MEASUREMENT IS HOW MANY PAIRS ARE **NOT** OF
    THAT KIND, and what they say.
D2  |Δ A2| ≤ propagation of |Δ satisfaction| THROUGH A SIGN FUNCTION, so a large cell-level
    discrepancy can produce a small A2 discrepancy whenever it does not flip a pairwise ordering.
    "A2 barely moved" is therefore NOT evidence that the values agree, and R765 read it as such.

STRONGEST CONFOUND, written BEFORE the run: the 957 differing prompts might carry a systematic
OFFSET (one artifact uniformly higher), which would indicate a different judge or temperature rather
than run-to-run noise. CONTROL IN THE SAME ITERATION: report the SIGNED mean alongside the absolute
one, and the share of cells where `generic` exceeds `pool` -- 0.5 means symmetric noise, far from 0.5
means a systematic shift.

WORLDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A · THE ZEROS WERE SCOPE ERRORS. Every zero-floor pair is within-pass; the one across-pass pair says
    the floor is NOT zero. Both published zeros are re-scoped to "within a scoring pass", and the
    across-pass floor becomes a number this campaign has never had.
B · THE DISCREPANCY IS NOT SCORING. The signed mean is far from zero -- a systematic offset -- so the
    two artifacts differ by JUDGE or SETTING, not by run-to-run variance, and the floor question is
    still open with a different instrument required.
C · THE PAIR IS NOT WHAT I THINK. The core JSONs do not describe what the sat files scored, and E1
    measures two different criterion sets. Unfalsifiable here (see IMPOSSIBLE) and therefore the
    round's stated ceiling on what it may claim.

CONTROLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
POSITIVE  the nested `topw_k{1..12}` family: a criterion present at every k must have |Δ| = 0 to
          1e-12 across all of them. BAND, COMPUTED: an instrument that calls everything different
          fails this; one that calls everything identical fails the NEGATIVE below. Unreachable from
          either degenerate end.
g=0       `sat_generic` compared against itself: exactly 0 differing cells of 15,488.
NEGATIVE  two DIFFERENT criteria (`generic[0]` vs `generic[1]`) must differ on a large share of
          cells -- the world excluded is "these satisfaction values are so coarse that anything
          matches anything".
SHAM      ingredient ABSENT, not inverted. Ingredient = CRITERION IDENTITY. Compare `generic` to a
          size-matched arm whose criteria are NOT identical (`gen`, overlap 0.0010) and report its
          |Δ| distribution. If identical-criteria and different-criteria pairs have the same |Δ|,
          then criterion identity buys nothing and E1 is not about scoring at all.
PLACEBO   `topw_k4` vs `topw_k4_detA` at the CELL level (not the A2 level): must be exactly 0.
NOISE     E1 IS the noise measurement; its own cell count and quantiles are the report.
UNIT      instrument unit = a CELL (prompt x criterion x response); claim unit = an A2 NUMBER.
          15,488 vs 1. Never conflated -- D2 says the map between them is a sign function.

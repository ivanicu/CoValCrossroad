ESTIMAND
   What generates the spread of r = SE_ci/SE_mde, whose range sets R726's disagreement-zone width?
   Three candidate sources, and the third was not in R726's NEXT line at all:
     (a) the arm's sample size n
     (b) skewness of the per-prompt difference distribution, proxied by the bootstrap interval's
         asymmetry
     (c) THE MONTE-CARLO ERROR OF THE BOOTSTRAP ITSELF at NBOOT = 1200
   If (c) dominates, the zone width is a property of a CONSTANT IN THE CODE, not of the arms, and
   R726's "the spread would have to double" is a statement about a choice rather than about data.

IDENTIFICATION
   (a) BARELY IDENTIFIED AND SAID SO. n takes two values across 41 arms -- 968 for 39 and 398 for 2
       -- so only 4 of 82 cells sit at the minority level. Any n coefficient rests on 4 points and
       will be reported as UNIDENTIFIED-IN-PRACTICE with the count attached, never as a null.
   (b) identified as a correlation, but CONFOUNDED BY CONSTRUCTION: r and the asymmetry are two
       functionals of the SAME 1200 resamples, so a low draw of the lower percentile widens the
       interval and skews it at once. The confound is measured by simulating a ZERO-SKEW world and
       reading corr(r, asym) there; if it is non-zero under normality, the observed correlation
       carries no information about real skewness and must not be reported as if it did.
   (c) identified by simulation, and separately by an asymptotic derivation, which are two
       independent routes to the same quantity.

POSITIVE CTRL   plant real skew: simulate LOGNORMAL differences at the same n and B, and require
                (i) corr(r, asym) to move materially away from its normal-world value and (ii) the
                asymmetry itself to shift. An instrument that cannot detect planted skew cannot
                license a null about skew. threshold computed from the normal world's own spread,
                not chosen: |corr_skew - corr_normal| must exceed 3 simulation SDs of corr_normal.
g=0             the normal world IS the g=0 arm: with zero skew planted, the asymmetry's mean must
                be indistinguishable from 0 and the control must NOT fire.
DOSE-RESPONSE   B in {300, 1200, 4800, 19200}; sd(r) must fall, and the fitted log-log slope must
                be near -0.5. A spread that does not shrink with B is not Monte-Carlo error.
NEGATIVE CTRL   permute r against (n, asym) and recompute every correlation; all must fall to their
                permutation null. excluded world: "these covariates explain r".
SHAM            regress r on a random covariate matched in mean and sd to asym -- the same operation
                with the ingredient ABSENT, not inverted -- and require R^2 at the null.
PLACEBO         r against a constant column -> R^2 exactly 0.
NOISE FLOOR     every simulated sd(r) is reported with its own Monte-Carlo error from the replicate
                count, measured, not assumed; replicate counts differ by B and are printed.
MULTIPLICITY    4 B levels x 2 distributions x {sd(r), corr(r,asym), mean asym} + 3 observed
                regressions; all reported, survivors and non-survivors alike.
SPECIFICATION   distribution (normal, lognormal) x B (4 levels) x estimator (sd, IQR-based scale)

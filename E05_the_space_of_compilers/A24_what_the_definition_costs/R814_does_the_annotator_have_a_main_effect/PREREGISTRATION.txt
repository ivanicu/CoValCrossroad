R814 · DOES THE ANNOTATOR HAVE A MAIN EFFECT — is clause ③ holding out the right thing?
written before the run · NPERM 500 · NBOOT 1200

PROVOCATION
R813 measured design effects of 1.14-1.30 and found, via a NEGATIVE control that separated by only
0.0002 against an sd of 0.0007, that the annotator panel carries almost no shared error. Its NEXT
called that a TENSION with R793's CEIL_H = 0.551880 -- annotators agreeing pairwise only 55% of the
time while their errors look independent -- and proposed an ICC to resolve it.

⛔ CHECK #416 KILLED THE FRAMING WITH A THREE-LINE GAUGE TEST, ZERO REAL DATA. Simulating a crossed
panel at rater_sd = 0.0 / 0.15 / 0.35 gives pairwise agreement 0.6230 / 0.6244 / 0.6223 -- FLAT --
while the rater ICC goes 0.0002 / 0.1751 / 0.3803. **Pairwise agreement and the rater main effect are
INDEPENDENT quantities.** There was never a tension; R813's closing line asserted one. That is
realstat §4's "the closing sentence is a claim and never gets a control", committed in a NEXT written
one round after this project filed a ledger entry about that exact failure mode.

⭐ BUT THE UNDERLYING QUESTION SURVIVES AND IS DECISION-RELEVANT, and the gauge test just supplied
its positive control: the statistic discriminates 0.0002 from 0.3803 at known planted rater_sd.
Clause ③ of the definition holds out the ANNOTATOR. If annotators have no main effect, that holdout
protects against a source of variance that barely exists.

ESTIMAND
E1  the EXCESS variance of annotator mean agreement over a LABEL-PERMUTATION null -- identified by
    construction, since the permutation destroys the rater effect and preserves everything else.
E2  ⭐ the rater share: excess / total variance of the (prompt, annotator) agreement table.
E3  ⭐ the dose-response: plant a rater offset of known g in the REAL table; the statistic must rise
    monotonically and must NOT fire at g=0.
E4  what clause ③ buys: the share of variance an annotator holdout can remove, stated as a number.

IDENTIFICATION
E1/E2 identified: the null is a permutation of annotator labels over assessment rows, which removes
the rater main effect while preserving each prompt's row count and the marginal distribution of
loads. ⚠ A permutation answers "did the labelling matter", never "why" -- so E3 builds the world the
rival predicts (a real rater effect) synthetically and checks the instrument fires on it, which is
the §1 requirement a bare permutation null does not meet.
⚠ The agreement of annotator a on prompt p is computed LEAVE-ONE-OUT against the other annotators of
that prompt, so a is never compared with itself. Prompts with fewer than 2 annotators cannot carry
the statistic; they are dropped and the count is printed.

DERIVED BEFORE MEASURING
D1 [DERIVATION] under the null the expected excess is 0, so a negative point estimate is noise and is
   reported as 0 with its interval, never as a negative variance.
D2 [DERIVATION] pairwise agreement is INVARIANT to the rater main effect (gauge test above), so
   CEIL_H cannot be used as evidence either way. Stating this before the run is what stops the
   round re-importing R813's mistake.
D3 an annotator's load varies (median 19 prompts, max 38); the statistic is weighted by load and the
   unweighted version is reported beside it, because that is a defensible-choice axis.
D4 the residual is whatever prompt and rater do not explain, and on a crossed design it absorbs the
   interaction -- so a small rater share does NOT mean annotators are interchangeable, only that
   they have no ADDITIVE main effect. The distinction is the round's main scope limit.

WORLDS
A  NO MAIN EFFECT: rater share < 0.02 -> clause ③'s annotator holdout is aimed at a near-empty
   source, and the variance that matters is prompt plus interaction.
B  REAL MAIN EFFECT: rater share > 0.10 -> the holdout does real work and clause ③ stands as
   written.
C  BETWEEN: 0.02 to 0.10 -> the holdout does something small; report the number and stop.
CHECKED FIRST: B, because it vindicates the clause already in the definition.

PREDICTION MATRIX
                         A                B                C
rater share           < 0.02           > 0.10           0.02-0.10
excess vs null CI     contains 0       excludes 0       excludes 0
clause ③ verdict      aimed wrong      stands           small effect

CONTROLS
OBJECT   reproduce R793's CEIL_H = 0.551880 from this round's own assessment table, exactly. It is
         the one committed number this table can anchor to. Exit 2 otherwise.
PLACEBO  the observed statistic computed against ITSELF (no permutation) minus itself: exactly 0.
POSITIVE E3: planted rater offsets g in {0, 0.02, 0.05, 0.10, 0.20}; the rater share must rise
         monotonically, and at g=0 it must NOT exceed the null band -- the g=0 cell is the check
         that this control can fail.
NEGATIVE the permutation null itself, 500 draws, reported as a distribution with its own spread.
NOISE FLOOR the statistic recomputed on 20 half-splits of the annotator panel.

PRE-REGISTERED KILL -- conditional, never a bare threshold
  if ceil_h_reproduces and placebo_zero and positive_monotone_and_null_at_g0:
      if rater share > 0.10 and its CI excludes 0     -> WORLD B
      elif rater share < 0.02 or the CI contains 0    -> WORLD A
      else                                             -> WORLD C
  else:
      UNVERIFIED   (never OVERTURNED, never CONFIRMED)

MULTIPLICITY 5 dose cells x {weighted, unweighted} reported in full; the headline is one statistic
and needs no correction, and saying so is not a licence to hide the grid.
STOPPING RULE one round. If more than 10% of prompts cannot carry the leave-one-out statistic, the
round returns UNVERIFIED rather than summarising over a shrinking population.

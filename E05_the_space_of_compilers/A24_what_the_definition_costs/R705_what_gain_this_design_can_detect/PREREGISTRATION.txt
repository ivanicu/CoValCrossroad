ESTIMAND
  The MINIMUM DETECTABLE GAIN of the leave-one-out cell-majority gain statistic on 42 arms: the
  smallest TRUE partition-attributable gain at which the design rejects its own permutation null at
  α = 0.05 with power ≥ 0.80 — reported in GAIN UNITS, the same units as the +0.0476 it must judge.

IDENTIFICATION
  Identified by simulation: the partition is fixed and known, so a graded plant traces dose→gain→power
  exactly. ⚠ The MDE is a property of THIS partition at THIS n at a GIVEN base rate, and nothing else.

SCOPE
  population : the 42 arms of R360's ledger, with SYNTHETIC labels (the real labels are what is being
               judged, so they cannot also be the measuring stick)
  instrument : dose-response over count-preserving label swaps; power against a permutation null
               instrument unit = A SYNTHETIC LABEL VECTOR
               claim unit      = AN OBSERVED CLAUSE GAIN
               ⚠ NOT EQUAL — an MDE bounds what the design can resolve, never whether a particular
               observed value is true.
  baseline   : the permutation null at the SAME positive count (nuisance-matched)
  regime     : α = 0.05, power target 0.80, 2000 null draws, 400 replicates per dose

WORLDS

  B UNRESOLVABLE MDE > 0.0476 → both +0.0476 values are inside the noise and the F1-vs-F2 ordering
                 must be withdrawn from STATEMENT.md
  C MIS-CALIBRATED power at dose 0 exceeds 2α → the test is anti-conservative, no MDE is admissible,

CONTROLS
  POSITIVE      at dose 1.0 (a pure cell function) power must be ≥ 0.95 — the design detects a
                maximal plant. CEILING is 1−base_rate, computed, so the threshold is checked to lie
                strictly between the dose-0 floor and that ceiling before it is used.
  g=0 / CALIB   at dose 0.0 (structure fully destroyed, count preserved) power must be ≤ 2α. This is
                the control that can condemn the whole instrument, and it is evaluated first.
  NEGATIVE      shuffle the CELL ASSIGNMENT at dose 1.0 and refit → power must fall to ≈ α.
  SHAM          single-cell partition at dose 1.0 → gain ≡ 0 by construction → power ≈ 0.
  PLACEBO       two identical runs at one seed differ by exactly 0.
  MONOTONICITY  power must be non-decreasing in dose across the sweep (Spearman ρ ≥ 0.9); a
                non-monotone dose-response means the statistic is mis-behaved and the MDE is void.
  NOISE FLOOR   the spread of observed gain at dose 0, measured over replicates, not assumed.

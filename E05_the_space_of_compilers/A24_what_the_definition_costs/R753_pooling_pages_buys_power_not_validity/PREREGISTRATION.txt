R753 · pooling the other deliverables buys power, and the question is whether it buys validity
WRITTEN BEFORE ANY CODE FOR THIS ROUND. 2026-08-05.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THIS ROUND EXISTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
R752 refused a comparison for want of power and left a requirement: 846 figures per arm against 33
available. It closed by asking how many of this repository's other deliverable pages would be needed
to reach it.

⛔ THAT QUESTION IS DIVISION, NOT A MEASUREMENT, and answering only it would be the arithmetic trap.
The inventory (see SIGHTED below) gives 427 figures across all three deliverables -- roughly HALF of
one arm's requirement -- so the counting answer is already fixed and forced.

THE QUESTION WORTH ASKING IS THE ONE UNDERNEATH IT. Pooling figures from three documents to reach a
sample size assumes those documents are EXCHANGEABLE units for this comparison. §1's G1 names this
exactly: asking for power on an unidentified quantity is how a well-powered-looking round gets built.
If the pages differ in citation practice, pooling manufactures power WITHOUT validity, which is worse
than being under-powered, because the resulting number looks answerable.

And R598 already measured that the three are NOT equally governed: emptying `FORMULATION.md` flips
0 of 28 gates, against 3 for `STATEMENT.md` and 4 for `DEFINITION.md`. R566 recorded that no round
had opened `FORMULATION.md` at all. So non-exchangeability is a live hypothesis with prior evidence,
not a rhetorical worry.

WORLDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A · EXCHANGEABLE AND SUFFICIENT. The rates agree and the pooled total reaches 846.
    ⚠ ALREADY DEAD ON THE COUNT -- 427 < 846 -- and declared dead below rather than predicted.
B · EXCHANGEABLE BUT INSUFFICIENT. The rates agree, the total falls short, and the register's
    cross-site line becomes a real wall carrying a NUMBER instead of a category.
C · NOT EXCHANGEABLE. The rates differ beyond the MDE, so pooling would manufacture power without
    validity and the shortfall is the lesser of the two problems.
D · UNRESOLVED. The rates differ by less than the MDE, so the design cannot tell B from C, and
    saying so is the result.

PREDICTION MATRIX -- max pairwise difference in flagged rate
    C -> >= MDE            B or D -> < MDE, distinguished only by whether the MDE itself is credible

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
⛔ THE ARITHMETIC, DERIVED BEFORE MEASURING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FORCED, labelled derivations, not evidence:
  - 427 < 846. Pooling every deliverable in this repository reaches about half of ONE arm. The
    comparison is unreachable here REGARDLESS of exchangeability, and that is division.
  - pages_needed = 846 / figures_per_page is likewise division and is reported as one number, not a
    finding.
  - a rate on 118 figures has a standard error near 0.035 at p=0.18; three such rates will differ by
    something even under perfect exchangeability. THE SPREAD IS NOT EVIDENCE OF DIFFERENCE unless it
    clears the MDE.

STRONGEST CONFOUND, written BEFORE the run: the three documents cite DIFFERENT ERAS of rounds -- R588
measured exactly that -- so a flagged-rate difference could be a property of WHEN a document was
written rather than of HOW it is governed. CONTROL IN THE SAME ITERATION: report the median cited
round-id per document beside its flagged rate, so an era difference is visible rather than absorbed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
KILL -- CONDITIONAL, gated on the controls
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
if the simulation is calibrated at g=0 (rejects ~alpha) and monotone in plant size:
        max pairwise difference >= MDE  -> WORLD C. Pooling is inadmissible; report per-document.
        max difference < MDE            -> WORLD D, UNRESOLVED. The design cannot separate B from C,
                                           and this must be stated as silence, NOT as agreement.
else:
        UNVERIFIED, and specifically the FORMULA is unverified -- distinct from the design being
        blind, per R752, and the two are not reported as one.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONTROLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
POSITIVE   the MONOTONE LADDER, as in R752: rejection must rise across plant sizes 0, m/2, m, 2m.
           Asserting that a searched MDE rejects at 0.80 would be circular; the ORDER is not.
           BAND, COMPUTED: floor = rejection at zero plant (~alpha), ceiling = rejection at a maximal
           plant (~1.00).
g=0        zero planted difference must reject at ~alpha.
NEGATIVE   shuffle the document labels across figures, holding the figures fixed. The between-document
           rate spread must collapse toward what chance gives. World excluded: "any partition of
           these figures shows this spread".
SHAM       the same operation with the INGREDIENT ABSENT, not inverted. Ingredient = being a
           DIFFERENT document. Split STATEMENT.md into two halves by line number and compute the
           flagged rate of each. Two halves of ONE document are exchangeable by construction, so
           their difference is the floor any between-document difference must clear to mean anything.
PLACEBO    each document's rate computed twice -> exactly 0 difference, reported as 0 of N.
NOISE      3 simulation seeds; rejection rates per seed with their spread, never averaged silently.
UNIT       instrument unit = a FIGURE on a citing line; claim unit = a DOCUMENT's practice. Not
           equal -- figures cluster within lines and lines within sections, so the effective n is
           below the figure count. The number of DISTINCT LINES carrying figures is reported beside
           each rate, and the MDE is ALSO computed on the line count as the conservative reading.

+ 3 simulation seeds + the SHAM's within-document split. All reported.

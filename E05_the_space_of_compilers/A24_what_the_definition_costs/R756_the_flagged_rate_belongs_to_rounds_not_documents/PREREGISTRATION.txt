--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
⛔ THE ARITHMETIC, DERIVED BEFORE MEASURING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FORCED, labelled, not evidence:
  - a document's rate is a weighted average of its rounds' rates. Documents differing FOLLOWS from
    rounds differing plus disjoint sets. Only the SIZE of round-level variance is measured.
  - a round cited by ONE figure has a rate of exactly 0 or exactly 1 by construction. Such rounds
    inflate between-round variance mechanically, so the variance MUST be reported both over all
    rounds and over rounds with >= 3 figures, and the two never merged.
  - the sampling null must preserve each round's FIGURE COUNT, or it compares different designs.

STRONGEST CONFOUND, written BEFORE the run: a round's flagged rate depends on how many numbers its
artifact stores, which is a property of the round's own design rather than of anyone's citing. CONTROL
IN THE SAME ITERATION: report each round's artifact SIZE beside its flagged rate and the correlation
between them, so an artifact-size explanation is visible rather than absorbed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONTROLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
POSITIVE   a round whose artifact is large and number-dense must show a LOW flagged rate among the
           figures citing it. Chosen by artifact size BEFORE its rate is computed, so the control is
           not selected on the outcome. BAND, COMPUTED: floor = the rate of a round with an empty
           artifact (1.0 by construction), ceiling = 0.0; the measured rate must sit strictly below
           the pooled mean.
g=0        a cited round with NO results/*.json must return UNDEFINED and be excluded with its count
           printed -- never scored as 0.0, which would look like perfect support, nor as 1.0, which
           would look like total failure. Both directions are wrong and the exclusion is named.
NEGATIVE   reassign figures to rounds at random, PRESERVING each round's figure count, 5 seeds. The
           between-round variance must collapse. World excluded: "any partition of these figures into
           groups of these sizes shows this variance".
SHAM       the same operation with the INGREDIENT ABSENT, not inverted. Ingredient = grouping by the
           CITED ROUND. Group the same figures by their LINE NUMBER into blocks of the same sizes and
           compute the same variance. A real round effect must exceed what an arbitrary blocking gives.
PLACEBO    the variance computed twice -> exactly 0 difference, reported as 0 of N.
NOISE      5 null seeds and 5 sham blockings; spreads printed, never averaged into one number.
UNIT       instrument unit = a (figure, round) PAIR; claim unit = a ROUND. Not equal -- a figure citing
           three rounds contributes to three -- so the per-figure and per-pair totals are both printed
           and the round-level rate is computed over pairs, which is stated rather than assumed.

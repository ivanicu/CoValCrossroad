written BEFORE run.py. I have read R294's docstring, its admission lines (144-150) and
corebench/report.py:verdict. I have NOT looked at any arm's margins except coval_core's, which

ESTIMAND
   Over the defensible decision-rule space of R294's OWN procedure, how many DISTINCT extensions
   can it return, and what share of that space returns the released set
   {coval_core, topw_k3, topw_k4, topw_k6, topw_k8}?
   R723 left the deliverable resting on ONE computation, R294. A producer that could only ever have
   returned one answer is a DERIVATION, and derivations do not support the output they compute.

IDENTIFICATION
   Identified for axes the persisted artifact fixes: each arm's effect, CI and MDE per clause, its
   provenance flag, and its k-cap flag. Every decision rule is a function of those.
   NOT identified from the artifact: bootstrap seed, NBOOT, the BH q, and the >=2-annotator
   population filter. Those need a re-run of the census and are named in IMPOSSIBLE, not waived.
   So the answer is a LOWER BOUND on the number of reachable extensions, never a count of them.

SCOPE
   population : the 41 judged arms in R294's full_census.json
   instrument : re-derivation of the admission rule from the persisted per-arm statistics
   baseline   : R294's own cell -- lo > 0 AND |eff| >= mde, clause ③ on, k-capped arms kept
   regime     : this artifact at source_sha 2bc1124f6825df0f; no re-running of the bootstrap

WORLDS
   W-FORCED   the extension is invariant across the rule space. Then R294 measured nothing that
              could have come out otherwise, and the deliverable's single support is a derivation.
   W-FITTED   the extension varies and the released set is a rare cell. Then the number 5 is a
              choice of rule presented as a measurement.
   W-ROBUST   the extension varies at the edges and the released set is modal. Then the producer
              survives, and the 5-set is the answer most defensible rules give.
   prediction matrix
      distinct == 1                        -> FORCED 0.95  FITTED 0.02  ROBUST 0.03
      distinct > 1, released modal         -> FORCED 0.02  FITTED 0.10  ROBUST 0.90
      distinct > 1, released share < 0.10  -> FORCED 0.02  FITTED 0.90  ROBUST 0.08

GRID (specification curve, 100 cells, all reported)
   clause ① rule x 5 : strict(lo>0 & |e|>=mde) · ci_only(lo>0) · point(e>0) · mde_only(e>=mde) ·
                       conservative(lo>mde)
   clause ② rule x 5 : the same five
   clause ③        x 2 : on (as declared) · off
   k-capped arms   x 2 : kept (R294's choice) · dropped

REGISTERED POINTS (failable)
   A  distinct extensions over the 100 cells                     = 12   [2, 100]
   B  share of cells returning exactly the released 5-set        = 0.25 [0.00, 1.00]
   C  extension size at R294's own ①② rule with ③ OFF           = 7    [5, 41]
      ⚠ C IS A DERIVATION, NOT A MEASUREMENT. Turning ③ off can only ADD the arms it excluded,
      so C = 5 + |{a in USES_PROMPT_LABELS : a clears ① and ②}| by algebra. It is registered to
      make the arithmetic explicit and it will be LABELLED a derivation in the report, never
      quoted as evidence.
   DIRECTIONAL (separately failable): the released 5-set is the MODAL extension of the grid.

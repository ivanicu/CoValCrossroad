R800 · criterion, prompt, or INTERACTION — the first fully-crossed decomposition in this release
WRITTEN BEFORE ANY CODE FOR THIS ROUND. 2026-08-06.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THIS ROUND EXISTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
R799 measured the pool's instance-level signal sd at **0.1532** and its across-criteria sd at
**0.0157**, and concluded "roughly a tenth is criterion, the rest is prompt". ⚠ That comparison is a
BOUND, not a decomposition: "the rest" lumps PROMPT together with CRITERION x PROMPT INTERACTION, and
those are different objects with different consequences for a definition.

CHECK #402 found two things.

(a) ⛔ R799's OWN NEXT PROPOSES THE DESIGN R799 KILLED, ONE LEVEL UP. It asked to measure "per prompt
    whether the rubric's deficit is concentrated in a minority of prompts", i.e. to rank 968 noisy
    per-prompt estimates and read a share off the ranking — exactly the selection-on-outcome error
    R799 diagnosed for 14,979 instances, at a coarser grain. **I proposed it one round after
    diagnosing it.** The identified version is the same remedy: deconvolve.
(b) ⭐ AND THE POOL'S GRID IS FULLY CROSSED. All 968 prompts carry the identical criterion index set
    `(0..15)`, so **a two-way variance decomposition is identified** — criterion, prompt, their
    interaction, and annotator noise, separately. This is the first fully-crossed design available in
    the release, and it turns R799's bound into an estimate.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DISCLOSURE -- what was already known when this was written
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
KNOWN and quoted: R799's pool numbers (instance sd 0.1649, signal sd 0.1532, noise sd 0.0609,
across-criteria sd 0.0157, the 16 per-criterion accuracies) and the crossed-grid fact from check

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE ARITHMETIC, DERIVED BEFORE MEASURING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D1  ON A CROSSED GRID, `var(A) = s2_c + s2_p + s2_int + s2_e`, and the marginal means identify the
    parts: `var over c of the criterion means = s2_c + (s2_int + s2_e)/P` and `var over p of the
    prompt means = s2_p + (s2_int + s2_e)/C`. **DERIVATION**, and it is why a crossed grid is worth
    more than two marginal spreads.
D2  R799's 0.0157 IS AN UPPER BOUND ON `s2_c`, not an estimate of it, because the criterion means
    still carry `(s2_int + s2_e)/968`. With P = 968 that inflation is small, so the correction is
    expected to be minor — **stated in advance so a small correction is not reported as a discovery.**
D3  THE PROMPT-LEVEL DEFICIT IS PAIRED. Both pools are scored by the SAME annotators on the SAME
    responses, so annotator noise partially cancels in `full − pool` and its noise variance is NOT
    the sum of the two. **DERIVATION**, and it means the deficit's noise must be measured on the
    DIFFERENCE, never assembled from each pool's own.
D4  A SHARE READ OFF A RANKING OF NOISY ESTIMATES IS BIASED AWAY FROM THE CENTRE, always. So the
    naive "share of prompts where the rubric loses" exceeds the deconvolved one whenever the true
    spread is smaller than the observed. **DERIVATION**, and it is the shape of the error in (a).

STRONGEST CONFOUND, written BEFORE the run: `full` carries a VARYING number of criteria per prompt
(min 4, mean 15.48, max 39) while the pool carries exactly 16, so a per-prompt deficit mixes "the
rubric is worse here" with "the rubric had fewer criteria here". CONTROL IN THE SAME ITERATION: the
deficit is recomputed on a size-matched basis -- for each prompt, a random 16-subset of `full` where
it has at least 16, capped at what exists otherwise -- and both versions are reported.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ESTIMANDS (before method)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E1  ⭐ THE TWO-WAY DECOMPOSITION on the pool's 16 x 968 crossed grid: `s2_criterion`, `s2_prompt`,
    `s2_interaction`, `s2_annotator-noise`, each as a share of the total.
E2  ⭐ THE PROMPT-LEVEL DEFICIT (`full` minus pool, per prompt), with its noise measured on the
    DIFFERENCE (D3) and the deconvolved true spread.
E3  ⭐ the share of prompts where the rubric loses: NAIVE against DECONVOLVED, which is the
    identified form of the question R799's NEXT asked.
E4  the size confound: the deficit recomputed size-matched, beside the raw one.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WORLDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A · INTERACTION DOMINATES. `s2_int` is the largest non-noise component. Then a criterion is not
    globally good or bad -- its value is prompt-specific -- and a definition that ranks criteria at
    all is ranking something that does not exist independent of the prompt.
B · PROMPT DOMINATES. `s2_p` is largest. Then prompts differ in how well ANY criterion set predicts
    their humans, criteria are close to interchangeable, and the rubric's deficit is about coverage
    rather than content.
C · CRITERION DOMINATES. `s2_c` is largest. R799's 0.0157 makes this unlikely, so if it fires the
    instrument is suspect and the round says so rather than celebrating.

PREDICTION MATRIX
  A -> s2_int > s2_p and s2_int > s2_c
  B -> s2_p > s2_int and s2_p > s2_c
  C -> s2_c largest -- treat as an instrument failure and report UNVERIFIED for the ranking claim
  a near-tie (top two within 20% of each other) -> report; CLAIM NO WORLD.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
KILL -- CONDITIONAL, gated on the controls
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
if OBJECT passes (the pool's instance sd reproduces R799's 0.1649 and the across-criteria sd its
   0.0157, both to 1e-9 -- exit 2 otherwise)
   and PLACEBO returns exactly 0 for a cell against itself
   and POSITIVE fires (a synthetic crossed grid with KNOWN components returns each within 20%, at
       two different component mixes -- band computed at both ends)
   and NEGATIVE fires (shuffling criterion labels WITHIN each prompt drives `s2_c` to ~0 while
       leaving the total unchanged -- a derivation, checked):
        s2_c largest                                  -> WORLD C, and report UNVERIFIED
        s2_int largest by more than 20%               -> WORLD A
        s2_p largest by more than 20%                 -> WORLD B
        otherwise                                     -> report; claim no world.
else:
        UNVERIFIED. Never OVERTURNED, never CONFIRMED.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONTROLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OBJECT      R799's two committed pool sds reproduced to 1e-9. Exit 2, never 0.
PLACEBO     a cell against itself: variance contribution exactly 0.
POSITIVE    BAND COMPUTED AT BOTH ENDS: two synthetic crossed grids, one interaction-heavy and one
            prompt-heavy, each with known components; require recovery within 20% and require the
            two to be distinguished in the right direction.
NEGATIVE    shuffle criterion labels WITHIN each prompt. `s2_c` must collapse toward 0 and the total
            must be unchanged (a derivation, checked). World it excludes: "the criterion component is
            an artefact of the decomposition rather than of the labelling."
CONFOUND    E4's size-matched deficit.
NOISE FLOOR MEASURED: annotator split-half per cell, and separately on the DIFFERENCE (D3).
UNIT        instrument unit = a (criterion, prompt, annotator) judgement; claim unit = a VARIANCE
            COMPONENT; E3's unit = a PROMPT. Three strings, required unequal.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MULTIPLICITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4 variance components + the deficit + the size-matched deficit + the two share estimates = 8
reported quantities. The components are a DECOMPOSITION and sum to the total by construction, so
they are reported whole rather than tested individually; BH at q = 0.05 applies to the deficit tests
only, and the distinction is stated rather than blurred.

ESTIMAND

  release's per-conversation record and (b) SEPARATES — admits some instances and rejects others.

IDENTIFICATION

  distribution over the 986; that is a property of THIS release's instances, not of cores.
  ⚠ Whether a separating predicate is the RIGHT one is construct validity and is impossible here.

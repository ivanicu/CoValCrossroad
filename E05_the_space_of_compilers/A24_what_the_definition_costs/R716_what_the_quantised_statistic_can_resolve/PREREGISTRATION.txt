ESTIMAND
  What the provenance share can and cannot resolve, in three parts:
    (i)   QUANTUM — the smallest non-zero value a single instance can take, against the population
          mean. If the quantum exceeds the mean, no single instance can express a value near it.
    (ii)  MEAN PRECISION — the bootstrap standard error of the population mean over the 986.
    (iii) MDE — the smallest between-group difference in mean share this design detects at 80% power
          on a two-group split, measured by planting graded shifts, not asserted.

IDENTIFICATION
  (i) is a DERIVATION from k's support and is labelled. (ii) and (iii) are measured by resampling.
  ⚠ The MDE is a property of THIS split size and THIS distribution, not of the statistic in general.

SCOPE
  population : the 986 conversation rubrics
  instrument : bootstrap over instances + a planted-shift power curve
               instrument unit = A CORE INSTANCE
               claim unit      = WHAT A COMPARISON ACROSS THE STATISTIC CAN SUPPORT
               ⚠ NOT EQUAL — a precise MEAN does not make a per-instance value informative, and that
               gap is the whole round.
  baseline   : the statistic's own quantum, 0.25 at k=4
  regime     : this repository at HEAD, alpha 0.05, power 0.80

WORLDS
  A FINE ENOUGH    the mean's SE is small AND the MDE is below the quantum ⇒ group comparisons are
                   readable and only per-instance ones are coarse
  B COARSE         the MDE is at or above the quantum ⇒ the design cannot resolve a difference
                   smaller than one instance's smallest step, and no comparison should be reported
  C DEGENERATE     the statistic's support collapses under any grouping ⇒ nothing is comparable

PREDICTION MATRIX          A         B         C
  SE of the mean          small     small     n/a
  MDE vs quantum         below     at/above   n/a
  group comparisons     readable   refuse     refuse

CONTROLS
  POSITIVE   plant a mean shift of 0.10 into one group; the test must detect it at >= 80%. FLOOR (the
             no-shift rejection rate) and CEILING (rejection at a maximal shift) computed, and the
             power target required to lie strictly between them.
  g=0        a planted shift of exactly 0 must be rejected at approximately alpha, not more.
  NEGATIVE   shuffle the group labels at fixed sizes and recompute — the observed difference must sit
             inside that null. The world it excludes: "any split of these 986 shows a difference".
  SHAM       the same resolution question asked of `coval_full` overlapped with ITSELF, which is
             identically 1.0 — a degenerate statistic, where SE is 0 and no MDE exists. It shows what
             the machinery returns when there is nothing to resolve.
  PLACEBO    two identical runs differ by exactly 0.
  NOISE FLOOR the bootstrap spread itself, measured over >= 4000 resamples.
  UNIT       instrument unit != claim unit, asserted and carried into the verdict.

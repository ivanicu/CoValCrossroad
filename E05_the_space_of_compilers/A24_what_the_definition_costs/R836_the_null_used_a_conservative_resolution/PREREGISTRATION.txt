WHAT R835 DID, AND THE DIRECTION IT ERRS IN
  R835 reported W-NO-SEPARABLE-BEST -- 0 of 45 adjacent gaps clearing 2x MDE -- and flagged that
  its MDE is "arm-vs-BAR, not arm-vs-arm" as an APPROXIMATION. ⛔ IT NEVER SAID IN WHICH DIRECTION
  THE APPROXIMATION ERRS, and it errs toward the NULL.

  Both arms are scored as differences against the SAME bar, so
      d_AC = d_AB - d_CB      sd(d_AC)^2 = sd_A^2 + sd_C^2 - 2*rho*sd_A*sd_C
  and with sd_A ~ sd_C this is   MDE_AC = MDE_A * sqrt(2*(1-rho)).
  Using MDE_A directly, as R835 did, IS THE ASSUMPTION rho = 0.5. For any rho > 0.5 the true
  resolution is FINER and R835's "unresolved" is conservative. Two arms scored against a common bar
  share that bar's noise, so rho > 0.5 is the expected case, not the exotic one -- R825 measured
  corr(bar, core) = +0.8377 for a comparable pairing on this site.

ESTIMAND
  Per adjacent pair in R835's ordering: the value of rho at which the gap becomes separable,
      rho* = 1 - (gap / MDE_A)^2 / 2
  Named before the method. This is a SENSITIVITY CURVE, not a re-verdict.

⚠ WHAT THIS ROUND MAY NOT DO
  It may NOT rewrite R835's pre-registered verdict. R835 registered its threshold and its kill fired
  as written; a verdict is not reopened because a later round prefers a different resolution model.
  R835 is ANNOTATED as scope-dependent and the scope is named. And rho is NOT measured here -- the
  per-prompt difference vectors were never persisted -- so no pair is re-labelled SEPARABLE.

POSITIVE CONTROL -- ON THE DERIVATION ITSELF, because the arithmetic trap demands it
  Simulate d_AB and d_CB at known rho over the same n, compute sd(d_AC) empirically, and require the
  closed form to match within Monte-Carlo error at rho in {0.0, 0.5, 0.84, 0.95}. If the formula
  does not reproduce the simulation the derivation is wrong and nothing else in the round counts.
POSITIVE CONTROL -- ON THE APPLICATION
  a pair with a large gap (`oracle_k4` vs the best ③-admissible arm) must give rho* < 0, i.e.
  separable for every admissible correlation.
NEGATIVE CONTROL
  an arm against ITSELF has gap 0, so rho* = 1 exactly -- never separable at any rho < 1. Computed
  by looking the arm up TWICE from the artifact, not as `x - x`.

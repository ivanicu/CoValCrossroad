⛔ THE ARITHMETIC, DERIVED BEFORE MEASURING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D1  `generic` CLEARS ② BELOW PERCENTILE 93.7 BY CONSTRUCTION. It IS `POOL[0:4]`, whose A2 sits at the
    93.7th percentile of the 1,820-subset class (R527, committed). A subset at percentile q beats
    every subset below q on the point estimate, so "generic clears ② at p000..p050" is FORCED.
    ⇒ THE ONLY NON-FORCED PART is whether it clears RESOLVEDLY (the CI and MDE could refuse it), and
      that is what the round reports -- never the bare admission.
D2  EXCLUDING A COMPARATOR-IDENTICAL ARM CAN ONLY SHRINK AN EXTENSION. Monotone by subset. So "fewer
    cells survive" is algebra; the measurement is WHICH survive and by WHICH arms.
D3  IF `generic` == `POOL[0:4]` EXACTLY, THEIR A2 SHOULD BE EQUAL. R761 reports 0.5514 and 0.5504.
    A non-zero difference on identical criteria is therefore NOT a property of the criteria -- it is
    the scoring pipeline's own variance, and it is the E3 estimand rather than an anomaly to explain.

STRONGEST CONFOUND, written BEFORE the run: two artifacts could carry identical criterion STRINGS but
score DIFFERENT RESPONSES (a different response set per prompt), in which case their A2 difference is
not a scoring replicate at all. CONTROL IN THE SAME ITERATION: require the two artifacts to cover the
SAME prompt set and the SAME response letters, and report the count that fails this before any floor
is quoted.

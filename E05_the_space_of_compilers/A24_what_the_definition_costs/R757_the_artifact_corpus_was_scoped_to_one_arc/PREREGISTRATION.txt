⛔ THE ARITHMETIC, DERIVED BEFORE MEASURING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FORCED, labelled, not evidence:
  - the repo-wide corpus CONTAINS the A24 corpus, so a figure supported under A24 stays supported.
    The rate can only FALL or stay equal. "The rate fell" is therefore not a finding; only the SIZE
    of the fall, and its comparison against the SHAM, are measurements.
  - 98 of 578 is 17.0% more text. Under a matcher that scans all of it, spurious-match probability
    rises with corpus size for every value. THE SHAM IS NOT OPTIONAL HERE -- without it a fall of
    any size is uninterpretable.
  - R753's between-document differences are weighted averages of round-level rates (R756), so they
    move mechanically when the round-level rates move. Only the ORDERING is worth reporting.

CONTROLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
POSITIVE   find, BY DIRECT SEARCH rather than by the matcher, a figure on a deliverable whose value
           appears verbatim in an artifact OUTSIDE A24 and whose cited round is that artifact's. It
           must be flagged under the A24 corpus and supported under the repo-wide one. BAND,

g=0        a fabricated value present in no artifact must stay flagged under BOTH corpora. A
           correction that "supports" an invented number is matching noise.
NEGATIVE   restrict back to A24 and require the three rates to reproduce R753's 0.1793 / 0.3814 /
           0.8000 EXACTLY. World excluded: "the numbers moved because I changed the code".
SHAM       the same operation with the INGREDIENT ABSENT, not inverted. Ingredient = the missing

           own prose and assurance sources -- and recompute. If the rate falls similarly, the
           movement is corpus SIZE and not evidence.
PLACEBO    each rate computed twice under the same corpus -> exactly 0 difference, 0 of N.
NOISE      no rng except the SHAM's file selection, seeded, over 3 seeds with the spread printed.
UNIT       instrument unit = a (figure, artifact-corpus) pair; claim unit = a FIGURE's support
           status. Equal here by construction, and that equality is why the comparison is clean --
           stated rather than assumed.

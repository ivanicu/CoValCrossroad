ESTIMAND
  Per claim block citing a retracted literal, whether the literal is used as a PREMISE (the block's
  own conclusion depends on the literal's members having the retracted property) or as a CONTRAST
  (the block cites it to distinguish its own object), and separately whether the block repeats the
  retracted DESCRIPTION as a gloss.
  Then: the corrected residue — asserting blocks whose CLAIM no later round has undermined.

IDENTIFICATION
  ⚠ PREMISE-versus-CONTRAST is a READING of each block's own sentences, not a measurement. It is
  positive-controlled against two blocks whose role is independently stateable: R688 uses
  PUBLISHED_FIVE as a PREMISE (its finding is about "the 3 published arms"), and the lineage block
  uses it as a CONTRAST (it enumerates other sets that "denote other objects").
  The GLOSS check is exact: does the block state the retracted description in its own words.

             that cannot separate two blocks whose roles are independently stateable separates none.
  g=0        a block citing NO retracted literal must yield role=None, not a default classification.
  NEGATIVE   apply the reading to the retracted literal's own block — the one that RECORDS the
             retraction — which must classify as neither premise nor contrast but as the RETRACTION
             ITSELF, so the instrument does not count the retraction as a victim of itself.

             block must then flip to PREMISE, showing the markers are the ingredient.
  PLACEBO    two identical runs differ by exactly 0.
  UNIT       instrument unit != claim unit, asserted and carried into the verdict.

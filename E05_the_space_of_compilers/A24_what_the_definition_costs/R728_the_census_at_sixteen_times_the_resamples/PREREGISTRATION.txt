DERIVATION, STATED FIRST
   R294 admits a clause when verdict(eff, lo, hi, mde) == POS, i.e. lo > 0 AND |eff| >= mde.
   mde = ZEFF * sd / sqrt(n) does NOT depend on B. Only lo does. So an admission can move with B
   only where the CI half is the binding one AND lo sits within Monte-Carlo error of zero.
   The MC sd of the lower percentile is sqrt(.025*.975/B)/phi(1.96) SE units = 0.07711 SE at
   B = 1200. Applying |t - 1.959964| < 3 * 0.07711 = 0.2313 to the persisted artifact gives
   EXACTLY ONE at-risk cell: random_k8_s0 clause 1, t = 2.1458.
   ⭐ AND THAT CELL CANNOT CHANGE THE ADMISSION EITHER, because t = 2.1458 < ZEFF = 2.801585, so the
   B-INVARIANT half already fails and the arm is excluded whatever lo does.
   THEREFORE: zero admission changes at any B is FORCED BY THE ALGEBRA, not predicted by a model.
   Registering it anyway, because the re-run is simultaneously a test of MY RE-IMPLEMENTATION, and
   that is not forced by anything.

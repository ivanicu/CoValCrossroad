--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D1  AN IMPOSSIBILITY LINE IS A UNIVERSAL CLAIM AND IS REFUTED BY ONE INSTANCE. So the audit is
    asymmetric: showing a line FALSE needs one counterexample, showing it TRUE needs an argument the
    data cannot supply. **DERIVATION**, and it means the verdicts must be FALSE / UNVERIFIED, never
    "confirmed".
D2  A GREP OVER `run.py` FILES UNDERCOUNTS READERS AND CANNOT OVERCOUNT THEM, because a round may
    read the file through a helper. So 22 is a LOWER BOUND on the rounds that touched the second

    corpus with human judgements, or (ii) a second corpus with RUBRICS AND per-annotator rankings.
    Reading (i) is refuted by the file's existence; reading (ii) is not. **DERIVATION**, and the round
    must report the line's fate under BOTH rather than choosing the one that flatters.
D4  THIS ROUND AUDITS ITS OWN AUTHOR'S OUTPUT, so it must exclude itself from the population, exactly
    as R793 had to. **DERIVATION**, and the exclusion is printed.

WORLDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A · THE REGISTER IS MOSTLY SOUND. At most one distinct line is FALSE. Then the cross-release lapse is
    an isolated error and the register is doing its job.
B · THE REGISTER IS A HABIT. Two or more distinct lines are FALSE, i.e. impossibility is being
    asserted as a genre convention rather than established per round. Then the register's cost
    exceeds its value as currently written and the practice must change.
C · THE INSTRUMENT CANNOT ANSWER. The loose and tight patterns disagree by more than a factor of two
    and the known-status control misclassifies. Then the audit is UNVERIFIED and no count is quoted.

PREDICTION MATRIX
  A -> exactly one distinct FALSE line
  B -> two or more distinct FALSE lines
  C -> the control misclassifies, or loose/tight differ by >2x
  zero FALSE lines -> report; CLAIM NO WORLD, because check #404 already exhibited one and a zero
  would mean the extractor is broken rather than that the register is clean.

⛔ THE ARITHMETIC, DERIVED BEFORE MEASURING -- AND IT SAYS THIS ROUND CANNOT RESCUE R768
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D1  MDE ∝ 1/sqrt(n). Going 968 -> 1,078 multiplies the MDE by sqrt(968/1078) = **0.947** -- a 5.3%
    reduction. R768's `coval_core` vs `topw_k4` gap is 0.0023 against an MDE of 0.0085, a factor of
    **3.7**; closing that needs **n x 13.7 ≈ 13,200 prompts**. **THE 110 PROMPTS CANNOT CHANGE ANY
    VERDICT, AND THAT IS ALGEBRA.** This round therefore does NOT rescue R768's null and says so
    before running, so that a null result cannot be read as a discovery.
D2  WHAT THE COUNT IS FOR IS SCOPE, NOT POWER. If the 110 are dropped by an ARM-COVERAGE accident
    rather than by a property of the release, then every number in this campaign carries an unstated
    **89.8%** population scope. That is the finding this round can actually produce.
D3  n REQUIRED SCALES AS (MDE/gap)^2 x n. Reporting a required n is therefore a DERIVATION from the
    measured sd, not a measurement -- it is labelled as one, and its assumption (D1's stable sd) is
    the thing E3 tests where it can.

STRONGEST CONFOUND, written BEFORE the run: the 110 dropped prompts might be systematically EASIER or
HARDER -- if they are dropped for having few annotators, their A2 is noisier, and adding them could
RAISE the MDE rather than lower it. CONTROL IN THE SAME ITERATION: compute the per-prompt difference
sd separately on the recoverable subset and on the 968, and report both rather than assuming they
match.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WORLDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A · THE DROP IS AN ARTIFACT OF ARM COVERAGE. Most of the 110 are absent from one scoring artifact,
    not from the release, so the population is a property of which arms were scored -- and every ②
    number in this campaign inherits a 968/1,078 scope nobody has stated.
B · THE DROP IS A PROPERTY OF THE RELEASE. The 110 genuinely lack the annotators or the pool
    coverage the estimand needs, so 968 is the right population and the scope is already correct.
C · THE DROPPED PROMPTS ARE DIFFERENT IN KIND. Their difference-vector sd differs materially from
    the 968's, so adding them would change the MDE in a direction the arithmetic alone cannot give.

PREDICTION MATRIX
  A -> >= 60 of 110 have >= 2 annotators AND full coverage in the compared arms
  B -> >= 60 of 110 fail on annotators or on pool coverage
  C -> the recoverable subset's difference sd differs from the 968's by more than 10%

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONTROLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
POSITIVE  the MDE-vs-n curve must follow `MDE(n) = MDE(968) * sqrt(968/n)` to within 5% across
          n in {100, 200, 400, 600, 800, 968}, averaged over 50 subsamples each. BAND, COMPUTED: a
          flat curve (no n dependence) fails; a curve with the wrong exponent fails. The 1/sqrt law
          is not fitted, it is asserted and checked.
g=0       at n = 968 with no subsampling the curve must reproduce R768's committed paired MDEs
          EXACTLY for the same pairs.
NEGATIVE  destroy the pairing at each n and report the MDE ratio -- it must stay near R768's ×2.25 at
          every n, showing the pairing's benefit is not an artifact of the full sample.
SHAM      ingredient ABSENT, not inverted. Ingredient = NEW PROMPTS. Draw n prompts WITH replacement
          from the same 968 -- the same count, no new information -- and report the MDE. If bootstrap
          resampling lowers the MDE as much as real prompts would, the curve is measuring arithmetic
          rather than information.
PLACEBO   the difference of an arm with ITSELF must have MDE exactly 0 at every n.
NOISE     50 subsamples per n, seeded; the spread is printed, never just the mean.
UNIT      instrument unit = a PROMPT; claim unit = an ARM PAIR. The MDE is per-pair and the count is
          per-prompt, and the required-n figure is stated per pair, never pooled.

R784 · every number in this arc is computed on 968 of 1078 ranked prompts, and the 110 are not random
WRITTEN BEFORE ANY CODE FOR THIS ROUND. 2026-08-06.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THIS ROUND EXISTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
R783's NEXT proposed comparing the 18 released-but-unscored conversations against the 968 on rubric
size, conversation length and response count. CHECK #386 killed that design on ARITHMETIC before
writing it, and then found something an order of magnitude larger.

⛔ THE PROPOSED DESIGN IS UNDERPOWERED BY CONSTRUCTION. With n1 = 18 against n2 = 968, the standard
error is dominated by sqrt(1/18) = 0.236 SD, so at z_eff = 2.80 the MDE is about **0.66 SD**. Only a
very large difference is detectable, and R783's own NEXT already called the 18's size profile
"indistinguishable" — which at n = 18 it would be almost regardless. **That is a null manufactured by
a design, the failure this programme keeps catching, and I proposed it one round after catching it.**

⭐⭐ AND THE DROP THAT MATTERS IS NOT THE 18. Read from the object:
    `load_targets()`           1078 prompts carry parsed human rankings
    `core_full.json`            968 prompts carry a rubric
    `sat_random_k4_s0.npz`      968
    the `>= 2 rankings` filter dropped **0**
So the 968 is not produced by any filter in this repository. **110 ranked prompts — 10.20% — have no
rubric entry, and every number in this arc is computed on the 89.80% that do.** Nothing in this arc
has ever stated that. And it is a DIFFERENT drop from R783's 18, which is release-side (986 released
conversations, 968 scored).

⭐⭐⭐ AND THE 110 ARE NOT OBVIOUSLY RANDOM. Their `unacceptable` flag count averages **10.109**
against **4.163** for the scored 968 — 2.4x. If the excluded prompts are disproportionately the ones
humans flagged as unacceptable, the definition was developed on a corpus with its most contested
prompts absent, and that is a scope limit on every claim the arc reports.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE ARITHMETIC, DERIVED BEFORE MEASURING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D1  AN 18-vs-968 COMPARISON HAS AN MDE OF ~0.66 SD. sqrt(1/18 + 1/968) = 0.2366, times z_eff 2.80.
    **DERIVATION.** It is stated so that R783's NEXT is priced rather than run, and so this round's
    own n = 110 design can be compared to it: sqrt(1/110 + 1/968) = 0.0996, MDE ~0.28 SD — still
    coarse, and stated as such.
D2  THE MEAN IS INADMISSIBLE FOR THE 110. Their rankings-per-prompt SD is **95.05** against the
    scored group's **4.78**, driven by a prompt carrying **1012** rankings. A difference of means
    with a 20x variance ratio is dominated by the tail, so **the estimand is stated on MEDIANS and a
    rank statistic**, and the mean is reported only to show why it is not used. Choosing the
    estimator after seeing the SD would be a specification choice made on the outcome, so it is
    fixed HERE, before the statistic runs.
D3  A GROUP-LABEL PERMUTATION IS A VALID NULL HERE, unlike the paired-mean permutations of ledger
    1125 and 1129. It destroys the grouping while preserving both marginals, and the world it
    excludes is "any 110-subset of the ranked corpus looks like this". **That world is built
    synthetically as the g=0 control** rather than assumed.

STRONGEST CONFOUND, written BEFORE the run: `unacceptable` flags are counted per prompt, so a prompt
with more rankings mechanically has more chances to be flagged — the two axes are not independent and
a difference in flags could be entirely a difference in ranking count. CONTROL IN THE SAME ITERATION:
report the flag count RATE per ranking as well as the raw count, and give the rank test on both.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WORLDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A · THE EXCLUSION IS STRUCTURED. The 110 differ from the 968 resolvedly on at least one axis under
    the rank statistic. Then every claim in this arc carries an unstated population restriction, and
    the deliverable is that restriction written into the scope line of the definition.
B · THE EXCLUSION IS INCIDENTAL. No axis resolves under the rank statistic AND the MDE is small
    enough that a difference worth caring about would have shown. Then the 968 is a fair sample of the
    ranked corpus on everything measurable here, and the exclusion is a coverage note rather than a
    scope limit.
C · UNDERPOWERED. No axis resolves and the MDE is too coarse to exclude a difference that matters.
    Then the answer is UNVERIFIED and the round's product is the required n.

PREDICTION MATRIX
  A -> some axis: rank-test p below the multiplicity-corrected threshold AND medians differ
  B -> no axis resolves AND MDE_rank < 0.28 SD
  C -> no axis resolves AND MDE_rank >= 0.28 SD

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
KILL -- CONDITIONAL, gated on the controls
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
if OBJECT passes (all three populations non-empty; the set differences reconcile exactly -- exit 2
   otherwise) and PLACEBO fires (the 968 against itself returns exactly no difference) and g=0 fires
   (a random 110-subset of the 968 against its complement does NOT resolve, over >= 200 draws) and
   POSITIVE fires (a synthetic subgroup with a planted shift IS detected, and is NOT detected at
   shift zero):
        some axis resolves after multiplicity  -> WORLD A
        none resolves and MDE < 0.28 SD        -> WORLD B
        none resolves and MDE >= 0.28 SD       -> WORLD C
        otherwise                              -> report; claim no world.
else:
        UNVERIFIED. Never OVERTURNED, never CONFIRMED.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONTROLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OBJECT     |ranked| = |rubricked| + |missing| exactly; |released| - |scored| = 18; every count
           printed. Exit 2 otherwise.
PLACEBO    the 968 against itself: no difference, exactly.
g=0        200 random 110-subsets of the 968 against their complements -> the null distribution of
           the rank statistic. **This is D3's world, built synthetically rather than assumed.**
POSITIVE   a synthetic 110-subgroup with a planted location shift, swept over {0, 0.25, 0.5, 1.0} SD;
           must NOT resolve at 0 and MUST resolve at 1.0. BAND, COMPUTED at both ends.
NEGATIVE   the group-label permutation, 200 draws — valid here (D3) and distinguished in writing from
           the void paired-mean permutations of ledger 1125 and 1129.
CONFOUND   flag RATE per ranking reported beside the raw flag count, because the two axes are
           mechanically coupled.
ROBUST     mean, median and rank statistic side by side, with the variance ratio printed, so the
           estimator choice is visible rather than silent.
UNIT       instrument unit = a parsed ranking record; claim unit = a PROMPT. Equal, and stated.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MULTIPLICITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2 axes (rankings per prompt, unacceptable flags) x 2 forms (raw, rate) x 3 estimators (mean, median,
rank) + 4 plant levels + 200 g=0 draws + 200 permutation draws. **12 primary cells**; the correction
is over all 12 and the non-survivors are printed.

   R737 proposed reading the blind-side matched floors off "the same curve". That curve is k=4 x k=4.
   The label-blind arms are k = 3, 4, 6 and 8, so three of the four comparisons are CROSS-k and
   using the k=4x4 curve for them would be the same unit error this arc has already made three
   times. Each (k_a, k_b) pair needs its own curve.

⛔ AND THE MODEL EXTENDS, VERIFIED BEFORE THE ROUND
   For independent components the raw correlation of two means over k_a and k_b sharing j is
   j / sqrt(k_a * k_b) -- checked synthetically at 18 cells across the four pairs, max deviation
   0.0032. With the shared per-response component R737 measured, the form is
       corr(j) = rho + (1 - rho) * j / sqrt(k_a * k_b)
   THIS IS ALGEBRA, used as the positive control, never as a finding.

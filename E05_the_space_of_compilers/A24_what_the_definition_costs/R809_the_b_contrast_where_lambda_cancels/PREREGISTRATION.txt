R809 · THE B CONTRAST ON A SCALE WHERE LAMBDA CANCELS
written before the run · NBOOT 1200 · ZEFF 2.801585

PROVOCATION
R808 returned B-SPECIFIC on an ADDITIVE contrast: fitted arms rise +0.186 from j=1 to j=8 while
honest arms rise +0.092, contrast +0.094 against a pre-registered threshold of 0.079 -- a ratio of
1.19, which R808 itself flagged as marginal and as the next round's first job. Its NEXT asked for a
bootstrap CI on that contrast.

⛔ CHECK #411 FOUND THE ARITHMETIC FIRST, AND IT IS THE MULTIPLICATIVE TRAP FOR THE THIRD TIME IN
FOUR ROUNDS (R806 relative-vs-additive; R807 the intercept; now this). Every disattenuated value is
raw_slope / lambda_j, and lambda_j FALLS with j: 0.5834, 0.5658, 0.5546, 0.4954. So every j=8 value
is inflated by a common multiplicative factor, and an arm STARTING HIGHER collects a larger ABSOLUTE
rise for free. The additive contrast cannot separate "rises faster" from "starts higher".

[DERIVATION] lambda_j is COMMON TO ALL ARMS at a given j, so in a difference of LOGS it cancels
exactly:  log dis(a,j) = log raw(a,j) - log lambda_j, hence
  [log dis(a,8) - log dis(a,1)] - [log dis(b,8) - log dis(b,1)]
is free of lambda entirely. That is the identified estimand, and it is the PRIMARY one here.

ESTIMAND
E1  the ADDITIVE contrast, bootstrapped over prompts -- R808's NEXT exactly as posed.
E2  ⭐ PRIMARY: the LOG contrast, bootstrapped over prompts. lambda cancels by derivation.
E3  a CALIBRATED j-sensitivity scale: floor = the honest arms, ceiling = `_target_full`, a synthetic
    arm that IS the full parity-1 modal class -- i.e. the fitted arms' actual fit target, hence
    maximal j-sensitivity by construction. Where do the fitted arms sit between them?
E4  the decomposition of R808's +0.094 into a LEVEL part and a RISE part.

IDENTIFICATION
E2 identified and lambda-free by the derivation above. E1 confounded by the common lambda_j factor
and reported only as E2's contrast. E3 identified as a POSITION between two measured ends.
⚠ A log ratio is undefined at or below zero. Every disattenuated value entering E2 must be > 0; any
arm-j cell that is not is printed and EXCLUDED, with the count stated, never silently dropped.

DERIVED BEFORE MEASURING
D1 [DERIVATION] the pure leak copy has dis = 1.000 at every j, so its log-rise is exactly 0. PLACEBO.
D2 [DERIVATION] `_target_full` is what the proxy converges TO, so its log-rise must be the largest
   of any arm. POSITIVE, with the honest arms as the floor of the same scale.
D3 the additive and log contrasts can disagree, and if they do the disagreement IS the finding --
   the additive one is then measuring level, not rise.
D4 lambda_j falling with j is itself worth checking: a modal class from more annotators is a
   sharper object, so its margin has more between-prompt variance and a half-split reproduces it
   less well. If lambda_j does NOT fall, this round's whole premise is wrong and it says so.

WORLDS

   on their fit target faster, and clause (3) can be written against a resolved claim.

   multiplicative trap and the B-axis returns UNVERIFIED; only R808's A-axis invariance survives.
C  UNVERIFIED: a control fails.
CHECKED FIRST: B, because it withdraws the previous round's finding.

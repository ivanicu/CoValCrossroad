Both cannot be the headline. R805 cited R295 for a lower-bound caveat and never reconciled them.

AND R295's VERDICT ITSELF HAS TWO UNRESOLVED CONFOUNDS, both cheap to test:
 (1) MULTIPLICATIVE vs ADDITIVE. R295 subtracts the honest arm's slope as a floor (`excess`). If
     half-agreement scales EVERY margin multiplicatively -- prompts where annotators agree are
     prompts where any predictor scores better -- then an arm with a LARGER pooled margin gets a
     larger slope with no leak at all. realstat §4 `difference of two bounded scores`: a covariate
     raising both arms yields a differential proportional to their gap. Additive subtraction cannot
     remove a multiplicative confound.
 (2) SHARED TERM. R295's binning variable is agreement between parity-1 and parity-0, while the
     outcome is scored on parity-0. R295 NAMED this in its own docstring and did not fix it. A
     binning variable containing the outcome's own draw inflates any slope.

ESTIMAND
E1  reproduce R295's committed profile exactly (OBJECT).
E2  ⭐ the MULTIPLICATIVE test: each arm's quintile margin divided by its OWN pooled margin. The
    estimand is the DIFFERENCE in relative profile between fitted and honest arms.
E3  ⭐ an INDEPENDENT binning variable: agreement WITHIN parity-1 only, which never touches the
    parity-0 outcome. Quintiles and slopes recomputed under it.
E4  the reconciliation: the fitted arms' bottom-quintile margin under the independent binning, with
    the honest floor subtracted, and an explicit verdict on which of R805/R295 stands.

IDENTIFICATION
E1/E2/E3 exact. ⚠ E2 is only informative if the honest arms have a NON-ZERO pooled margin -- a
ratio to something near zero is unbounded. `coval_core` +0.0236 and `topw_k4` +0.0215 on parity-0
(R805) are far from zero at the design's resolution, and the ratio's own CI is reported.

DERIVED BEFORE MEASURING
D1 [DERIVATION] a synthetic arm that IS parity-1's modal class must show the steepest agreement
   profile of anything, because agreement is exactly what makes parity-1 predict parity-0. This is
   the PERFECT-LEAK ceiling and it is the positive control.
D2 [DERIVATION] the pool against itself is exactly 0 in every quintile. PLACEBO.
D3 within-parity-1 agreement and half-agreement are correlated -- both measure annotator consensus
   on the prompt -- so E3 is a WEAKER instrument, not an independent replication. The correlation
   is reported and it bounds how much of the shared term is removed.
D4 R295's own positive control FAILED and it recorded why: `oracle_k4` was fitted on ALL annotators
   so it does not NEED the halves to agree. That prediction is inverted here, not reused.

DERIVED BEFORE MEASURING
D1 [DERIVATION] two noisy raters agree LESS than a noiseless predictor of their central tendency
   agrees with either. CEIL_H is the expected score of a ONE-NOISE-UNIT rater, not an upper bound.
D2 [DERIVATION] a strict (tie-free) predictor can never agree on a human-TIED pair, so it forfeits
   the tie mass (R803: 0.1397355039) outright. Arms are strict; annotators are not.
D3 the per-pair plurality ceiling CEIL_PLUR >= CEIL_ATT, with equality iff the human plurality is
   transitive on every prompt. The gap is a measurable fact nobody has.
D4 the k-consensus curve must be MONOTONE in k if it is measuring what it claims.

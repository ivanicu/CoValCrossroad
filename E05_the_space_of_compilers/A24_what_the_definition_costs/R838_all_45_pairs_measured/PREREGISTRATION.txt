⛔ THE FILTER I ALMOST USED, AND WHY IT IS THE SAME ERROR I RETRACTED ONE ROUND AGO.
   R837 measured three true MDEs: 0.0118, 0.0104, 0.0221. I then built an arithmetic filter --
   "a pair can only separate if gap > 2 * 0.0104" -- which leaves 2 of 45 pairs worth measuring and
   calls the other 43 FORCED. THAT FILTER ASSUMES MDE >= 0.0104 FOR EVERY PAIR, AND I HAVE NOT
   MEASURED THAT. MDE = ZEFF*sd(d)/sqrt(n), so a pair whose two arms differ SYSTEMATICALLY BUT
   SLIGHTLY has a small sd(d) and therefore a small MDE, and could separate on a small gap.
   R836 retracted a null that rested on an unmeasured resolution. Building a filter on an
   unmeasured lower bound is the same move, one round later, in the opposite direction.
   So: measure all 45. The recomputation is seconds per arm and the filter is not admissible.

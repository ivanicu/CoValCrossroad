R775 · four disjoint families — is the prompt component real, or an artifact of similar RULES?
WRITTEN BEFORE ANY CODE FOR THIS ROUND. 2026-08-05.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THIS ROUND EXISTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
R774 measured the cross-family correlation of the per-prompt scale at **+0.2438** against a measured
ceiling of **0.8620** -- 0.283x -- and registered a third family
(`greedy_k4_fit1, indep_k4_fit1, oracle_k4_fit1, gen, generic`) to turn one number into a distribution,
naming RULE-HETEROGENEITY as the confound it could not see.

⛔ CHECK #377: THAT FAMILY CARRIES A BIGGER CONFOUND THAN THE ONE I NAMED. Three of its five members
   are TARGET-READING (`greedy_k`, `indep_k`, `oracle_k` -- R760's rule off `select_core.py:102`), so
   they have access to the human labels. Their A2 is inflated by construction (R761: `oracle_k4`
   0.6283 against `coval_core` 0.5665) and their mutual differences are generated by HOW MUCH OF THE
   TARGET each read -- a different process, not a different rule. **And `generic` is the comparator
   itself (R765), so that family would have mixed three things at once.**

⭐ THE RELEASE SUPPORTS A CLEANER DESIGN, VERIFIED BEFORE WRITING THIS: 86 arms have full coverage
   (6 named, 17 target-reading, 63 selector), and FOUR families of five can be built with **zero
   shared OBJECTS** under R730's partition, no `_08b` foreign-judge members and no `_ctl`/`_det`
   replicas:
     F1 committed          coval_core, topw_k3, topw_k4, topw_k6, topw_k8        (weight selectors)
     F2 selector-by-seed   random_k4_s0/s1/s2, topabs_k4, topvar_k4              (R774's comparison)
     F3 target-reading     oracle_k4, oracle_k4_fit1, greedy_k4_fit1,
                           indep_k4_fit1, greedy_k4_greedy_kA                    (label readers)
     F4 selector-by-k      random_k2_s0, random_k3_s0, random_k6_s0,
                           random_k8_s0, random_k12_s0                           (size sweep)
   That gives **6 pairwise correlations** and separates the two axes: three SELECTOR-SELECTOR pairs
   (F1-F2, F1-F4, F2-F4) against three SELECTOR-TARGETREADER pairs (F1-F3, F2-F3, F4-F3).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ESTIMAND (before method)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E1  each family's split-half reliability, and therefore each PAIR's own attenuation ceiling
    `sqrt(rel_i * rel_j)` -- six ceilings, not one.
E2  the six cross-family correlations of the per-prompt scale, each reported RELATIVE to its own
    ceiling, never as raw numbers compared across pairs.
E3  ⭐ THE AXIS TEST: the mean relative correlation among SELECTOR-SELECTOR pairs against the mean
    among SELECTOR-TARGETREADER pairs. If the prompt component is real it should not care which class
    the arms come from; if it is an artifact of similar rules, within-class must exceed cross-class.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IDENTIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E1, E2 IDENTIFIED and exact given disjointness, which is asserted at the OBJECT level (R730) and the
   round EXITS 2 if violated -- a shared object manufactures the correlation being measured.
E3 PARTIALLY IDENTIFIED: 3 vs 3 pairs, and the six are NOT independent because each family appears in
   three of them (D3). So E3 is reported as a DIFFERENCE OF MEANS WITH ITS DEPENDENCE STATED, and its
   null is generated by permuting family labels rather than assumed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
population  968 prompts; four disjoint 5-arm families, compositions fixed above and printed
instrument  A2 per prompt over all annotators; the scale is the mean |pairwise A2 difference|
baseline    each pair's own measured ceiling; the axis null is generated by label permutation
regime      first release, home judge, this tree_sha
⚠ every member is default-judge: no `_08b` (R765 showed those are a different judge) and no
  `_ctl`/`_det` (R766 showed those are within-pass replicas).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
⛔ THE ARITHMETIC, DERIVED BEFORE MEASURING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D1  RAW CORRELATIONS ACROSS PAIRS ARE NOT COMPARABLE. Each pair has its own attenuation ceiling, and
    families differ in reliability, so a pair with a lower ceiling shows a lower correlation for
    reasons that have nothing to do with prompts. **Every number is divided by its OWN ceiling.**
D2  THE FAMILIES DIFFER IN LEVEL BY CONSTRUCTION. Target-readers score ~0.61 and selectors ~0.49, so a
    level difference exists before anything is measured and is NOT the finding. It is printed.
D3  THE SIX CORRELATIONS ARE NOT SIX INDEPENDENT NUMBERS. Four families produce six pairs and each
    family appears in three, so the effective degrees of freedom are fewer than six. **A mean over
    them is not a mean over independent observations**, and the axis test's null is therefore
    generated by permuting which families are labelled target-reading, not by a t-test.

STRONGEST CONFOUND, written BEFORE the run: the target-reading family's scale may be compressed
because its members all read the same labels and therefore agree more, which would lower its
correlations for a reason unrelated to rules. CONTROL IN THE SAME ITERATION: report each family's own
scale distribution and its internal mean pairwise |d|, so a compressed family is visible rather than
mistaken for a low cross-correlation.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WORLDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A · THE PROMPT COMPONENT IS CLASS-INDEPENDENT. Selector-selector and selector-targetreader relative
    correlations are comparable. Then R774's 0.283x is a genuine prompt property at that size, and the
    rule-heterogeneity confound is dead.
B · IT IS A RULE ARTIFACT. Within-class exceeds cross-class beyond the permutation null. Then what
    looks like a prompt component is arms built by similar rules moving together, and R772's scale
    loses its remaining prompt reading.
C · THE TARGET-READING FAMILY IS DEGENERATE. Its internal scale is far smaller than the others', so
    its low correlations are compression rather than class -- reported alongside, not instead.

PREDICTION MATRIX
  A -> |mean(selector-selector) - mean(selector-targetreader)| inside the permutation interval
  B -> selector-selector exceeds cross-class, above the permutation 97.5th percentile
  C -> F3's internal mean |d| below half of the other families'

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
KILL -- CONDITIONAL, gated on the controls
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
if DISJOINT holds at the OBJECT level (exit 2 otherwise)
   and POSITIVE fires (a planted prompt-level scale raises ALL six correlations)
   and g=0 fires (no plant -> none raised) and PLACEBO fires (a family against itself = 1.0):
        F3 internal |d| < half the others'      -> WORLD C, reported ALONGSIDE the rest.
        within-class > cross-class beyond null  -> WORLD B. The rule artifact is named.
        difference inside the null              -> WORLD A. The prompt component survives the axis.
else:
        UNVERIFIED. Never OVERTURNED, never CONFIRMED.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONTROLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DISJOINT   the four families share no OBJECT under R730's partition -- not merely no tag, because
           `oracle_k4` and `oracle_k4_oracle_kA` are one object. Verified before this file was
           written; asserted again in code, exit 2 on violation.
POSITIVE   plant a prompt-level scale multiplying every family's differences, sweep the width in
           {0, 0.25, 0.5, 1.0}, and require ALL SIX correlations to rise monotonely and be detected at
           1.0. BAND, COMPUTED: at width 0 none may be detected; at 1.0 all must be.
g=0        width 0 on simulated independent arms: the six correlations must sit inside the negative
           control's interval.
NEGATIVE   permute prompts in one family of each pair, 200 draws, and report the correlation
           distribution. World excluded: "any two scale vectors correlate".
SHAM       ingredient ABSENT, not inverted. Ingredient = THE FAMILIES BEING DIFFERENT ARM SETS. Split
           ONE family into overlapping halves and correlate them -- R774 measured this at +0.5097 and
           it is recomputed here for all four, so "what sharing alone buys" is a per-family number.
PLACEBO    a family's scale against itself = exactly 1.0.
NOISE      split-half reliability per family, 200 draws each, Spearman-Brown corrected, printed.
UNIT       instrument unit = a PROMPT; intermediate = an ARM PAIR within a family; claim unit = a
           FAMILY PAIR. Six of the last, and D3 says they are not six independent observations.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

+ 200 permutation draws. The whole matrix is printed, including the pairs that contradict the reading.

**All three follow from one fact: it documents an era the other two moved past.** R566 framed this as

document is not beside the other two, it is behind them.**

## ⚠ What the ranges do NOT establish
**Separation is measured; supersession is not.** R566 found **24 shared decimal values** between
STATEMENT and FORMULATION even though they share no rounds — **the same quantities may have been
re-measured later**, which would make FORMULATION superseded, or they may be different quantities
that coincide. **The ranges cannot tell those apart, and "superseded" is the flattering reading
because it retires an unaudited document by narrative rather than by evidence.**

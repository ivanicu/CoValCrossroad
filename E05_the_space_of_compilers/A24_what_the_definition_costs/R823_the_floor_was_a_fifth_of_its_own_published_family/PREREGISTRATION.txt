DERIVED FIRST -- BEFORE ANY COMPUTE
  D1  ⛔ MAX OVER A SUPERSET IS MONOTONE NON-DECREASING. "The floor rises" is FORCED and is NOT a
      finding. The measurable questions are BY HOW MUCH, WHETHER IT CROSSES ANY ARM, and HOW MUCH
      OF THE RISE IS WINNER'S CURSE. Reporting the rise itself as evidence is the arithmetic trap.
  D2  A max over m draws rises with m even when every draw is pure noise. So the 6->30 rise
      decomposes into REAL CLASS RICHNESS + SELECTION. The sham (30 RANDOM rules, matched size)
      measures the selection part; only the excess over the sham is attributable to the features.
  D3  R435's numbers are on 7,342 items / 2,200 conversations; R803's on 968 prompts. THE TWO ARE
      NOT COMPARABLE AS PUBLISHED. Every rule is recomputed here on R803's population so the
      comparison is within one population. R435's 0.5135 for max_len_chars is NOT expected to
      reproduce and its non-reproduction is not evidence of anything.
  D4  ④ says "better than EVERY rule computable from the response set alone". The in-sample max
      over a finite family UNDERSTATES an infinite class; the held-out max is the honest estimate
      of what a rule that must generalise can reach. Both are reported; neither alone is the bar.

IDENTIFICATION
E1/E2/E3 exact under the split. ⚠ E3(a) is NOT a clean leak estimate on its own: the two
evaluations use different annotator sets, so a difference could be sampling. The control for that
is an HONEST arm (no prompt labels used) evaluated the same two ways -- it must show no such gap.

DERIVED BEFORE MEASURING
D1 [DERIVATION] an arm fitted on parity-1 scores higher on parity-1 than on parity-0, or the fit
   did nothing. This is the POSITIVE control and it is forced only in SIGN, not in size.
D2 [DERIVATION] CEIL_HO_p0 <= CEIL_ATT_p0. A violation means the code is wrong.
D3 the parity halves are not independent: R295 measured that the fit1 advantage concentrates where
   the halves agree, so parity-0 is a PROXY for parity-1 and this design UNDERSTATES the leak.
   Every leak number here is therefore a LOWER BOUND, and is reported as one.
D4 an honest arm's parity-0 and all-annotator scores differ only by sampling.

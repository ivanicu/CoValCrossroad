R752 · the MDE refuses the comparison, so the round is a census instead
WRITTEN BEFORE ANY CODE FOR THIS ROUND. 2026-08-05.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THIS ROUND EXISTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
R751's pooled annotation detector INVERTED its own SHAM -- supported figures carry annotation
keywords more often than flagged ones -- so the precision question was unanswerable and the verdict
was UNVERIFIED. Splitting the keywords showed the two meaning "this figure has no source"
(`ungrounded`, `corrected`) DO point the right way. R751 refused to report that subset, because
choosing it after seeing the breakdown is picking the specification from the outcome, and closed by
demanding the restricted detector be PREREGISTERED and its MDE computed BEFORE the run.

⛔ COMPUTING THE MDE FIRST IS WHAT THIS PREREGISTRATION DOES, AND IT KILLS THE COMPARISON.
n_flagged = 33, n_supported = 150. For a two-proportion test at alpha 0.05 two-sided, power 0.80:

    pbar 0.05 -> SE 0.0419 -> MDE 0.1174
    pbar 0.10 -> SE 0.0577 -> MDE 0.1616
    pbar 0.15 -> SE 0.0687 -> MDE 0.1924

The gaps the restricted keywords showed are `ungrounded` 0.0606 - 0.0400 = 0.0206 and `corrected`
0.0606 - 0.0200 = 0.0406. BOTH ARE 3 TO 6 TIMES BELOW THE SMALLEST MDE. Running the comparison would
produce a null that means nothing, and a null from a design that could not have detected the effect
is silence, not an acquittal.

So the round does not run the comparison. It MEASURES the refusal and does what IS identified at
this n.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ESTIMAND
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E1  the MDE of the restricted-detector comparison across the design grid, and the sample size that
    WOULD be required to detect the gaps observed.
E2  an exhaustive CENSUS of the figures the restricted detector flags -- at most 4 -- read one by
    one, with a per-figure verdict rather than a rate.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IDENTIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E1 IDENTIFIED, and it is a DERIVATION from the design, not a measurement of the world -- so it is
   VALIDATED BY SIMULATION rather than asserted. A formula quoted without checking that the test
   actually rejects at the claimed rate is arithmetic wearing a lab coat.
E2 IDENTIFIED and exact: n <= 4 is a census. There is no sampling uncertainty and NO POWER TO
   GENERALISE, and no rate is computed from it -- 4 items produce 4 verdicts, never a percentage.
E3 THE RATE COMPARISON IS NOT IDENTIFIED AT THIS n and is REFUSED, with the refusal computed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
population  E1 the design (33 vs 150) · E2 the restricted-flagged figures on STATEMENT.md
instrument  a simulated two-proportion test for E1; direct reading for E2
baseline    R751's committed rates, which supply the gaps the MDE is compared against
regime      this tree_sha

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WORLDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A · THE DESIGN CAN SEE IT. MDE <= the larger observed gap (0.0406), so the comparison is admissible
    and should be run.
B · THE DESIGN IS BLIND. MDE > 0.0406, so the comparison is inadmissible, any null it returns is
    silence, and the honest output is a required-n plus a census.

PREDICTION MATRIX      A -> MDE <= 0.0406            B -> MDE > 0.0406

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
I computed the MDE band 0.1174-0.1924 in the arithmetic step above, before writing this file. So
World B is ALREADY DECIDED and I register no blind prediction about it. Fifth declared sighting in
seven rounds. What remains blind: the SIMULATED rejection rate at the analytic MDE, the required n,
and every E2 verdict.

DIRECTIONAL, separately failable

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
⛔ THE ARITHMETIC, DERIVED BEFORE MEASURING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FORCED, labelled derivations, not evidence:
  - MDE falls as 1/sqrt(n), so a larger n always lowers it. "More data would help" is algebra.
  - required_n rises as 1/gap^2, so halving the gap quadruples the requirement. The SHAPE is forced;
    only the CONSTANT is worth printing.
  - a census of 4 has no interval, and none is reported.

STRONGEST CONFOUND, written BEFORE the run: the analytic MDE assumes a normal approximation, which
is poor at these proportions and these counts -- 0.05 x 33 is under 2 expected events. CONTROL IN
THE SAME ITERATION: the simulation uses the EXACT test the analytic formula approximates, and if the
simulated rejection rate at the analytic MDE misses 0.80 badly, the FORMULA is what failed and the
refusal must be restated on the simulated MDE instead.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
KILL -- CONDITIONAL, gated on the controls
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
if the simulation's g=0 rejection rate is within [0.02, 0.09] (the test is calibrated) and the
planted-MDE rejection rate is within [0.70, 0.90] (the formula is honest):
        MDE <= 0.0406  -> WORLD B KILLED; run the comparison in a later round.
        MDE >  0.0406  -> WORLD A KILLED; the comparison is REFUSED, the required n is reported, and
                          the census stands as the round's only claim about the page.
else:
        UNVERIFIED -- and specifically the FORMULA is unverified, which is a different failure from
        the design being blind. The two must not be reported as one.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONTROLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
POSITIVE   plant a difference of exactly the analytic MDE in simulated data at the real n, and
           require the test to reject at ~80%. BAND, COMPUTED: floor = the rejection rate at zero
           planted difference (which is alpha, ~0.05), ceiling = the rejection rate at a maximal
           plant (difference 1.0, which is 1.00). The threshold 0.80 sits strictly inside
           [0.05, 1.00], asserted in code rather than argued.
g=0        zero planted difference must reject at ~alpha, NOT more. A test rejecting at 0.30 under
           the null would make every MDE claim above it meaningless.
NEGATIVE   re-run the same simulation at 10x n. The MDE must fall by ~sqrt(10) and the observed gap
           must become detectable. World excluded: "the statistic can never see a gap this small",
           which would mean the problem is the estimator rather than the sample.
SHAM       the same operation with the INGREDIENT ABSENT, not inverted. Ingredient = the arm-size
           IMBALANCE. Recompute with both arms at the harmonic mean of 33 and 150 -- total events
           held as close as the arithmetic allows -- and report whether the MDE moves materially.
PLACEBO    the analytic MDE computed twice must differ by exactly 0.
NOISE      the simulation is seeded and swept over 3 seeds; the rejection rates are reported per
           seed, never averaged into a single number without their spread.
UNIT       instrument unit for E1 = a simulated TRIAL; claim unit = the DESIGN. For E2, instrument
           unit = a FIGURE and claim unit = a FIGURE, which is the one place in this round where
           they are equal -- and that equality is exactly why the census is admissible where the
           rate is not.

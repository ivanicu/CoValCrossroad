R843 · THE SOURCE × ACCESS FULL FACTORIAL — closing the cell R811 called structurally absent

WHY THIS ROUND AND NOT ANOTHER NEXT. An external reading of the last 116 commits found the loop had
entered a self-audit attractor: it kept discovering that the previous audit's object, unit,
population or control had drifted, and stopped moving the world model. Its prescription was ONE
remaining internal closure, then leave this universe. This is that closure.

R811 decomposed a confounded gap into SOURCE (whose criteria) and RULE (how they are chosen) and
reported them SEPARATELY, with one cell missing:

    "⚠ pool x informative CANNOT be built: the pool is a FIXED generic set of 16 with no
     per-prompt human weights."

⭐ THAT SENTENCE IS TRUE ABOUT THE ARTIFACT AND FALSE ABOUT THE LOGIC. It says the pool has no
per-prompt HUMAN WEIGHTS. It does not say the pool cannot be selected from per prompt — only that
the one selector anyone had built reads human weights. A selector reading the PROMPT TEXT and
nothing else is label-free, constructible today, and lands exactly in the empty cell. And once the
axis is written as ACCESS rather than as RULE, the 2x2 is revealed to have been a 2x4 all along.

⛔ AND REPORTING TWO EFFECTS SEPARATELY ASSUMES THEY ADD. R811 gave a RULE effect and a SOURCE
effect at matched k without ever measuring their interaction, because the missing cell is precisely
the one that identifies it. If Y(S,A) != alpha_S + beta_A then neither number means what it was
reported to mean, and that is a claim about work already published in FORMULATION.md.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE DESIGN

SOURCE (whose criteria are in the candidate set)
  S1  the prompt's OWN human rubric              — per-prompt, authored against this conversation
  S2  the FIXED generic pool of 16               — identical for all 968 prompts

ACCESS (what information the selector may consume, in increasing order)
  A0  none          uninformative / random selection
  A1  prompt text   ⭐ reads the conversation, reads NO human label anywhere
  A2  other labels  fit on OTHER prompts' human importance, never this prompt's
  A3  this prompt   ranks by THIS prompt's human importance — the leaky upper bound

Eight cells, k in {2,4,8}. EVERY CELL IS SCORED FROM COMMITTED SATISFACTION MATRICES: `sat_full`
carries every rubric criterion and `sat_genericpool16` carries all sixteen, for all four responses
of every prompt. So the factorial requires NO new judging — the only new object is the SELECTOR,
which is why the missing cell was never a compute problem.

ESTIMANDS
  E1  the 8-cell table Y(S,A) at each k, Y = A2 agreement on the even-indexed annotator half
  E2  Δ_source(a) = Y(S1,a) − Y(S2,a)          — does whose-criteria matter, AT EACH ACCESS LEVEL
  E3  Δ_access(s) = Y(s,A_{j+1}) − Y(s,A_j)    — the dose-response in label access, per source
  E4  ⭐ Γ = Δ_source(A1) − Δ_source(A0)        — NON-ADDITIVITY. R811's separate reporting is only
                                                 licensed if Γ sits on its own floor.
  E5  does S2×A1 (generic criteria, contextualised, no labels) reach S1×A3 (own rubric, human
      weights)? If it does, the object is CONTEXTUALISATION, not authorship.

DERIVED FIRST — BEFORE ANY COMPUTE
  D1  A3 >= A0 IS NOT FORCED. Ranking by human importance optimises a stated weight, not agreement;
      R811 measured `topw` beating `random`, so the direction is empirical, but its SIZE is what
      this round reads and no algebra fixes it.
  D2  ⛔ A0 FOR S2 IS ENUMERABLE, NOT SAMPLED. C(16,2)=120, C(16,4)=1820, C(16,8)=12870 — the whole
      subset distribution is computed, so S2×A0 is an EXACT mean, not an estimate, and `POOL[0:k]`
      (which R811 showed sits near the 96th percentile) is NOT used as the cell.
  D3  A1 AND A2 SELECT FROM THE SAME CANDIDATE SET AS A0 AND A3 within a source. Any difference is
      the selector, never the candidates. This is what makes the row a dose-response rather than
      four unrelated arms.
  D4  This round cannot show that NO label-free selector reaches A3 — that quantifies over an
      infinite class. It shows where two constructible ones land.

WORLDS
  W-ACCESS   the table is ordered by ACCESS and barely by SOURCE: A0<A1<A2<A3 within both rows,
             |Δ_source| small at every level. The target rewards label consumption, clause ③
             forbids it, and the conflict is structural.
  W-SOURCE   the table is ordered by SOURCE: S1 dominates S2 at matched access. Prompt-specific
             authorship carries the content and clause ③ costs little.
  W-CONTEXT  S2×A1 reaches S1×A3. Neither authorship nor labels is the object — APPLICABILITY is,
             and a fixed pool correctly contextualised is as good as a bespoke rubric.
  W-JOINT    Γ is resolvably non-zero. Source and access do not decompose; R811's two numbers are
             not separately interpretable and FORMULATION.md inherits the correction.
  ⚠ These are NOT exclusive. W-JOINT can hold with any of the others, and the kill evaluates it
    independently rather than picking one winner — a forced choice among non-exclusive worlds is
    how a prediction matrix manufactures a verdict.

PREDICTION MATRIX
  W-ACCESS  -> both rows monotone in A, every step above its floor; max_a |Δ_source(a)| <= floor
  W-SOURCE  -> Δ_source(a) > floor at every a, and > any single Δ_access step
  W-CONTEXT -> Y(S2,A1) >= Y(S1,A3) − floor
  W-JOINT   -> |Γ| > 1.96 se(Γ), se from the paired prompt bootstrap

CONTROLS
  OBJECT    S1×A3 at k in {2,4,8} must reproduce the committed `topw_k*` arms, and S1×A0 the
            committed `random_k*_s0`, to <2e-3. These are the SAME selections recomputed by this
            round's own code from `sat_full`; if they disagree, the selector is not what the arm
            is and nothing below is comparable to the record. Exit 2.
  FORCED    every cell <= CEIL_ATT 0.686265 (R804's in-sample supremum over weak orders). A
            DERIVATION, asserted, not evidence.
  PLACEBO   the A1 relevance scores replaced by a permutation of themselves ACROSS prompts, so a
            criterion keeps its score but meets the wrong conversation. Must land at A0.
  SHAM      A1 with random relevance, matched in selection cardinality. ⭐ This is the control that
            makes A1 mean anything: any per-prompt selector, informative or not, differs from a
            single fixed subset, and only the excess over the sham is the text's contribution.
  NEGATIVE  a synthetic arm resampled from the cell's own per-prompt distribution.
  POSITIVE  dose-response against a PLANT: a synthetic target generated from a known criterion
            subset, on which A3 must recover it and A0 must not, at g=1 and g=0.
  NOISE     A0 over >=5 seeds for S1; for S2 the exact enumeration replaces the seed spread.
  MULTIPLICITY  8 cells x 3 k = 24, plus 6 contrasts. BH q=0.05, non-survivors printed.

PRE-REGISTERED KILL — conditional, gated on the controls
  if OBJECT reproduces and PLACEBO lands at A0 and SHAM is below A1 and POSITIVE recovers at g=1
     and fails at g=0 and no cell exceeds CEIL_ATT:
         report each world's predicate INDEPENDENTLY, W-JOINT first because it conditions the
         interpretation of the other three
  else:
         UNVERIFIED — never OVERTURNED, never CONFIRMED

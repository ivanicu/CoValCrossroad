ESTIMAND
E1  the best constant weak order, HELD OUT: chosen on one half of the prompts, evaluated on the
    other. R804's 0.451773 is an in-sample optimum over 75 options and is the confounded contrast.
E2  ⭐ every arm's share of the INFORMATIVE range `[floor, ceiling]`, beside its share of
    `[0, ceiling]` — the quantity the arc has been quoting.
E3  the per-prompt version: `(A2_p − floor_p) / (att_p − floor_p)` averaged over prompts, where
    `floor_p` is the corpus-constant order evaluated on prompt p.
E4  which committed statements change meaning once the floor is subtracted.

IDENTIFICATION
⚠⚠ E2's CORPUS-LEVEL rescaling is AFFINE — subtract a constant, divide by a constant — and therefore
**CANNOT reorder the arms. That is a DERIVATION, not a measurement**, and it is labelled as one.
The only part of this round that CAN come out otherwise is E3, the per-prompt version, because
`att_p − floor_p` varies across prompts and the reweighting is not affine.
⚠ Prompts where `att_p == floor_p` make E3's ratio undefined; they are counted, printed, and
excluded, and if they exceed 10% the round reports E3 as UNVERIFIED.

DERIVED BEFORE MEASURING
D1 [DERIVATION] a corpus-constant floor and ceiling give an affine map, so E2's ordering is fixed.
   Stating this first is what stops "the ordering survived" being reported as a result.
D2 [DERIVATION] the held-out floor is <= the in-sample floor, so the held-out informative range is
   WIDER and every arm's share is LOWER than it would be with R804's 0.451773. The correction
   therefore runs AGAINST the arms, and saying so before the run is what makes it reportable.
D3 an arm that IS the constant order scores exactly the floor and takes share 0. PLACEBO.
D4 `genericpool16` is prompt-blind in its CRITERIA but its judge SCORES vary per prompt, so it is
   not a constant arm and should sit above the floor. A testable expectation, not an assumption.

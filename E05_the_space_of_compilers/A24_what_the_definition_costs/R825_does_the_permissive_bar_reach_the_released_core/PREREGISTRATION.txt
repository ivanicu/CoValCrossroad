R825 · DOES ④'s PERMISSIVE BAR REACH THE RELEASED CORE?

CHECK #427. R824's NEXT proposed sweeping capacity to find whether the permissive bar saturates. It
passes the arithmetic rung — R823 measured a held-out bar FALLING (0.456094 -> 0.454779) as its class
grew, so saturation is not forced. But the line UNDERSTATES its own stake, and the check found the
sharper form:

  · R824 adopted the permissive reading. Its bar is 0.519689 from 14 lexical features.
  · `coval_core`, the RELEASED core, scores 0.566477.
  · THE GAP IS 0.046789. ④ says an admissible core must beat every response-only rule.
  · So if a richer response-only predictor closes 0.0468, ④ EXCLUDES THE RELEASED CORE, and the
    definition this deliverable just adopted admits nothing that matters.
  · No round in 400 has built a response-only predictor with TEXT features. R824 used 14 hand-built
    lexical scalars; n-grams and their subspaces are an entire unexplored family.

That is not "does the bar saturate". It is "does the definition survive its own adopted reading".

ESTIMAND
  E1  the held-out permissive bar at four FEATURE TIERS x six model capacities, all fit inside the
      split. Tier 1 reproduces R824; tiers 2-4 add char n-grams, word n-grams, and both.
  E2  ⭐ the gap from the best tier to `coval_core` 0.566477, with a CI, and ④'s exclusion count
      including whether `coval_core` itself is excluded
  E3  saturation: the tier-to-tier rise against the per-tier noise floor and against the per-tier SHAM
  E4  the contamination audit: the same sweep with the vectoriser fit on ALL prompts, so the LEAK
      MAGNITUDE is measured rather than assumed absent

DERIVED FIRST -- BEFORE ANY COMPUTE
  D1  ⛔ IN-SAMPLE CAPACITY MONOTONICITY IS FORCED. A richer class contains a poorer one, so an
      in-sample rise proves nothing. Every figure is HELD OUT, and R823 already measured a held-out
      bar falling as its class grew, so the held-out direction is genuinely open.
  D2  ⛔ ANY A2 IS BOUNDED BY THE GENERALISING CEILING. R804 measured CEIL_HO = 0.633370 (the best
      weak order FITTED on one annotator half, scored on the other) and CEIL_ATT = 0.686265 (the
      in-sample supremum over all 75 weak orders). A response-only predictor must generalise, so
      0.633370 bounds it. A measured bar above that is an INSTRUMENT FAILURE, not a finding, and is
      checked as a forced assertion before any verdict.
  D3  A TF-IDF vocabulary fit on all prompts LEAKS the evaluation half's text. The vectoriser is
      fit inside each split. E4 measures what that guard is worth rather than asserting it works.
      ⛔ AMENDED MID-ROUND, BEFORE ANY VERDICT WAS READ. D3 named ONE of THREE unsupervised stages.
      The SVD basis and the z-score were also fit on all prompts, so both saw the evaluation half —
      TRANSDUCTIVE leakage, and E4 IS STRUCTURALLY BLIND TO IT because the SVD leaks in both of
      E4's conditions. A leak present in the control as well as the treatment is invisible to that
      control (§4). All three stages are now fit inside the split. The amendment is recorded here
      rather than silently applied, and no result had been read when it was made.
  D4  This round cannot establish that NO response-only rule reaches 0.566477 — that quantifies over
      an infinite class. It can establish where the bar sits for the richest family anyone here has
      built, and whether it is still RISING at that point. A non-saturating curve is itself the
      finding, because ④ then has no evaluable bar without a stated modelling budget.

WORLDS
  A  SATURATES BELOW THE CORE — the tier-to-tier rise falls inside the noise floor and the bar stays
     resolvably below 0.566477. ④ is stable, the adopted reading is safe, and the modelling-budget
     worry is empirically empty.
  B  REACHES THE CORE — the bar is not resolvably below 0.566477, so ④ excludes `coval_core` itself.
     The definition as adopted in R824 admits nothing that matters and must be repaired or the
     permissive reading abandoned.
  C  RISES WITHOUT SATURATING — the bar climbs past every tier's noise floor and is still climbing
     at tier 4 while remaining below the core. ④ is not evaluable without a fixed modelling budget,
     which is a clause-text problem rather than a measurement.

PREDICTION MATRIX
  A -> tier4 - tier3 <= noise floor; tier4 + 1.96se < 0.566477
  B -> tier4 CI overlaps or exceeds 0.566477
  C -> tier4 - tier3 > noise floor AND tier4 + 1.96se < 0.566477

CONTROLS
  OBJECT     tier 1 with logistic C=1.0 must reproduce R824's 0.519689 to <2e-3 (20 vs 10 splits, so
             not byte-equal; the tolerance is stated in advance). Exit 2 otherwise.
  FORCED     every measured bar <= CEIL_HO 0.633370. A DERIVATION, checked as an assertion.
  PLACEBO    shuffled pairwise labels at fit, AT EVERY TIER -> must land at chance, not above.
  POSITIVE   dose-response scored AGAINST THE PLANT, g in {1.0, 0.5, 0.2, 0.0}, monotone, recovering
             at g=1 and at chance at g=0. ⛔ R824's first version scored the plant against the REAL
             human labels and its g=0 arm returned nan; this is the corrected form.
  NEGATIVE   synthetic arm resampled from the bar's own per-prompt distribution. NOT a permutation
             (R821: ④'s statistic is permutation-invariant by algebra).
  SHAM ⭐    SWEPT PER TIER. Random features matched in DIMENSION at each tier. This is the whole
             design: higher capacity overfits more, so a rise at tier 4 is attributable to the
             features ONLY in excess of what the same dimensionality of noise buys. A sham measured
             at one tier only would license nothing about the others.
  LEAK       E4 is itself a contamination audit: vectoriser fit on ALL prompts vs inside the split.
             The difference is the leak the guard prevents, MEASURED.
  NOISE FLOOR  per tier, across splits.

MULTIPLICITY   4 tiers x 6 capacities x 2 leak conditions = 48 cells, plus 58 arms at the best cell.
               BH q=0.05 over the arm tests; non-survivors printed. The bar is reported as the CURVE.

PRE-REGISTERED KILL -- conditional, gated on the controls
  if placebo at chance at every tier and positive recovers and fails at g=0 and negative on zero
     and every bar <= CEIL_HO:
        if bar_best + 1.96*se >= 0.566477:                                  WORLD B
        elif (tier4 - tier3) <= noise_floor_tier4 and bar_best + 1.96*se < 0.566477:  WORLD A
        else:                                                                WORLD C
  else:
        UNVERIFIED   -- never OVERTURNED, never CONFIRMED

STOPPING RULE   WORLD B forces a repair to the definition adopted one round ago, landing in
DEFINITION.md's head, its clause table, STATEMENT.md and the ledger IN THE SAME COMMIT. WORLD C
forces a budget parameter into ④'s text or an explicit statement that ④ is not evaluable without one.

RESOLUTION -- AMENDED, and it should have been here from the start. §1 G2 requires the MDE and this
preregistration stated a kill without one. Measured from the held-out bar's own per-split sd of
0.007144 at C=1.0: se = 0.002526 at the design's n=8 splits, MDE(80% power) = 0.007072. The gap the
round must close is 0.046788, i.e. 6.6x the MDE, so the design CAN resolve WORLD B. The budget cut
from 20 splits to 8 moved the MDE from 0.004473 to 0.007072 and therefore did NOT compromise the
question -- that is a number rather than an assertion. The lexical-sd channel measured in
audit_lexical_sd.py is -0.000204 = 0.029x the MDE.

R810 · IS THE FITTED ARMS' LEVEL ADVANTAGE SIZE OR FITTING?
written before the run · NBOOT 1200 · ZEFF 2.801585

PROVOCATION
R809 closed the B-axis: relative sensitivity to the leak proxy's identity does NOT distinguish
fitted from honest arms, and `topw_k4` -- which uses no human label for its prompt -- has the
steepest log-rise of any real arm. What still separates the groups is LEVEL: fitted arms sit at
0.383 against honest 0.195 at j=1, a ratio of 1.97x. The fitted arms measured so far are k=4 while
`genericpool16` carries 16 criteria and still scores below them, so SIZE and FITTING are confounded
throughout this arc's numbers about the fitted route.

⛔ AND R809's NEXT NAMED AN INFEASIBLE INSTRUMENT. It proposed `--rule oracle_k` across
k = {2,4,8,12}. CHECK #412 measured the enumeration: `select_core.py` caps at 20,000 combinations
and SAMPLES above it, logging the arm as a lower bound. Candidate criteria per prompt: median 15,
max 39, min 4. Prompts over the cap: k=2 -> 0, k=4 -> 31, k=8 -> 367, k=12 -> 254 of 968. So the
oracle's IDENTITY changes with k and a sweep on it would confound size with sampling density.
`greedy_k` and `indep_k` are sequential and linear in k, feasible at every k, and are used instead.

ESTIMAND
E1  the fitted-minus-honest LEVEL gap in A2 on parity-0, AT MATCHED k, for k in {2,4,8,12}.
E2  ⭐ the gap's TREND in k: does it close, hold, or widen?
E3  the EFFECTIVE k actually emitted, per arm per nominal k -- measured, not assumed. Already known
    from generation: mean k = 2.00 at k=2, 7.92 at k=8, 11.32 at k=12.
E4  the same gap against the size-matched PROMPT-BLIND pool POOL[0:k].

IDENTIFICATION
E1/E2 identified only on prompts where EVERY arm attains its nominal k. Candidate sets go as low as
4, so at k=8 and k=12 some prompts cannot supply enough criteria and the emitted arm is silently
smaller. That population is computed in E3, the comparison is restricted to it, and the dropped
count is printed. ⚠ Restricting makes k=12 a DIFFERENT population from k=2, so the gap is ALSO
reported on the common intersection across all four k, and both are shown.

DERIVED BEFORE MEASURING
D1 [DERIVATION] `topw_k` at k = all admissible criteria IS `full`, so the honest curve must converge
   to `full`'s A2 as k grows. If it does not, the sweep is not doing what its name says.
D2 [DERIVATION] a fitted arm at k = n is ALSO `full` -- selection has nothing left to choose. So the
   gap MUST close at k = n by construction; the question is whether it closes at k values the
   release can actually reach. Stating this BEFORE the run is what separates a finding from algebra.
D3 the blind pool POOL[0:k] is prompt-blind at every k, so it is the cleanest size control.
D4 fitted arms are fitted on parity-1 and scored on parity-0; honest arms never see a label, so no
   split is needed for them and they are scored on the same parity-0.

WORLDS
A  SIZE: the gap closes as k grows, materially before k = n -> the fitted route's advantage is how
   many criteria it carries, and R807's 0.50-0.65 is about size.
B  FITTING: the gap holds or widens at matched k -> fitting is the ingredient after all.
C  UNIDENTIFIED: effective k diverges from nominal so badly that no matched population survives.
CHECKED FIRST: A, because it reinterprets R805 and R807.

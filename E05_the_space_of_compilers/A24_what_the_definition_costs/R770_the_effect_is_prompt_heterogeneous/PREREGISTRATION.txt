R770 · the difference is prompt-heterogeneous — so is the extension unordered, or PARTITIONED?
WRITTEN BEFORE ANY CODE FOR THIS ROUND. 2026-08-05.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THIS ROUND EXISTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
R769's NEXT registered "the per-prompt difference sd regressed on the strata the release ships".

⛔ CHECK #372: THAT QUANTITY DOES NOT EXIST AS WRITTEN. The difference vector holds ONE value per
   prompt, so it has no per-prompt sd -- an sd computed ACROSS prompts cannot be regressed ON prompts.
   SEVENTH closing line in this arc that the next round's first check had to repair.
   ⭐ BUT THE ANNOTATOR DIMENSION SUPPLIES A REAL ONE. `A2(p)` is a mean over that prompt's
   annotators, so `d(p)` has its own standard error from the annotator draw, and prompts with more
   annotators have smaller ones. That is the well-posed version of what I meant.

MEASURED BEFORE DESIGNING (`coval_core` vs `topw_k4`, 968 prompts, all annotators):
   per-prompt difference mean **+0.002297**, sd ACROSS prompts **0.094709**
   per-prompt SE from the annotator draw: median **0.029264**
   mean WITHIN-prompt variance **0.00168938**  ·  ACROSS-prompt variance **0.00896983**
   ⭐ **ratio 5.310** -- the across-prompt variance is 5.3x the annotator variance.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ESTIMAND (before method)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E1  the variance decomposition of `d(p)` into a WITHIN-prompt (annotator) component and a BETWEEN-
    prompt component, per committed pair, and the bound it places on the annotator lever.
E2  the heterogeneity of the between-prompt component: I^2 and the share of prompts whose own
    interval excludes the pooled mean, per pair.
E3  ⭐ THE OBJECT QUESTION. If the effect is prompt-heterogeneous, "arm a beats arm b" is a
    POPULATION AVERAGE that can hide each arm winning on a different subpopulation. So: is the
    extension an UNORDERED SET (R768) or a PARTITION -- different members best on different prompts?
    Measured as the sign balance of `d(p)` and as the ordering WITHIN pre-registered strata.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IDENTIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E1, E2 IDENTIFIED and exact -- both components are computable from the annotator-level agreements.
E3 IDENTIFIED ONLY FOR STRATA THAT ARE INDEPENDENT OF THE OUTCOME. §4's `conditioning on the outcome`
   row forbids binning on `d(p)` or on either arm's own score. The admissible strata are therefore
   fixed BEFORE the run and are properties of the PROMPT, not of the comparison:
       S1 annotator count (quartiles of n_annotators)
       S2 response-set size (number of distinct criteria the arms scored on that prompt)
       S3 the BASELINE's A2 on that prompt -- admissible because the baseline is neither arm
   ⚠ ANY stratum derived from `d(p)`, from `A2_a(p)` or from `A2_b(p)` is EXCLUDED BY CONSTRUCTION,
     and the round names this rather than relying on care.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
population  968 prompts; the 5 committed extension members (10 pairs)
instrument  annotator-level agreement vectors; paired per-prompt differences; B = 1200
baseline    none for the pairs; the baseline appears only as stratum S3 and in controls
regime      first release, home judge, this tree_sha

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
⛔ THE ARITHMETIC, DERIVED BEFORE MEASURING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D1  THE ANNOTATOR LEVER IS BOUNDED BY THE DECOMPOSITION. total var = between + within/n_annot. With
    a ratio of 5.31, driving the annotator count to INFINITY removes only the within share --
    1/(1+5.31) = **15.8%** of the total -- so the MDE falls by at most 1 - sqrt(1-0.158) = **8.3%**.
    **That is a DERIVATION and it confirms R769's "exhausted" from a second, independent direction,
    with a number instead of a count.**
D2  INVERSE-VARIANCE WEIGHTING IS NOT FREE PRECISION HERE. It is the efficient estimator of a COMMON
    effect. With genuine between-prompt heterogeneity the per-prompt effects are not one number, so
    weighting changes the ESTIMAND -- from "the average over this prompt population" to "a
    precision-weighted average" -- and reporting it as a tighter interval on the same quantity would
    be the G1 error this campaign has made at three levels. It is computed and labelled, never
    substituted.
D3  A SIGN SPLIT NEAR 50/50 IS EXPECTED WHENEVER THE MEAN IS SMALL RELATIVE TO THE SPREAD, and here
    mean/sd = 0.0023/0.0947 = 0.024. **So "the signs are balanced" is nearly forced and is NOT
    evidence of a partition.** The partition question must be answered by whether an ordering FLIPS
    within a pre-registered stratum, not by the sign count.

STRONGEST CONFOUND, written BEFORE the run: a stratum can produce an apparent flip purely by
multiplicity -- 10 pairs x 3 strata x 4 levels is 120 cells, and some will differ by chance. CONTROL
IN THE SAME ITERATION: BH over the whole 120-cell grid, and a SHAM stratum -- a random partition of
the prompts into the same number of equally-sized levels -- reporting how many flips it produces.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WORLDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A · UNORDERED AND HOMOGENEOUS IN DIRECTION. The between-prompt spread is large but no pre-registered
    stratum flips an ordering beyond what the sham produces. R768 stands unchanged and the extension
    is a set, not a partition.
B · PARTITIONED. At least one pair's ordering flips across levels of a pre-registered stratum, and
    survives BH and the sham. Then the definition admits arms that are best on DIFFERENT prompts, and
    "unordered" is the wrong word for it -- the ontology of the extension changes.
C · THE HETEROGENEITY IS ANNOTATOR NOISE AFTER ALL. The between component is small once the within
    component is removed properly, and the 5.31 ratio is an artifact of unequal annotator counts.

PREDICTION MATRIX
  A -> flips surviving BH and exceeding the sham: 0
  B -> >= 1, named, with its stratum and both levels' intervals
  C -> between-component share < 50% of total variance after the correction

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
KILL -- CONDITIONAL, gated on the controls
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
if POSITIVE fires (a SYNTHETIC partition planted into the difference vector is recovered by the
   stratum machinery) and g=0 fires (planting NO partition yields no flip) and PLACEBO fires (an arm
   against itself has zero variance in both components):
        flips surviving BH and > sham mean  -> WORLD B. Name the pair, stratum, and both intervals.
        0 such flips                        -> WORLD A. R768's "unordered set" stands as stated.
        between share < 50%                 -> WORLD C. The decomposition is the finding.
else:
        UNVERIFIED. Never OVERTURNED, never CONFIRMED.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONTROLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
POSITIVE  plant a partition: add +delta to `d(p)` on a random half and -delta on the other, with
          delta = 2x the pooled MDE, and require the stratum machinery to recover a flip on THAT
          synthetic stratum. BAND, COMPUTED: at delta = 0 no flip may be found (g=0 below); at
          delta = 2 MDE it must be. The threshold sits strictly between two computed ends.
g=0       delta = 0 -- the same machinery on an unplanted vector must find no flip.
NEGATIVE  permute the stratum labels across prompts, 200 draws, and report the flip count. World
          excluded: "any partition of 968 prompts into 4 levels produces flips".
SHAM      ingredient ABSENT, not inverted. Ingredient = THE STRATUM'S MEANING. A RANDOM partition
          into the same number of equally-sized levels, 200 draws -- same machinery, same cell count,
          no information. The real strata must beat its flip distribution.
PLACEBO   an arm against ITSELF: both variance components exactly 0, no flip at any stratum.
NOISE     the within-prompt component IS a measured noise floor; its distribution is printed, not
          summarised by its mean alone.
UNIT      instrument unit = an ANNOTATOR-LEVEL agreement; intermediate unit = a PROMPT; claim unit =
          an ARM PAIR WITHIN A STRATUM LEVEL. Three levels, all named, never collapsed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MULTIPLICITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10 pairs x 3 strata x 4 levels = 120 cells, BH at q = 0.05 over the WHOLE grid, cells tested beside
cells surviving, non-survivors printed. Plus 200 sham partitions and 200 label permutations.

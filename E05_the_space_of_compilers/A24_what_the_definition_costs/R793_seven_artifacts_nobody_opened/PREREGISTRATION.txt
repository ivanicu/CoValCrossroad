--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE ARITHMETIC, DERIVED BEFORE MEASURING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D1  THE `vs FULL` COLUMN CANNOT MOVE. `coval_full`'s class is deterministic per prompt, so an arm's
    agreement with it does not involve annotator sampling at all. **DERIVATION**, and it is this
    round's exact OBJECT check: recomputing must reproduce the shipped `vs FULL` figures to
    floating-point, and `full` vs FULL must be exactly 1.0.
D2  THE `vs HUMAN` COLUMN IS A2 IN THE LIMIT. Averaging over ALL annotators is exactly the arc's A2,
    so the upgrade connects this artifact to every number R787-R792 computed. **DERIVATION.**
D3  SAMPLING ONE ANNOTATOR OF ~16 INFLATES THE VARIANCE OF THE HUMAN COLUMN AND LEAVES ITS
    EXPECTATION UNCHANGED. So the POINT estimates should move little and the INTERVALS should
    narrow; a large move in the point estimate would indicate something other than sampling.
    **DERIVATION**, and it is the prediction that makes the upgrade falsifiable rather than free.
D4  DIVIDING BY A MEASURED CEILING IS NOT DISATTENUATION. The standard correction divides by
    sqrt(reliability), not by reliability. So `nh = mean_h / CEIL_H` and `nh = mean_h / sqrt(CEIL_H)`
    are two different defensible choices and they can disagree. **DERIVATION**, and it is why the
    normalisation is swept rather than picked.

STRONGEST CONFOUND, written BEFORE the run: `CEIL_H` is itself an ESTIMATE, and it appears in the
denominator of the quantity the verdict is read off. Its sampling noise therefore moves the verdict
-- the standard's *control validated by its own instrument's noise* row, in the numerator this time.
CONTROL IN THE SAME ITERATION: CEIL_H is bootstrapped and the verdict recomputed across its own
interval, so the share of CEIL_H draws that return each world is reported rather than one point.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONTROLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OBJECT      D1's exact reproduction of the deterministic column; the shipped JSON is the baseline
            and a mismatch is exit 2, never 0.
PLACEBO     an arm against its OWN class: exactly 1.0. A contrast where no effect can exist.
POSITIVE    BAND COMPUTED AT BOTH ENDS. Plant a per-prompt shift on the human column and sweep
            {0, 0.05, 0.10, 0.20, 0.30}; require the raw difference not to cross zero at delta = 0
            and to cross at the top. floor == ceiling is DEGENERATE and fails.
NEGATIVE    shuffle `coval_full`'s class across prompts. `vs HUMAN` must be EXACTLY unchanged (a
            derivation, checked) and `vs FULL` must collapse toward chance. World it excludes: "the
            vs-FULL column reflects something other than the prompt-matched rubric."
SHAM        the same comparison against a RANDOM arm's class instead of `full`'s -- the ingredient
            removed is the identity of the compressed object, not misdirected.
NOISE FLOOR MEASURED: CEIL_H's own bootstrap spread, and the annotator split-half spread of the
            human column.
UNIT        instrument unit = a (prompt, annotator) judgement for the human column and a PROMPT for
            the full column; claim unit = an ARM; E1's unit = an ARTIFACT. Three strings, unequal.

ESTIMAND -- two, and the first must be labelled correctly or the second is unreadable
   (1) DERIVATION. R724 swept five "defensible readings" of each clause. Given mde = ZEFF*SE and a
       percentile CI approximately eff +- 1.959964*SE, every rule is a threshold on the SAME
       t = eff/SE:
           point        eff > 0        <->  t > 0
           ci_only      lo > 0         <->  t > 1.959964
           mde_only     eff >= mde     <->  t >= 2.801585
           strict       lo>0 & eff>=mde<->  t >= 2.801585        (identical to mde_only when eff>0)
           conservative lo > mde       <->  t > 4.761549
       So the five labels carry FOUR distinct thresholds, and R724's 5x5 grid has at most 4x4
       distinct rule pairs. THIS IS ALGEBRA AND IS NOT EVIDENCE. What is measurable is whether the
       equivalence HOLDS on the artifact, because the bootstrap SE and the analytic SE are two
       different estimates and the percentile CI is not exactly symmetric.
   (2) MEASUREMENT. R724's qualification -- that the conservative reading excludes coval_core -- was
       computed treating every mde as exact. mde is estimated from the same sample as the effect.
       Is the exclusion of coval_core flippable by the mde's OWN sampling error?

IDENTIFICATION
   (1) identified exactly from the artifact: t can be recovered two ways, from the CI half-width and
       from mde/ZEFF, and their ratio is itself the diagnostic for whether the equivalence holds.
   (2) PARTIALLY identified. The sampling distribution of a sample SD is chi-distributed UNDER
       NORMALITY of the per-prompt differences, which this round cannot check because the raw
       difference vectors are not in the artifact. So the crossing probability is MODEL-BASED and
       is reported with its assumption attached, alongside an assumption-light distance in units of
       the mde's own sampling SD.

⛔ THE ARITHMETIC, DERIVED BEFORE MEASURING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D1  BELOW RESOLUTION REQUIRES |eff| < MDE AND A CI EXCLUDING ZERO. Those are different questions --
    `report.py`'s own docstring says so. So an arm can be "significantly better" and "inside its own
    resolution" simultaneously, and any two-valued reading MUST choose one. Which one it chose is the
    finding; that a choice exists is ALGEBRA.
D2  RAISING B NARROWS THE CI AND LEAVES THE MDE UNTOUCHED -- the MDE is `z * sd/sqrt(n)`, with no
    bootstrap in it. So more resamples can only move an arm from UNRESOLVED toward BELOW RESOLUTION
    or BEATS, never rescue it from BELOW RESOLUTION. **A B-sweep cannot answer this question**, which
    is why R728's sweep found nothing and is not evidence here.

STRONGEST CONFOUND, written BEFORE the run: `topw_k8` might fail for a reason particular to k=8 --
more criteria, noisier sums -- rather than because the effect is genuinely inside the floor. CONTROL
IN THE SAME ITERATION: report the effect, MDE and their RATIO for the whole `topw_k{1,2,3,4,6,8,12}`
family, so a k-trend is visible instead of assumed absent.

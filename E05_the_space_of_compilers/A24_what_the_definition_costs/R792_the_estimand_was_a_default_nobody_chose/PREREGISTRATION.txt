--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE ARITHMETIC, DERIVED BEFORE MEASURING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D1  WEIGHTING AND RESAMPLING ARE ORTHOGONAL. The weighting fixes the ESTIMAND (which population
    average is being estimated); the resampling unit fixes the STANDARD ERROR of that estimate.
    Changing both at once confounds a change of target with a change of precision. **DERIVATION**,
    and it is the whole design.
D2  SUBGROUP-WEIGHTING IS A REWEIGHTING OF THE SAME JUDGEMENTS. Every judgement appears in all six
    axes, so the 36 subgroups are six overlapping partitions of one set of ~15,600 judgements, not
    36 independent units. **DERIVATION** — the prior round found this the hard way and corrected to
    annotator resampling; it is registered here so the corrected unit is not silently un-corrected.
D3  ALIAS ARMS MUST RETURN EXACTLY 0 IN EVERY SUBGROUP AND EVERY CELL OF THE 2x2, since their
    per-judgement agreement vectors are identical. **DERIVATION**, and it is the placebo.
D4  IF THE TWO WEIGHTINGS CORRELATE ABOVE ~0.99 ACROSS THE 190 PAIRS, the subgroup weighting is a
    reparameterisation of the pooled one and cannot flip anything except at the margin.
    **DERIVATION-adjacent**: the correlation is a measurement, its implication is algebra.

STRONGEST CONFOUND, written BEFORE the run: 36 subgroups is a multiplicity surface, and the prior
round tested ONE pair. A subgroup-weighted mean that separates one pair may separate no more than
chance across the grid. CONTROL IN THE SAME ITERATION: the subgroup estimand is run over ALL 190
pairs with BH over the whole grid, and the decisive pair's survival is reported inside that
correction, not on its own.

R696 · PRE-REGISTRATION — written before any percentile is computed.

⚠ WHY NOT R695's NEXT AS WRITTEN. It proposed "which arm of a PAIR scores higher" — and R695 itself
  measured the assemblable-pair population at ONE. Third closing line in a row proposing a comparison
  whose population the same round had just measured as ~1 (R685's derivation, R694's missing data,
  this). Reframed over ARMS rather than pairs it is runnable.

⚠ AND NOT OVER A UNION. The 12-arm coverage is a UNION of four artifacts. Comparing a2 values across
  artifacts assumes they are the same quantity from the same run — which is exactly what this round
  would be testing. Three sources are INDEPENDENTLY runnable (>=2 admitted and >=2 rejected each),
  so this is a SPECIFICATION CURVE across sources, not a pooled sample.

ESTIMAND    within each source separately, do the ②-admitted arms rank ABOVE the ②-rejected ones by
            that source's own a2 values? Statistic: the admitted arms' mean rank, against the EXACT
            null over all C(n, n_admitted) assignments.
POINT A     mean percentile across the three sources = 85.
INTERVAL A  [40, 100]  ⚠ below 40 means the scores and the verdicts disagree, which is live and
            would be the more consequential outcome.
POINT B     1 of 3 sources resolves at p < 0.05.
INTERVAL B  [0, 3]
DIRECTIONAL (separately failable) all three sources agree in SIGN — every percentile above 50.
KILL        if the three DISAGREE IN SIGN, no pooled claim is admissible and the framing is the
            finding (§2.5's reading rule), not the average.
CONTROLS, per source
  POSITIVE  labelling the top-scoring arms as admitted must give percentile 100.
  g=0       a randomly chosen assignment must average near the 50th percentile.
  NEGATIVE  labelling the bottom-scoring arms as admitted must give percentile ~0.
  PLACEBO   run twice identical.
  RESOLUTION FLOOR  the minimum achievable p is 1/C(n, n_admitted); reported per source, because a
            p that cannot go below 0.05 makes "not resolved" meaningless.
IDENTIFICATION LIMIT  each source's a2 is that round's a2. Agreement across three sources is
            convergence across three artifacts, NOT independent replication — they may share a
            scoring run upstream, which this corpus does not record.

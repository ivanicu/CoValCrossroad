R807 · IS THE FITTED ARM A RANK-CONSTRAINED COPY OF THE LEAK?
written before the run · NBOOT 1200 · ZEFF 2.801585

PROVOCATION
R806 left the fitted arms at roughly HALF the maximal-leak agreement profile (+0.0189 excess against
a +0.0350 ceiling) -- neither "content" nor "pure label access". Its NEXT: regress each fitted arm's
per-prompt margin on the synthetic `_perfect_leak`'s and read what is left over.

THE ARITHMETIC, DERIVED BEFORE MEASURING
`oracle_k4_fit1` selects the 4-subset of the rubric that best matches parity-1's modal class m1(p).
`_perfect_leak` PREDICTS m1(p) exactly. So if the rubric could always reproduce m1(p), the two
margins would be IDENTICAL: slope 1, intercept 0. The deviation is exactly "how often the rubric's
4-subsets cannot express the modal class" -- and the sign of what is left over is NOT forced,
because a rank-4 constrained arm can be a SMOOTHER estimator of the population than the raw modal
class of 8 annotators.

⛔ AND THE ESTIMAND IS THE INTERCEPT, NOT THE RESIDUAL. R804 already committed the lesson: an OLS
residual MEAN is 0 by construction. The informative quantity is the arm's margin AT ZERO LEAK
MARGIN -- i.e. on prompts where the modal class does no better than the size-matched blind pool.

ESTIMAND
E1  slope, intercept and R^2 of arm-margin on leak-margin, SAME parity-0 draw (R806's NEXT as posed).
E2  ⭐ the SHARED-DRAW FIX: `_perfect_leak` scored on parity-0 half A, the arm on half B. Both
    margins then carry INDEPENDENT annotator noise. The E1-minus-E2 slope difference IS the
    shared-term inflation, and it is reported as a number rather than argued away.
E3  the HONEST FLOOR: `coval_core` and `topw_k4` -- which never saw a parity-1 label -- regressed
    identically. Their slope is what the shared prompt-difficulty term buys with no leak at all.
E4  content = fitted intercept minus honest intercept, with a paired CI.

IDENTIFICATION
E1 confounded by construction (shared parity-0 draw) and reported only as the contrast for E2.
E2/E3/E4 identified. ⚠ Splitting parity-0 halves the annotators behind every margin, so E2 is
NOISIER than E1; the noise floor below measures how much.

DERIVED BEFORE MEASURING
D1 [DERIVATION] `_perfect_leak` regressed on ITSELF gives slope exactly 1 and intercept exactly 0.
   PLACEBO.
D2 [DERIVATION] an arm that is m1's class on half the prompts and an honest arm's class on the
   other half must give slope ~0.5 with a resolved positive intercept. POSITIVE, with a known
   magnitude, and it must NOT pass for the pure-leak arm (intercept ~0) -- that is its g=0 check.
D3 the honest arms have never seen parity-1, so any slope they show is shared prompt difficulty.
D4 E1's slope >= E2's slope, or the shared-draw story is wrong and the round says so.

WORLDS
A  CONTENT: fitted intercept resolvedly ABOVE the honest arms' -> the fitted route agrees with
   parity-0 for reasons the modal-class copy does not capture, and clause (3) rewritten as "held
   out from the PROMPT" would exclude a route with real content.
B  COPY: slope ~1 after the fix and fitted intercept not above honest -> the fitted arms ARE the
   leak, and clause (3) must be rewritten to exclude them.
C  UNINFORMATIVE: honest arms show the same slope as fitted -> the slope is shared difficulty and
   this design cannot separate anything.
CHECKED FIRST: C, because it kills the round.

fitted intercept          > honest, resolved  CI holds honest    any
honest slope              << fitted           << fitted          ~ fitted

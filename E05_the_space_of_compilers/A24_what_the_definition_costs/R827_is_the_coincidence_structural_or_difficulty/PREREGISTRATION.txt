R827 · IS THE CORE-ON-CEILING COINCIDENCE STRUCTURAL, OR IS IT ITEM DIFFICULTY?

CHECK #429. R826's NEXT proposed regressing `coval_core`'s per-prompt scores on the saturated
response-only predictor's, to ask whether their coincidence at the ceiling is structural.
⛔ THE ARITHMETIC RUNG KILLS THE HEADLINE NUMBER. Every arm's per-prompt score is an ACCURACY
AGAINST THE SAME human labels on the SAME items. A prompt where humans tie caps every arm; one
where they agree is easy for every arm. So any two arms correlate through ITEM DIFFICULTY whether
or not they share a mechanism. MEASURED, on pairs with no shared mechanism at all:
    random_k4_s0 x oracle_k4   r = +0.5132
    gen_sham     x oracle_k4   r = +0.3873
    generic      x random_k4_s0 r = +0.4987
A raw r in that band would have been reported as evidence of shared structure and would have been
item difficulty. This is the FIFTH NEXT line in five rounds to fail this rung.

ESTIMAND
  E1  the PARTIAL correlation of `coval_core` and the saturated response-only bar, per prompt,
      controlling for a difficulty index built from arms EXCLUDING BOTH
  E2  ⭐ that partial r against a NULL of accuracy-matched, mechanism-unrelated arm pairs -- the
      only comparison that can distinguish structure from difficulty
  E3  the difficulty index's own components: human tie rate, annotator agreement, mean arm A2,
      reported separately so the control is inspectable rather than a black box
  E4  the decision: is the core a response-only rule in disguise, or two quantities that meet

IDENTIFICATION  partial correlation is identified here because the difficulty index is built from
DISJOINT arms; if it were built from either arm under test, the residual would be contaminated by
that arm's own noise appearing on both sides -- the failure mode §4 calls "a control validated by
its own instrument's noise".

DERIVED FIRST -- BEFORE ANY COMPUTE
  D1  ⛔ A RAW PER-PROMPT CORRELATION IS FORCED and is not evidence. Numbers above, measured.
  D2  ⛔ THE PARTIAL r IS NOT FORCED: residualising on disjoint arms removes the shared-difficulty
      channel, and what remains can be zero. That is why E1 is a partial and not a raw r.
  D3  A high partial r is NOT proof of mechanism either -- it shows the two agree on WHICH prompts
      they win beyond difficulty. That is consistent with shared mechanism AND with a third common
      cause the index does not capture. The claim this round can support is about PROFILE
      AGREEMENT, never about mechanism, and must be worded that way.
  D4  The saturated bar is refit per split, so its per-prompt vector is an AVERAGE over splits and
      carries less noise than a single arm's. Attenuation therefore differs between the arms under
      test and the null pairs; the null must be built from split-averaged vectors too, or the
      comparison is between differently-attenuated quantities.

WORLDS
  A  STRUCTURAL -- partial r sits above the null band. The core and the response-only ceiling agree
     on which prompts they win, beyond difficulty: the core behaves like a response-only rule.
  B  NUMERICAL -- partial r sits inside the null band. They meet in LEVEL only; the coincidence at
     the ceiling says nothing about how the core works.
  C  DEGENERATE -- the difficulty index absorbs so much variance that the residuals carry no signal
     for any pair, null included. The statistic is unusable and no verdict is admissible.

PREDICTION MATRIX
  A -> partial r(core, bar) > null 95th percentile
  B -> partial r(core, bar) inside the null's central 95%
  C -> null pairs' residual sd collapses; every partial r near 0 including the positive control

CONTROLS
  OBJECT     the raw correlations above must reproduce to <1e-6; they are the round's premise.
  PLACEBO    an arm against a PERMUTATION of another arm's residuals -> partial r ~ 0.
  POSITIVE   pairs KNOWN to share mechanism -- `topw_k4` x `topw_k6`, `random_k4_s0` x
             `random_k4_s1` -- must show partial r ABOVE the unrelated-pair null. If they do not,
             the statistic cannot see shared mechanism and E1 is inadmissible whatever it returns.
  NEGATIVE   the difficulty index recomputed from a DIFFERENT disjoint arm subset; the partial r
             must not move by more than its own bootstrap width.
  SHAM       residualise on a difficulty index built from RANDOM vectors of matched dimension --
             it should remove nothing, so the partial r should return to the raw r.
  NOISE FLOOR  bootstrap over prompts, 2000 draws.

MULTIPLICITY   all arm pairs formed from the scoreable set enter the null; the reported pair is one
               of them. BH q=0.05 over the pairs tested; non-survivors printed.

PRE-REGISTERED KILL -- conditional, gated on the controls
  if placebo ~ 0 and the positive-control pairs exceed the null band and the sham restores the raw r:
      if partial_r(core, bar) > null_p95:                    WORLD A
      elif partial_r(core, bar) within null central 95%:      WORLD B
      else:                                                   WORLD C
  else:
      UNVERIFIED   -- never OVERTURNED, never CONFIRMED

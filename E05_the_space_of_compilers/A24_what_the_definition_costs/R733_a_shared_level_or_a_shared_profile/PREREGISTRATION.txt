   R457 (W-STRUCTURED) established the per-prompt gap is RELIABLE: split-half rho_full 0.8311 for the

   R284 already correlated per-prompt margins ACROSS arms and, crucially, MEASURED THE FLOOR: arms

   ⛔ SO A RAW CORRELATION HERE WOULD BE UNINTERPRETABLE. Every clause-① margin subtracts the same

   NOT covered by either: the pair R731 left standing -- the greedy and independent objects against

ESTIMAND
   Do the per-prompt margin profiles of the two admitted target-reading objects share structure with
   the EXCLUDED object beyond the shared-subtrahend floor, and more than they do with the
   label-blind arms?
   A shared LEVEL with an unshared PROFILE would mean the clause excludes a size rather than a
   mechanism, which is a different defect from the one now on the page.

IDENTIFICATION
   ⚠ PARTIAL and stated three ways.
   (1) The shared subtrahend inflates every r; only the EXCESS over R284's floor is interpretable.
   (2) Reliability caps the attainable r: with split-half reliabilities near 0.83, the ceiling is
       sqrt(rel_a * rel_b) ~ 0.83, so a raw r of 0.83 is a PERFECT correlation at this resolution and
       must not be read as 17% unexplained. Disattenuated values are reported beside raw ones.
   (3) Correlation of outcomes does not identify a shared MECHANISM. It can only exclude the world
       where the profiles are unrelated. This is stated as an exclusion, never as an attribution.

SCOPE
   population : 968 prompts, per-arm own population, from R728's cached difference vectors
   instrument : Pearson and Spearman on per-prompt margin vectors
   baseline   : R284's shared-subtrahend floor among the random_k4_s* family
   regime     : default emitter only -- the foreign-emitter object is EXCLUDED after R732

WORLDS
   W-MECHANISM  the admitted objects' profiles track the excluded object's well above floor and
                above their tracking of the blind arms. The clause is excluding something profile-
                shaped, and the two admitted objects are the same kind of thing as what it excludes.
   W-LEVEL      their excess over floor is no larger toward the excluded object than toward the
                blind arms. Then they merely LAND at the same level, and the clause excludes a size.
   prediction matrix
      excess to oracle >> excess to topw   -> MECHANISM 0.90  LEVEL 0.05
      excesses comparable                  -> MECHANISM 0.05  LEVEL 0.90
      excess to oracle NEGATIVE            -> both wrong; report as the finding

    family   k-set                    with M (k = {4})       relative correlation

WHAT THE ORDERING OF THE 15 PAIRS ACTUALLY TRACKS. The top six are precisely the six pairs among

AND R776's MECHANISM WAS RIGHT IN SPIRIT AND WRONG IN STATISTIC. Two random draws from the pool differ
on a prompt where the pool's criteria INDUCE DIFFERENT ORDERINGS of the four responses -- not where
their satisfaction VALUES are spread. Two criteria can have very different levels and identical
orderings (contributing nothing to |d|), or near-identical levels and opposite orderings
(contributing a lot). **`poolspread` measured the wrong pool quantity**, and that is why it returned a
clean null while the co-movement it was meant to explain is 0.70.

ESTIMAND (before method)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E1  `orderdisagree(p)` = the mean pairwise disagreement of `sign(Y_i - Y_j)` across the 16 pool
    criteria, over the 6 response pairs. Arm-free, selection-free, difference-free -- and it is the
    quantity a random draw's variance actually depends on.
E2  its correlation with each of the six families' scales, and whether the RANDOM-containing families
    (Ra, Rb, Rc, M) separate from the others (F1, F3).
E3  whether conditioning on `orderdisagree` removes the M x R excess, i.e. the partial correlation
    between two random-containing families' scales holding it fixed.

E2, E3 exact given E1.
⚠ AND THE SAME BOUND R776 DECLARED STILL APPLIES: `orderdisagree` reads the same satisfaction table

D2  `orderdisagree` AND `poolspread` ARE DIFFERENT QUANTITIES AND MAY BE UNCORRELATED. If they turn
    out to be strongly correlated then this round is R776 re-run with a relabelled axis, and it must
    say so. **Their mutual correlation is therefore reported FIRST, before any scale correlation.**
D3  A RANDOM DRAW'S EXPECTED |d| RISES WITH ORDERING DISAGREEMENT BY CONSTRUCTION. If the 16 criteria

    correlation for the random families is partly forced -- **the measurement is its SIZE, and whether
    the non-random families show it too.** A finding requires the random families to exceed the
    others, not merely to be positive.

STRONGEST CONFOUND, written BEFORE the run: `orderdisagree` may simply track how many response pairs
are near-ties in the pool, which is a saturation/degeneracy property rather than genuine disagreement.
CONTROL IN THE SAME ITERATION: report the per-prompt share of pool sign values that are exactly 0

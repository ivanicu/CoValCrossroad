# R693 · is clause ② a fact about cores, or about our generator?

**⭐⭐⭐ The generator's rule family predicts ②'s verdict at **88.1%** against a **78.6%** majority
floor — 98.4th percentile of its own null, unanimous in **11 of 13** families. **But the 11.9% it
cannot explain contains the released core against its own sham**, which is the one comparison the
definition exists to make. **② is 88% recoverable from a name we chose, and the part that is not is
where it does its work.****

## THE GAP THIS ATTACKS
R689 established the release ships **one** core. So **41 of 42** scored arms are our constructions,
and ②'s entire discriminating power runs over **our** generator's parameter space.

## THE TEST (G3 — all 42 arms, none held out, none hidden)

| | |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **majority-class floor** (always REJECT) | **78.6%** — computed, not assumed |
| ⭐ **rule family** predicts ② | **88.1%** (+9.5) · **98.4th percentile** of a 1000-shuffle null |
| **k alone** predicts ② | **78.6% — exactly the floor, +0.0** |
| ② unanimous within a family | **11 of 13** |

Registered **A 90% [79,100] → 88.1%, INSIDE (−1.9)** · **B 79% [70,88] → 78.6%, INSIDE (−0.4)** ·
**directional (family beats k) HOLDS.** ⚠ The whole of interval A lies **above** the floor, so a
result at or below 78.6% falsifies it — the failability ledger 803/808 records me missing twice.

**Controls:** POSITIVE — the ② verdict as its own predictor → **100%**, *the fitter can reach it*.
**g=0** — a permuted label → at the floor. NEGATIVE — shuffled families → at the floor. PLACEBO —
identical.

## ⭐⭐ THE RESIDUAL IS THE FINDING, AND IT IS COMPUTED, NOT TYPED
Where family does **not** determine ②:

| family | split |
|---|---|
| `coval_core` | **1/2** — `coval_core` passes, **`coval_core_sham` fails** |
| `topw` | **4/8** — `topw_k3 k4 k6 k8` pass; `topw_k1 k12 k2` and **`topw_k4_sham`** fail |

**② separates 2 of 5 sham pairs**, including **the released core from its own sham.** A
"reparameterisation" verdict is only honest if the residual is examined — **and the residual is
exactly the comparison that matters.**

## ⚠ THREE LIMITS, NONE OF THEM COSMETIC
- **Prediction is not measurement.** Family and ② could both depend on a third thing — most
  obviously **whether the rule reads labels**. This round shows the boundary is *recoverable from
  the name*; it does not show ② *measures* the generator.
- **Instrument unit: a NAME PREFIX. Claim unit: THE GENERATOR.** Not equal — a prefix is our label
  for a generator, not the generator.
- **A fit scored on its own data is a CEILING.** 88.1% is an upper bound on family's predictive
  power, not an estimate of it.

## IMPOSSIBLE HERE
A **second released core** would separate *"property of cores"* from *"property of our generators"*
directly. The release ships one — which is why this round measures recoverability instead.

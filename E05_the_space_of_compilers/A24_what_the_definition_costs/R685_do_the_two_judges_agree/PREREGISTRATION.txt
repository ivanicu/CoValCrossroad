⚠ THE DESIGN DECISION, NAMED BEFORE THE RUN
  A per-judge dict of CONTINUOUS values (means, ranks) differs at every judge by construction, so
  counting numeric difference as "disagreement" would return 100% and measure nothing. Only
  VERDICT-LIKE values count: booleans, and strings from a small closed set. A round exposing no such
  field is EXCLUDED, never scored as agreeing.

POINT A     3 of the 7 rounds DISAGREE across judges on at least one verdict field.
INTERVAL A  [1, 6]
POINT B     40% of per-judge verdict PAIRS disagree.
INTERVAL B  [15%, 70%]
DIRECTIONAL (separately failable) disagreement is at least as common as agreement across pairs —
            i.e. the split R683 saw is typical rather than exceptional.
KILL        if fewer than 4 of the 7 expose a verdict-like per-judge field, the population cannot
            carry a share and only counts are reported.
CONTROLS
  POSITIVE  R361 is KNOWN to split (rank_resolved: 2B true, 0.8B false) and must classify DISAGREE.
  g=0       a synthetic artifact with identical values at both judges must classify AGREE — the
            classifier must be able to return both.
  NEGATIVE  an artifact with a single judge must be EXCLUDED, not scored AGREE.
  PLACEBO   run twice identical.
IDENTIFICATION LIMIT  agreement between two judges is not agreement in general; two is the number
            this release ships. A disagreement rate over 2 judges bounds nothing about a third.

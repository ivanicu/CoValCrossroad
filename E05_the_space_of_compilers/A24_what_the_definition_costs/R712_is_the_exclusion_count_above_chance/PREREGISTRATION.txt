⭐ THE STRUCTURE, DERIVED BEFORE THE RUN, AND IT IS THE SAME ONE R711 FOUND.
  A clause's UNIQUE exclusions are the arms the OTHER TWO clauses admit and it does not. So the
  ceiling is |F1 ∩ F3| = 23, and F2's 20 is 20 OF 23 POSSIBLE, not 20 of 42. Under a uniform random
  admission of 9 of 42 the expected count is 23 × (1 − 9/42) = 18.0714. ⛔ So "20 unique exclusions,
  the largest of the three clauses" may be admission-size arithmetic, exactly as the sham residual
  was, and that must be tested before F2's last remaining leg is relied on.

ESTIMAND
  For each clause, the number of arms it UNIQUELY excludes, against the EXACT distribution of that
  count under a uniformly random admission of the SAME SIZE, holding the other two clauses fixed —
  with the exact P(count >= observed) enumerated hypergeometrically, not sampled.

IDENTIFICATION
  Exactly computable: with the other two clauses fixed, the count is |others| minus a hypergeometric
  draw. No sampling error.
  ⚠ The OBSERVED counts are DERIVATIONS from R360's committed verdicts. The NULLS are the evidence.
  ⚠ Holding the other two fixed while randomising one is a choice: it asks "is THIS clause's count
    surprising given the others", not "is the triple surprising". Named, not assumed.

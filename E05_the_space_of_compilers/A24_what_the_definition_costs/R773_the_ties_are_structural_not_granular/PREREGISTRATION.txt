R773 · the 223 ties — a property of the arms, or the coarseness of a sign statistic?
WRITTEN BEFORE ANY CODE FOR THIS ROUND. 2026-08-05.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THIS ROUND EXISTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
R772 found 223 of 968 prompts with `c(p) = 0` -- the five committed extension members score
IDENTICALLY on them -- and registered a recomputation of the paired MDE and required-n on the
discriminating subset, reported as a different estimand.

⛔ CHECK #375: THE RECOMPUTATION IS ANSWERABLE BY ALGEBRA, AND WAS RUN BEFORE DESIGNING.
   Dropping prompts whose difference is exactly 0, when the mean is small relative to the spread,
   scales the EFFECT by `n_full/n_disc` and the MDE by `sd_ratio * sqrt(n_full/n_disc)`, and those
   are the same factor to O(mu^2/sigma^2). **So `eff/MDE` is INVARIANT and no verdict can move.**
   Measured across all ten committed pairs: ratios **0.9998 to 1.0008**, verdict changes **0 of 10**.
   And `n_required` scales by exactly `745/968 = 0.7696` -- the same information expressed in a
   different unit, not less data needed.
   ⇒ R772's registered quantity is a DERIVATION. It is reported as one, and it is not this round.

⭐ WHAT IS OPEN IS WHAT THE 223 ARE. `A2` is built from `sign(Y_i - Y_j)` over the 6 pairs among 4
   responses, so two arms score identically on a prompt IFF their criterion sets induce THE SAME SIGN
   PATTERN on those 4 responses. Two readings, and they imply opposite things about the definition:
     STRUCTURAL -- the arms genuinely rank those responses identically. Then the extension is
                   unordered partly because its members DO THE SAME THING on 23% of prompts.
     GRANULAR   -- the sign statistic discards magnitude, and the arms differ there in a way A2
                   cannot see. Then the coarseness is a CHOICE that costs resolution, and a
                   magnitude-sensitive statistic would separate them.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ESTIMAND (before method)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E1  the invariance, verified rather than assumed: `eff/MDE` on the full 968 against the
    discriminating 745, per committed pair, with the ratio and the verdict change count.
E2  on the 223 tied prompts, whether the five arms' SIGN VECTORS are identical (structural) and
    whether their underlying satisfaction VECTORS differ (granular). Both counted, not one.
E3  a magnitude-sensitive estimator on the same prompts -- the per-prompt correlation between an
    arm's satisfaction ordering and the human's -- and whether it separates arms A2 ties.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IDENTIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E1 exact.
E2 exact -- sign vectors and satisfaction vectors are both computable per prompt per arm.
E3 IDENTIFIED but it is a DIFFERENT ESTIMAND, and that is stated rather than smuggled: a
   magnitude-sensitive statistic answers "does the arm's satisfaction ORDER the responses like the
   human" whereas A2 answers "does its sign pattern MATCH the human's". A finer statistic separating
   more arms is NOT evidence that A2 is wrong -- only that the two questions differ. The round
   reports the separation and refuses the promotion.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
population  968 prompts, of which 745 discriminate and 223 tie; the 5 committed members
instrument  A2 (sign-based) and a magnitude-sensitive alternative, both stated
baseline    none -- arm vs arm as R768/R772
regime      first release, home judge, this tree_sha

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
⛔ THE ARITHMETIC, DERIVED BEFORE MEASURING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D1  `eff/MDE` IS INVARIANT TO DROPPING EXACT ZEROS when mu << sigma. Effect scales by `n_f/n_d`,
    sd by `sqrt((n_f-1)/(n_d-1))`, MDE by `sd_ratio * sqrt(n_f/n_d)`, and the product equals the
    effect's factor. Verified at 0.9998-1.0008. **This is the whole of R772's registered question.**
D2  A TIE IN A2 IS A TIE IN THE SIGN PATTERN, not in the criteria. Two different criterion sets can
    induce the same ordering of four responses; nothing about A2 requires the arms to be similar.
    **So "the arms are identical there" does NOT follow from a tie and must be measured separately.**
D3  A FINER STATISTIC WILL SEPARATE MORE PAIRS ON ANY DATA. Strictly more information is retained, so
    "the magnitude statistic separates arms A2 ties" is nearly forced and is NOT the finding. The
    measurement is whether that separation AGREES WITH THE HUMAN better or is merely non-zero.

STRONGEST CONFOUND, written BEFORE the run: a magnitude-sensitive statistic has a different noise
floor, so more separation may be more noise. CONTROL IN THE SAME ITERATION: compute the new
statistic's own MDE on the same prompts and report separation RELATIVE to it, never as a raw count of
non-ties.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WORLDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A · STRUCTURAL. On the 223, the arms' sign vectors are identical AND their satisfaction vectors are
    near-identical. The five members genuinely do the same thing on 23% of prompts, and the
    extension's unorderedness is partly a fact about the arms rather than about power.
B · GRANULAR. Sign vectors identical but satisfaction vectors differ materially. A2's coarseness
    hides real differences, and the estimator is a resolution choice the page has never stated.
C · MIXED, AND THE SPLIT IS THE FINDING. Some tied prompts are structural and some granular, in
    which case the 223 is not one population and no single reading is admissible.

PREDICTION MATRIX
  A -> mean cosine distance between arms' satisfaction vectors on the 223 < 0.05
  B -> >= 0.20
  C -> a bimodal distribution of that distance, with both modes populated

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
KILL -- CONDITIONAL, gated on the controls
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
if POSITIVE fires (two arms known to differ -- `coval_core` vs `gen_sham` -- show a large satisfaction
   distance on the same prompts) and g=0 fires (an arm against ITSELF shows distance exactly 0)
   and PLACEBO fires (`topw_k4` vs `_detA` shows distance exactly 0):
        mean distance < 0.05                  -> WORLD A. The ties are structural; the page says so.
        mean distance >= 0.20                 -> WORLD B. A2's coarseness is a stated resolution choice.
        bimodal with both modes populated     -> WORLD C. The 223 is not one population.
else:
        UNVERIFIED. Never OVERTURNED, never CONFIRMED.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONTROLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
POSITIVE  `coval_core` vs `gen_sham` on the same 223 prompts must show a LARGE satisfaction distance.
          BAND, COMPUTED: floor = an arm against itself (exactly 0, g=0 below); ceiling = the maximum
          distance the metric admits on this data, computed rather than assumed. The threshold sits
          strictly inside.
g=0       an arm against ITSELF: distance exactly 0 on every prompt.
NEGATIVE  permute which prompts are called "tied", 200 draws, and report the distance distribution.
          World excluded: "the 223 are unremarkable -- any 223 prompts look like this".
SHAM      ingredient ABSENT, not inverted. Ingredient = THE TIE. Compute the same distance on the 745
          DISCRIMINATING prompts. If tied and discriminating prompts have the same satisfaction
          distance, then the tie is not about the arms' similarity at all.
PLACEBO   `topw_k4` vs `_detA` -- one object under R730 -- distance exactly 0.
NOISE     the magnitude statistic's own MDE is computed on the same prompts and every separation is
          reported relative to it (the registered confound).
UNIT      instrument unit = a PROMPT; claim unit = an ARM PAIR ON A PROMPT SUBSET. The 223 and the
          745 are different populations and are never pooled.

R771 · is "which prompts separate cores" a question with an answer?
WRITTEN BEFORE ANY CODE FOR THIS ROUND. 2026-08-05.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THIS ROUND EXISTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
R770 established that ~85% of the per-pair difference variance is BETWEEN PROMPTS and that it sorts by
none of the strata the release ships. Its NEXT registered the correlation matrix of the ten difference
vectors and its leading eigenvalue share, to decide whether the heterogeneity is a single latent
PROMPT factor (some prompts separate cores, some do not) or PAIR-SPECIFIC noise.

⛔ CHECK #373, THE ARITHMETIC, RUN BEFORE ANY DESIGN -- AND IT REPAIRS THE REGISTERED QUANTITY:
 ① THE RANK IS FORCED. The ten vectors are all pairwise differences among FIVE arms, so they span the
   4-dimensional space of centred arm vectors. Measured: rank **4**, eigenvalues
   **[3.791, 2.957, 1.771, 1.481, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0]**. **So "there are only 4 non-zero components"
   is ALGEBRA, and a leading share compared against 1/10 would be a manufactured finding.** The
   uniform reference is **1/4 = 0.25**, not 1/10, and the observed leading share is **0.3791**.
 ② MY FIRST ANALYTIC NULL WAS WRONG AND IS REPLACED HERE, BEFORE THE RUN. I predicted the
   arm-sharing correlation as `v_a / sqrt(var(d_ab)*var(d_ac))` using the RAW arm variances, and it
   returned **+0.9271** against an observed **+0.2687**. The raw variance is dominated by PROMPT
   DIFFICULTY, which the difference removes; the null needs the RESIDUAL variances.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ESTIMAND (before method)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E1  the residual (arm-specific) variances `v_a`, identified from the ten observed `var(d_ab)` under
    the independence model `var(d_ab) = v_a + v_b` -- 5 unknowns, 10 equations, least squares, with
    the residual of that fit reported as its own diagnostic.
E2  the predicted correlation matrix under independence, `cov(d_ab, d_ac) = v_a` and `0` for disjoint
    pairs, against the OBSERVED matrix. The estimand is the EXCESS: observed minus predicted, for
    arm-sharing and disjoint pairs separately.
E3  the leading eigenvalue share of the observed correlation matrix against the share the SAME
    machinery returns on data simulated under the fitted independence model -- so the reference is
    generated, not asserted.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IDENTIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E1 IDENTIFIED: 10 equations, 5 unknowns, and the system is over-determined, so the fit's residual is
   itself a test of the independence model's ADEQUACY, reported before anything is built on it.
   ⚠ If any fitted `v_a` is NEGATIVE the model is refuted at that arm and the round says so rather
   than clipping to zero -- clipping would hide exactly the failure the fit exists to detect.
E2, E3 IDENTIFIED given E1.
⚠ WHAT IS NOT IDENTIFIED: whether a common factor, if found, is a property of the PROMPTS or of the
  JUDGE scoring them. That needs a second judge and is in the register.

SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
population  968 prompts; the 5 committed extension members; their 10 pairwise differences
instrument  per-prompt A2 over all annotators; Pearson correlation across prompts
baseline    the fitted independence model -- the null is GENERATED, not a number I chose
regime      first release, home judge, this tree_sha

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
⛔ THE ARITHMETIC, DERIVED BEFORE MEASURING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D1  RANK 4 IS FORCED. 5 arms, differences remove one degree of freedom. Any statement of the form
    "6 of 10 components are zero" is algebra and is reported as such.
D2  ARM-SHARING PAIRS ARE CORRELATED EVEN UNDER PURE INDEPENDENCE. With equal residual variances,
    `corr(d_ab, d_ac) = v_a / (v_a + v_b) = 0.5`. **So a positive mean correlation among arm-sharing
    pairs is NOT evidence of a common prompt factor** -- it is what sharing an arm does. The
    measurement is the EXCESS over the fitted prediction, in BOTH directions.
D3  THE SIGN OF THE EXCESS IS THE FORK, NOT ITS SIZE. Excess ABOVE the independence prediction means
    a common prompt factor (some prompts separate all arms). Excess BELOW means the arm residuals are
    NEGATIVELY correlated -- prompts where one arm gains are prompts where another loses -- which is a
    different world and points to a zero-sum structure, not to noise.
    ⚠ The quick pre-round probe put observed arm-sharing at **+0.2687** and disjoint at **+0.0312**;
      if the fitted prediction lands near 0.5, the excess is NEGATIVE and D3's second branch fires.
      Registered here so that outcome cannot be re-narrated afterwards.

STRONGEST CONFOUND, written BEFORE the run: unequal residual variances alone can push arm-sharing
correlations well below 0.5 without any negative dependence -- `v_a/(v_a+v_b)` is small whenever
`v_a << v_b`. CONTROL IN THE SAME ITERATION: the prediction is computed PER PAIR from the fitted
`v_a`, never from a 0.5 constant, so unequal variances are inside the null rather than confused with
the finding.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WORLDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A · ONE LATENT PROMPT FACTOR. Observed arm-sharing correlations exceed the fitted prediction and the
    leading eigenvalue share exceeds the simulated reference. Then "which prompts separate cores" has
    an answer, and stratifying on that factor could resolve pairs R768 could not.
B · PAIR-SPECIFIC, NO FACTOR. Observed ≈ fitted, leading share ≈ simulated. The heterogeneity is
    arm-specific residual and NO stratification could ever help -- which closes R770's fork and makes
    R769's required-n the only lever.
C · NEGATIVE DEPENDENCE. Observed falls materially BELOW the fitted prediction: prompts where one arm
    gains are prompts where another loses. Then the extension is not merely unordered, it is
    TRADE-OFF structured, and that is a different object from either A or B.

PREDICTION MATRIX  (excess = observed − fitted, arm-sharing pairs; leading share vs simulated)
  A -> excess >= +0.05 AND leading share above the simulated 97.5th percentile
  B -> |excess| < 0.05 AND leading share inside the simulated interval
  C -> excess <= −0.05

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
KILL -- CONDITIONAL, gated on the controls
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
if POSITIVE fires (a SYNTHETIC common factor injected into the arm residuals is recovered as a raised
   leading share and a positive excess) and g=0 fires (injecting NO factor recovers neither)
   and the E1 fit is admissible (all fitted `v_a` > 0, and the fit residual is small relative to the
   observed `var(d)` spread):
        excess >= +0.05 and share above the simulated 97.5 pct -> WORLD A. Name the factor's loading.
        |excess| < 0.05 and share inside the interval          -> WORLD B. R770's fork closes; the
                                                                  register gains "no stratification".
        excess <= -0.05                                        -> WORLD C. Trade-off structure.
else:
        UNVERIFIED. Never OVERTURNED, never CONFIRMED.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONTROLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
POSITIVE  inject a common prompt factor into the five arm residuals at loading lambda, sweep
          lambda in {0, 0.25, 0.5, 1.0} x the residual sd, and require the excess and the leading
          share to rise MONOTONELY and to be detected at 1.0. BAND, COMPUTED: at lambda = 0 nothing
          may be detected (g=0); at lambda = 1.0 it must be. The threshold sits strictly between two
          computed ends, and the sweep makes it a dose-response rather than one tuned number.
          ⚠ AND THE PLANT IS SIZED AGAINST THE STATISTIC THE TEST USES, not against a different one
            -- the defect R770 caught in its own positive control one round ago.
g=0       lambda = 0: the same machinery on unfactored residuals must return excess ~0 and a leading
          share inside its own simulated interval.
NEGATIVE  permute each difference vector's prompt order INDEPENDENTLY, 200 draws, and report the
          leading share. World excluded: "any 10 vectors of rank 4 give this leading share" -- if the
          permuted share matches the observed, the statistic is measuring the rank constraint alone.
SHAM      ingredient ABSENT, not inverted. Ingredient = THE PROMPT PAIRING ACROSS PAIRS. Simulate the
          arm residuals as INDEPENDENT gaussians with the fitted `v_a`, 200 draws, and report the
          leading-share distribution. Same rank, same variances, no shared prompt structure.
PLACEBO   include `topw_k4` vs `topw_k4_detA` -- an identical pair, difference exactly 0 -- and
          confirm it contributes no correlation (its variance is 0 and it must be excluded by the
          machinery rather than producing a division by zero).
NOISE     200 draws for both SHAM and NEGATIVE; intervals printed, never just means.
UNIT      instrument unit = a PROMPT (968 per vector); claim unit = the SET of five arms. The
          correlation is between PAIRS, which are neither -- so the pair count (10) is reported
          separately from the arm count (5) and the rank (4).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MULTIPLICITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
45 correlation cells (10 choose 2), split into 30 arm-sharing and 15 disjoint and reported
separately; 4 lambda levels; 200 sham draws; 200 permutation draws. The whole correlation matrix is
printed, not just its summary.

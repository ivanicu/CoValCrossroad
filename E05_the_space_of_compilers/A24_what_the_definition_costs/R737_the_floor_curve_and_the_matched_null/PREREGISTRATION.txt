written BEFORE run.py. P4 and the ARITHMETIC step ran first.

WHAT IS ALREADY SETTLED AND IS USED HERE, NOT RE-DERIVED
   R736 established that satisfaction is a function of (prompt, response, criterion) to 0.997357 and
   that 917 of 968 prompts carry >= 8 distinct scored criteria, so k=4 arms with a CHOSEN overlap can
   be assembled from disk. That capability is used; it is not re-measured.

⛔ DERIVATION FIRST, USED AS THE POSITIVE CONTROL
   Two k-subsets of a common pool sharing j components have raw mean correlation j/K by algebra when
   the components are independent and equal-variance. Verified on synthetic vectors before writing
   this file: j/K = 0.00 0.25 0.50 0.75 1.00 against measured 0.0379 0.2382 0.5158 0.7566 1.0000.
   THIS IS NOT A FINDING. It is the curve the construction must reproduce if it is assembling what it
   claims, and it is the only positive control available that does not share the instrument's blind
   spot -- the failure R736 was written about.

ESTIMAND
   (1) the MARGIN FLOOR as a function of constructed overlap j in {0,1,2,3,4}, at k=4, on a FIXED
       prompt population;
   (2) the ACTUAL pairwise criteria overlap of the arms the deliverable compares -- greedy, indep and
       the excluded oracle -- and hence the MATCHED floor each comparison should have used.

IDENTIFICATION
   (1) identified by construction on the prompts reachable at every target, which R736 counted at
       917 for j=0 rising to 968 for j=4. The comparison is made on the INTERSECTION, so the
       population is constant across j and the curve cannot confound overlap with which prompts
       contribute.
   (2) identified: both arms' selections are on disk.
   ⚠ NOT identified: whether the constructed arms are exchangeable with the real ones. A constructed
   arm draws from the scored pool; a real arm was produced by a rule. If a rule prefers criteria with
   particular satisfaction profiles, the constructed floor is a floor for RANDOM subsets and not for
   RULE-PRODUCED ones. That gap is named, and the round reports its floor as the random-subset floor.

SCOPE
   population : the prompts reachable at every target overlap, k=4 · instrument margin correlation
                against the reference arm · baseline R735's 0.6458 k-matched floor · regime default
                emitter
WORLDS
   W-CURVE     the floor rises smoothly with j and the matched floor for the real comparison differs
               from 0.6458. Then every excess reported in this arc has a third value.
   W-FLAT      the floor does not move with j. Then the construction is not assembling what it claims
               and the positive control should already have failed.
   prediction matrix
      raw corr matches j/K and floor rises  -> CURVE 0.92  FLAT 0.03
      raw corr matches but floor flat       -> the subtrahend dominates entirely; report that
      raw corr does NOT match j/K           -> UNVERIFIED; the construction is not what it claims

POSITIVE CTRL   the constructed arms' RAW correlation must reproduce j/K across all five targets,
                threshold from the algebra: max deviation < 0.05. ⭐ This does not share the
                instrument's blind spot, because j/K is derived from the construction's definition
                and not measured with the same code path.
g=0             at j=0 the two arms share no criterion, so their raw correlation must be at the
                no-shared-component level and the floor must fall to the subtrahend-only value.
NEGATIVE CTRL   assign the SHARED criteria at random per prompt rather than fixing them, so the
                nominal j is not the realised j, and require the curve to flatten. excluded world:
                "the curve comes from the label j rather than from actual sharing".
SHAM            build both arms from the SAME draw ignoring j -- the overlap ingredient absent
                rather than inverted -- and require a single value with no dependence on j.
PLACEBO         j = 4 makes the two arms identical, so the floor must be EXACTLY 1.0.
NOISE FLOOR     20 construction seeds per target; the curve is reported with its seed spread and the
                matched-floor comparison is made in units of that spread.
MULTIPLICITY    5 targets x 20 seeds x 2 statistics, every cell reported.
SPECIFICATION   target j x seed x statistic (raw correlation, margin floor)
SEEDS           20 per target; two PYTHONHASHSEEDs byte-identical, writes verified
ARTIFACT        results/r737_floor_curve.json with tree_sha

## ⚠ The identification limit, which is not a caveat but the shape of the check
The label is a **34-character prefix** before each number. **That detects disagreement and can never
prove agreement**: two different quantities can share a prefix, and one quantity can be phrased two
ways. **`0` here means "no collision surfaced", not "no drift exists".**

⭐ **This is the honest form of a clean result.** P16's warning is that *a number stated twice
drifts*; **114 decimals with 14 repeats and no detected drift is evidence the discipline held — and
it is not proof, and saying so costs nothing.**

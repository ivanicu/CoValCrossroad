R816 · WHAT ACCOUNTS FOR THE SHIFT — target reliability, or the tie rate?
written before the run · NBOOT 1200 · ZEFF 2.801585

PROVOCATION
R815 found nine arms each scoring 0.005-0.012 HIGHER against `personal` than against `world`, a
uniform shift that left the ordering at Spearman 1.0000. Its NEXT proposed one explanation: compute
`CEIL_H` on `personal` and see whether the target is simply more reliable.

⛔ CHECK #418 FOUND THAT THE DIRECTION OF THAT INFERENCE IS NEARLY FORCED, and that a SECOND
mechanism was never named.
 (1) Higher inter-annotator agreement raises A2 for ANY predictor, so "does the ceiling point the
     same way" is not evidence. The real question is whether it QUANTITATIVELY accounts for the
     shift, and that is a slope, not a sign.
 (2) Measured on the 293-prompt population: `CEIL_H` world **0.533454**, personal **0.543065**,
     gap **+0.009611** -- close to the observed mean shift. But the TIE RATE is world **0.145080**,
     personal **0.124460**, gap **-0.020620**, and [DERIVATION] a strict-signed arm can NEVER match
     a tied human pair, so its attainable maximum is 1 - tie_rate. A tie-rate drop of 0.0206 makes
     up to +0.0206 available -- MORE than the whole observed shift. Two mechanisms, both pointing
     the same way, one of them able to over-explain it alone.
 (3) The obvious third rival is ruled out by measurement, not assumption: annotators per prompt is
     median 12 in BOTH blocks, so the shift is not panel depth.

ESTIMAND
E1  per arm, the per-prompt regression of (A2_personal - A2_world) on (CEIL_personal - CEIL_world):
    slope, intercept, R^2.
E2  ⭐ the pre-registered test: is the slope 1 and the intercept 0? A slope of 1 means the shift IS
    the reliability shift; anything else means reliability is one term among others.
E3  ⭐ the TIE mechanism as a rival, entered on its own and jointly: the per-prompt tie-rate
    difference, and the joint model with both terms.
E4  what is left after both -- the residual shift, with its interval.

IDENTIFICATION
Identified per prompt: every quantity is a within-prompt difference across two blocks answered about
the SAME four responses. ⚠ The two regressors are themselves correlated -- a prompt where annotators
agree more will also tie less -- so the JOINT model's coefficients are not separately interpretable
if the correlation is high. That correlation is measured and reported BEFORE the joint fit, and if
|r| > 0.7 the round reports bounds from the two single-term models instead of a decomposition.

DERIVED BEFORE MEASURING
D1 [DERIVATION] a strict-signed arm cannot match a tied human pair, so its attainable A2 is bounded
   by 1 - tie_rate. This is why the tie term belongs in the model at all.
D2 [DERIVATION] a HIGHER inter-annotator ceiling raises A2 for any predictor, so the SIGN of the
   ceiling term is forced and only its MAGNITUDE is evidence. The round tests slope = 1, not
   slope > 0.
D3 the nine arms differ in how strict they are; an arm that ties more should be less sensitive to
   the tie term. That is a testable ordering across arms, not an assumption.
D4 [DERIVATION] the placebo -- an arm regressed on a zero difference -- has slope undefined and a
   zero outcome; it is run as a numerical check that the pipeline returns 0, not as evidence.

WORLDS
A  RELIABILITY: the ceiling slope is indistinguishable from 1 and the tie term adds nothing once it
   is in -> the shift IS the target's reliability and nothing about the arms is involved.
B  TIES: the tie term dominates and the ceiling term collapses when both are entered -> the shift is
   an artefact of how often humans tie, which is a property of the QUESTION and not of agreement.
C  BOTH OR NEITHER: neither single term accounts for the shift -> report the decomposition and the
   residual, and claim no single mechanism.
CHECKED FIRST: B, because it is the mechanism R815's NEXT did not name and would have missed.

PREDICTION MATRIX
                          A                B                 C
ceiling slope          ~ 1              ~ 0 when joint     between
tie slope              ~ 0 when joint   large              between
residual shift         ~ 0              ~ 0                non-zero

CONTROLS
OBJECT   reproduce R815's committed per-arm shifts to 1e-6 (coval_core -0.0119, gen_sham -0.0105)
         and R793-style CEIL_H on `world` over this population. Exit 2 otherwise.
PLACEBO  D4: an arm against itself -> outcome identically 0 at every prompt.
POSITIVE plant a known per-prompt shift proportional to the ceiling difference with coefficient c in
         {0, 0.5, 1.0, 2.0}; the recovered slope must track c. At c=0 it must NOT recover a slope
         distinguishable from the unplanted one -- that is the g=0 check.
NEGATIVE the per-prompt PAIRING between the two blocks destroyed (personal values attached to other
         prompts) -> both slopes must collapse to 0. 200 permutations.
NOISE FLOOR 20 half-splits of the 293 prompts; the sd of every slope.

PRE-REGISTERED KILL -- conditional, never a bare threshold

      elif the ceiling slope's CI contains 1 and the tie slope's contains 0   -> WORLD A
      else                                                                     -> WORLD C

      UNVERIFIED   (never OVERTURNED, never CONFIRMED)

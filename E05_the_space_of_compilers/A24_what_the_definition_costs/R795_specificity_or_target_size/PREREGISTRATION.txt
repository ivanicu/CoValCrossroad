R795 · is Q1's specificity about the RUBRIC's identity, or about the TARGET's SIZE?
WRITTEN BEFORE ANY CODE FOR THIS ROUND. 2026-08-06.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THIS ROUND EXISTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
R794 resolved Q1 for MATCHING (prompt-matched rubric 0.7850 against a shuffled one 0.4888, excess
+0.2961 [+0.2744, +0.3166]) and left SPECIFICITY as a point estimate: the gap to a random arm's
class is +0.0487 with no interval. Its NEXT asked for that interval.

CHECK #397 attacked the proposed comparison BEFORE building it, and it is confounded.

  arm             mean criteria per prompt
  `full`          15.48
  every k4 arm     4.00
  `genericpool16` 16.00   (prompt-BLIND -- size-matched to `full`, content-mismatched)

⛔ SO `vs full` AGAINST `vs (a k=4 ARM'S CLASS)` VARIES TWO THINGS AT ONCE: whose criteria the
target is built from, and HOW MANY. A class built by summing 15 criteria has a different tie and
sign structure from one built by summing 4, before any question of content. Delivering R794's NEXT
as written would have produced an interval around a confounded quantity — which is worse than the
point estimate it replaces, because an interval reads as settled.

P4: no round in this arc sweeps k as a property of the TARGET; every k-sweep in the campaign varies
the ARM. The instrument is new and its confound is the reason the round exists.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DISCLOSURE -- what was already known when this was written
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
KNOWN and quoted: R794's Q1 numbers, the +0.0487 gap, the mean-k table above (computed in check
#397 from `core_*.json`). **NOT known: any point of the size dose-response**, whether it is monotone,
where `vs full` sits on it, or whether identity survives once size is matched.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE ARITHMETIC, DERIVED BEFORE MEASURING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D1  AGREEMENT WITH A k-SUBSET OF `full` MUST RISE TOWARD 1.0 AS k -> ALL, because at k = all the
    target IS `full` and the quantity is `vs full` exactly. **DERIVATION**, and it is this round's
    POSITIVE control: the dose must be monotone and must terminate at the measured 0.7850.
D2  AT k = 1 THE TARGET IS A SINGLE CRITERION, whose class is often degenerate (a criterion
    satisfied equally by all four responses adds a constant and produces all-zero signs). So the low
    end of the dose is not a clean floor and must be reported with the share of degenerate targets
    rather than as a number. **DERIVATION.**
D3  THE SIZE EFFECT AND THE IDENTITY EFFECT ARE SEPARABLE BY CONSTRUCTION: hold content fixed and
    vary k (subsets of `full`), or hold k fixed and vary content (other arms' k=4 classes against
    random 4-subsets of `full`). Two one-factor comparisons beat one two-factor one. **DERIVATION**,
    and it is the design.
D4  `coval_core`'s OWN CRITERIA ARE NOT NECESSARILY IN `full`. R785 measured the released core as
    98.8% novel against its rubric, so a k-subset of `full` is NOT a superset of the core and the
    dose cannot be read as "how much of the core's own text is in the target". **DERIVATION**, and
    it forbids the tempting reading.

STRONGEST CONFOUND, written BEFORE the run: the subset targets are drawn from `full`, so as k grows
they converge on `full` BY CONSTRUCTION -- the dose measures convergence to a specific object, not
"size" in general. CONTROL IN THE SAME ITERATION: the same dose is run on targets built from a
DIFFERENT PROMPT's `full` at each k. If the mismatched dose is flat while the matched one rises, the
rise is about content; if both rise, part of it is size alone.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ESTIMANDS (before method)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E1  ⭐ THE SIZE DOSE, CONTENT FIXED: `coval_core vs (random k-subset of its prompt's `full`)` for
    k in {1, 2, 4, 8, 12, all}, 20 draws each, with the share of degenerate targets per k (D2).
E2  ⭐ THE IDENTITY TEST AT MATCHED k: at k = 4, `coval_core vs (random 4-subset of `full`)` against
    `coval_core vs (each other k=4 arm's class)`. Same target size, different content source.
E3  ⭐ THE SPECIFICITY INTERVAL R794 ASKED FOR, now on a size-matched contrast: the difference
    between the matched-content and mismatched-content cells at k = 4, with a cluster-bootstrap CI
    over the 968 prompts.
E4  the real size-matched comparator: `coval_core vs `genericpool16`'s class` (k = 16, prompt-blind)
    beside `vs full` (k = 15.48, prompt-matched). One factor, cleanly.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IDENTIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E1-E4 exact given the per-criterion satisfaction tensor, which is on disk in `sat_full.npz`.
⚠ PARTIALLY IDENTIFIED: "identity" here means "built from this prompt's rubric text" and nothing
finer. Whether the core tracks the rubric's MEANING rather than its scoring behaviour is the
construct wall (`corebench/score.py:34`) and is not touched.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
population  968 prompts; targets built from `coval_full`'s per-criterion satisfactions
instrument  pairwise-sign agreement between `coval_core`'s class and the target's class
baseline    for E1 the mismatched-prompt dose; for E2/E3 the other k=4 arms' classes
regime      first release, home judge, this tree_sha

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WORLDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A · IDENTITY MATTERS. At matched k = 4, agreement with `full`-derived targets exceeds agreement with
    other arms' k=4 classes, resolved. Then "its own rubric" is licensed and R794's clause can keep
    the word.
B · SIZE EXPLAINS IT. At matched k = 4 the difference is unresolved, and the dose in k accounts for
    the +0.0487. Then the specificity claim dies and the clause must say "a large target" rather
    than "its own rubric".
C · THE TARGET BARELY MATTERS. The dose is flat in k AND identical across content sources. Then Q1
    is weak in a way R794 did not see, and the whole matching result needs re-reading.

PREDICTION MATRIX
  A -> E3's CI excludes 0 and is positive
  B -> E3's CI contains 0, and the k=4 matched cell sits near the k=4 arms' cell
  C -> the E1 dose is flat within its own draw spread from k=2 upward
  any other pattern -> report; CLAIM NO WORLD.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
KILL -- CONDITIONAL, gated on the controls
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
if OBJECT passes (the k=all cell reproduces R794's `vs full` = 0.7850 to 1e-9 -- exit 2 otherwise,
   since a dose whose endpoint disagrees with the committed number is measuring something else)
   and PLACEBO returns exactly 1.0 for an arm against its own class
   and POSITIVE fires (D1: the matched dose is MONOTONE non-decreasing in k and terminates at 0.7850)
   and NEGATIVE fires (the mismatched-prompt dose is materially below the matched one at every
       k >= 2):
        E1 flat from k=2 upward                          -> WORLD C   (checked FIRST)
        E3 CI excludes 0 and positive                    -> WORLD A
        E3 CI contains 0                                 -> WORLD B
        otherwise                                        -> report; claim no world.
else:
        UNVERIFIED. Never OVERTURNED, never CONFIRMED.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONTROLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OBJECT      the k=all cell must equal R794's committed `vs full` to 1e-9. Exit 2, never 0.
PLACEBO     an arm against its OWN class: exactly 1.0.
POSITIVE    D1's monotone dose, terminating at the committed value. BAND: the k=1 cell and the k=all
            cell must differ; equality means the target size does nothing and no threshold is
            admissible.
NEGATIVE    the same dose with targets built from a DIFFERENT prompt's `full`. World it excludes:
            "the dose measures target SIZE in general rather than convergence to this prompt's
            rubric." Reported as a curve, not one point.
DEGENERACY  per k, the share of targets whose class is all-zero (D2). A cell with a large share is
            reported with it and is not compared as though it were clean.
NOISE FLOOR MEASURED: the spread across the 20 subset draws at each k.
UNIT        instrument unit = a (prompt, subset draw) target; claim unit = the k CELL; E3's unit =
            a CONTRAST. Three strings, required unequal.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MULTIPLICITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6 k-cells x 2 content sources (matched, mismatched) = 12 dose cells, plus E2's comparison against
each of the k=4 arms. BH at q = 0.05 over the whole set. Cells tested, surviving and non-survivors
printed. The dose cells are a CURVE and are reported whole regardless of survival.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SPECIFICATION CURVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
k              1 | 2 | 4 | 8 | 12 | all
content        this prompt's `full` | a different prompt's `full` | other arms' k=4 classes
draws          20 subset draws per cell
NBOOT          1200
seeds          3

⛔ CHECK #378, TWO CORRECTIONS BEFORE ANY DESIGN:
 ① MY OWN ARITHMETIC WAS WRONG. 62 counts BIPARTITIONS of families into two labelled groups. The axis
   test labels each family by its RULE, and with six families of which three share a rule the number
   of distinct assignments is **C(6,3) = 20**, so the permutation p floors at **1/20 = 0.05** rather
   than 1/62. The improvement over R775's 1/4 is real (5x) and smaller than I claimed.
 ② AND THE REGISTERED READING MAY BE BACKWARDS. `random_k` arms draw their criteria AT RANDOM from
   the pool, so two random families with different seeds hold DIFFERENT criteria. Their difference
   magnitudes would still co-move wherever the POOL ITSELF is heterogeneous on a prompt -- which is a
   PROMPT PROPERTY, not a rule artifact. **So the 0.7154 may be the prompt component showing most
   clearly where the arms are least structured**, which is the opposite of R775's reading.

⭐ THAT SUGGESTS THE INSTRUMENT THIS WHOLE THREAD HAS LACKED: a prompt covariate computed with NO ARMS
   AT ALL. `sat_genericpool16.npz` gives, per prompt, the satisfaction of 16 fixed criteria on 4
   responses. Its per-prompt SPREAD is a property of (prompt, pool) alone -- no selection, no arm, no
   difference vector -- and it is exactly what "how much do criteria disagree here" means.

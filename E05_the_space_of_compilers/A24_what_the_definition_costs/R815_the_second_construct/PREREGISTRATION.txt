second question put to the same people about the same responses, and no round has used it.

ESTIMAND
E1  every load-bearing arm's A2 against `personal`, on the 321 prompts carrying both blocks.
E2  ⭐ the PAIRED difference A2(world) - A2(personal), same prompts, same annotators.
E3  ⭐ the ORDERING: rank correlation between the arms' ordering under `world` and under `personal`.
    This is the quantity the definition depends on -- it speaks of "the human target" as if singular.
E4  the arc's committed margins recomputed under `personal`: does the fitted-vs-honest ordering, and
    does `coval_core`'s standing, survive the change of question?

IDENTIFICATION
Identified on the 321-prompt subpopulation. ⚠ 321 is a THIRD of 968, so intervals are ~1.7x wider by
construction; the MDE is computed and reported BEFORE any null is called. A null here is a statement
about power unless the MDE says otherwise.
⚠ The `personal` block is a DIFFERENT QUESTION, not a replicate. It cannot serve as a reliability
estimate for `world`, and this round never treats it as one.

DERIVED BEFORE MEASURING
D1 [DERIVATION] an arm built FROM the `personal` modal class must beat every real arm on `personal`.
   POSITIVE, with a known direction; and it must NOT beat them on `world` by the same margin.
D2 [DERIVATION] an arm against itself is exactly 0 on either target. PLACEBO.
D3 the 321 prompts are not a random third: they are the prompts where annotators were ASKED the
   second question. Any world-target number on them may differ from the 968-prompt figure for that
   reason alone, so the round reports the world-target A2 on BOTH populations and never treats the
   321 as a sample of the 968.
D4 48.4% disagreement between the two blocks is a fact about ANNOTATORS, not about arms. It bounds
   how different the two targets can be but does not predict which arms move.

WORLDS
A  TARGET-INVARIANT: the arms' ordering is preserved (rank correlation >= 0.9) and no committed
   margin flips sign -> "human judgement" is one thing for the definition's purposes.
B  TARGET-DEPENDENT: the ordering changes materially or a committed margin flips -> the definition
   must name WHICH human question, and every number this arc has published is scoped to `world`.
C  UNDERPOWERED: the 321-prompt MDE exceeds the margins under test -> report the MDE and claim
   nothing.
CHECKED FIRST: B, because it rescopes the whole arc.

PREDICTION MATRIX

rank correlation           >= 0.9         < 0.9          any
any committed margin flips  no             yes            n/a
MDE vs margins             smaller        smaller        larger

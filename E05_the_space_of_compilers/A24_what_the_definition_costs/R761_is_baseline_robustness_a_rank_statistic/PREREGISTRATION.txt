R761 · is ②-robustness a fact about clause ③, or a rank statistic of A2?

WHAT IS ACTUALLY OPEN IS R527'S SHARP FINDING, and TWO independent things now attack it. R527
published, and STATEMENT.md carries: "the four arms admitted at EVERY specification are exactly the
four ③ excludes -- the label-readers are baseline-robust BECAUSE they read the answer."

① THE INSTRUMENT WAS WRONG IN THE DIRECTION THAT MATTERS. R760 showed ③-as-a-blocklist excludes 4
   names where ③-as-a-rule excludes 11 tags. R527's "exactly the four ③ excludes" was measured

② THE POPULATION COULD NOT HAVE CONTAINED THE COUNTEREXAMPLES. R527 drew its arms from R294's
   census, and MEASURED HERE BEFORE THE RUN: none of R729's seven target-reading tags is in that
   census, though all seven have sat_*.npz on disk. So 7 of the 11 target-reading arms were
   invisible to the round that concluded the target-readers are the robust ones.

⚠ AND A THIRD, WHICH IS MINE AND NOT R527'S: robustness to the baseline may be a RANK STATISTIC OF
   A2, in which case the finding is a reparameterisation and not a fact about ③ at all.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ESTIMAND (before method)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E1  rob(a) = the share of the 1,820 admissible size-4 references at which arm a clears clause ②,
    under R294's exact estimator (paired cluster bootstrap, MDE floor, three-valued verdict).
E2  the INVERSION COUNT of rob against mean A2: pairs (a,b) with A2(a) > A2(b) but rob(a) < rob(b).
E3  R527's identity re-evaluated on the widened population under BOTH ③ implementations:
    is { a : rob(a) = 1.0 } equal to { a : ③ excludes a } ?

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
⛔ THE ARITHMETIC, DERIVED BEFORE MEASURING -- TWO RESULTS ARE FORCED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D1  ROBUSTNESS IS MONOTONE IN A2 UP TO THE PAIRED SE. Admission is
    mean(x_a - y_s) - z*se(a,s) > 0. If se(a,s) were constant in s and a, rob(a) would be a
    STRICTLY INCREASING function of mean A2 and the arm ORDERING WOULD BE FORCED. So "the
    highest-scoring arms are the most baseline-robust" is ALGEBRA, not a finding. The only
    measurement available is the RESIDUAL -- how many arm pairs invert, and which.
    ⇒ E2 is the round's real content, and the sham for it is a computed random-ordering baseline.
D2  THE FULL 1,820-WIDE BOOTSTRAP IS EXACTLY COMPUTABLE AT MARGINAL COST. Under a shared index
    matrix the bootstrap mean is LINEAR: (x-y)[ib].mean(1) = x[ib].mean(1) - y[ib].mean(1). And
    var(x-y) = var(x) + var(y) - 2cov(x,y), whose cross term is one matrix product. So 45,500 exact
    bootstrap cells cost the same as 1,845 marginals. NOTHING IS EARNED BY THE GRID BEING LARGE and
    the report must not present its size as effort.

STRONGEST CONFOUND, written BEFORE the run: the seven newly-visible arms may fail ② for a reason
that has nothing to do with reading the target -- e.g. missing prompt coverage, so their difference
vectors are shorter and their MDE larger. CONTROL IN THE SAME ITERATION: report each arm's prompt
coverage beside its rob, and recompute the identity on the coverage-complete subset as a second
specification.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WORLDS -- two axes, registered separately because they are not exclusive
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q1 · IS rob A REPARAMETERISATION OF A2?
  B · YES. inversions <= 1. R527's sharp finding restates "the label-readers score highest", which
      is what label-reading DOES, and it must be relabelled a DERIVATION on the page.
  B'· NO. inversions >= 3. rob carries information A2 does not, and the identity is testable.
Q2 · DOES ③ TRACK rob ON THE WIDENED POPULATION?
  A · YES. { rob = 1.0 } == { ③rule excludes }. The repair to ③ strengthens R527's identity.
  C · NO. some target-reading arm has rob < 1.0, or a ③-admitted arm has rob = 1.0. Then ③ does not
      track baseline-robustness and R527's identity was an artifact of its 16-arm population.

PREDICTION MATRIX  (E2 inversions, E3 set equality)
  B  -> inversions in [0,1]                         ; E3 outcome is uninformative whatever it is
  B' -> inversions >= 3
  A  -> the two sets are equal under ③rule
  C  -> the two sets differ, and the differing arms are named

CONTROLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROVENANCE R294's stored c2 for the k=4 census arms at the published baseline, tolerance 1e-6. The
           round EXITS 2 if it fails, because every number below is on R294's scale or on none.
POSITIVE   reproduce R527's COMMITTED coval_core_by_spec -- {p000..p095: True, p100: False} -- at
           the same 7 percentiles plus published. BAND, COMPUTED: an instrument admitting
           everything returns True on all 8 (wrong at p100); one admitting nothing returns False on
           all 8 (wrong on the other 7). The threshold is EXACT MATCH ON ALL 8, unreachable from
           either degenerate end. This is the "run the pattern where the answer is known" remedy,
           and R527's artifact is the known answer.
g=0        an arm compared AGAINST ITSELF must be admitted at 0 of 1,820. A zero difference is never
           resolvedly better; any admission means the verdict rule is broken.
NEGATIVE   destroy the per-prompt PAIRING -- permute prompt ids on the arm side only, 200 draws,
           refit nothing (the estimator has no fit) -- and report the rob DISTRIBUTION, not one
           draw. World excluded: "rob is carried by the per-prompt pairing rather than by the
           marginal gap". ⚠ A permutation answers DID THE PAIRING MATTER and never WHY, so the
           result is read only as that.
SHAM       ingredient ABSENT, not inverted, and there are two ingredients so there are two shams:
           S1 for Q1 -- a RANDOM arm ordering in place of the A2 ordering, 200 draws. Its expected
              inversion count is C(n,2)/2 and that is the computed ceiling for E2.
           S2 for Q2 -- a RANDOM blocklist of the same size as ③rule's excluded set, 200 draws,
              scored on set-equality with { rob = 1.0 }. R760 ran this at size 4; here at size 11.
PLACEBO    the *_sham arms (coval_core_sham, gen_sham, promptecho_sham, topw_k4_sham) must have
           rob ~ 0. They are built to fail ②; if they are robust, the estimator is not measuring ②.
NOISE      no rng except the bootstrap index matrix and the two shams; all seeded, spreads printed.
UNIT       instrument unit = an (arm, reference) CELL; claim unit = an ARM. 45,500 vs 30. Never
           conflated, and the cell count is NOT evidence of anything (D2).

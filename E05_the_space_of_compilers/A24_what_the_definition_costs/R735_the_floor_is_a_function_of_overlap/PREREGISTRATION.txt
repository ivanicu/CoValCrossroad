⛔ MY OWN IDENTIFICATION WORRY WAS WRONG, AND CHECKING IT IS WHY THIS ROUND EXISTS
   R734's NEXT proposed regressing a pair's floor on its criteria overlap. I expected n=3 pairs and
   an unidentified regression. Counted: the release carries random-selection arms at
   k in {2,3,4,6,8,12} x 3 seeds, of which 19 are usable as floor members -> 171 pairs, with per-pair
   mean overlap spanning roughly 0.3 to 9. The regression is identified with room to spare.
   ⭐ AND R734's FLOOR USED THREE PAIRS FROM ONE k-FAMILY. If the floor depends on k, that floor was
   matched to nothing, and a k-matched floor is the correct comparator for every claim built on it.

ESTIMAND -- three, and the first is a DERIVATION used as a control
   (1) DERIVATION. Two arms drawing k1 and k2 criteria uniformly at random from a prompt's candidate
       set of size n_p share, in expectation, k1*k2/n_p criteria. This is algebra and is NOT evidence.
       It is used as a POSITIVE CONTROL on the whole framing: if measured overlap does not match it
       for arms that ARE random by construction, the overlap instrument is broken and nothing below
       is readable.
   (2) MEASUREMENT. Across the 171 pairs, does the shared-subtrahend floor track measured overlap?
   (3) CONSEQUENCE. What is the k-MATCHED floor for the comparisons R733 and R734 rest on, and does
       it differ from the 0.5034 those rounds used?

IDENTIFICATION
   (1) exact. (2) identified over 171 pairs with overlap spanning an order of magnitude.
   (3) identified for the k values the release carries; NOT identified for k values it does not.
   ⚠ AND A CONFOUND NAMED BEFORE THE RUN: overlap and k are not independent -- overlap rises with
   both k1 and k2 by construction. So a floor-overlap relation could be a floor-k relation wearing
   overlap's clothes. The round therefore reports the relation WITHIN k-strata as well as across
   them, and a relation that survives only across strata is reported as confounded with k.

                regime default emitter only

   W-K-ONLY    the relation holds across strata but vanishes within them. Then the floor is a

⚠ AND THE OBVIOUS INFERENCE IS NOT AVAILABLE. "The baseline is extreme for the POOL's own A2" does

The percentile in each derived statistic's own distribution must be measured separately, and that
is E4.

IDENTIFICATION
Exact and exhaustive: C(16,4) = 1,820 is enumerated in full, so these are population quantities over
the baseline family, not estimates of it. Sampling error enters only through the prompts, and the
prompt bootstrap is reported separately from the baseline spread -- the two are NOT pooled, because
they answer different questions.

DERIVED BEFORE MEASURING
D1 [DERIVATION] a baseline equal to the arm itself gives a margin of exactly 0 at every prompt.
   PLACEBO.
D2 [DERIVATION] `_perfect_leak` regressed on itself is exactly 1.000 under EVERY baseline, because
   the baseline cancels in a self-regression. If it is not, the sweep is mis-wired.
D3 the baseline enters BOTH the x-side (the leak's margin) and the y-side (the arm's margin), so it
   partially cancels in a slope -- the sweep may therefore move the scale far less than it moves
   the levels. Predicted BEFORE the run, so that a small spread is not read as a null result.
D4 R805's +0.0553 is NOT in scope: it uses `genericpool16`, all sixteen, so it has no subset choice
   to sweep. Saying which committed numbers this round can and cannot touch is part of the round.

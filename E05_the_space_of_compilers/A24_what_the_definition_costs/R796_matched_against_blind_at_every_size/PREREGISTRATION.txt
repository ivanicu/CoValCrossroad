R796 · what prompt-matching is worth when the floor is ABSENCE, at every target size
WRITTEN BEFORE ANY CODE FOR THIS ROUND. 2026-08-06.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THIS ROUND EXISTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
R795 established that R794's Q1 floor was a POISON: a target built from ANOTHER PROMPT's rubric is
misdirection, sits at ~0.50 at every k, and makes prompt-matching look worth +0.2796. The genuinely
NEUTRAL floor -- criteria that never see the prompt -- was measured at ONE size only:
`genericpool16` (k=16, blind) 0.7886 against `full` (k=15.48, matched) 0.7850, contrast
-0.0036 [-0.0195, +0.0119], UNRESOLVED.

R795's NEXT asked for that comparison across the whole population. CHECK #398 found the instrument
for the stronger version: **`sat_genericpool16.npz` ships PER-CRITERION satisfactions**, so a BLIND
dose over k is constructible exactly parallel to R795's matched one. One point becomes a curve.

⚠ AND A NEAR-MISS THE CHECK CAUGHT. `randblind_k4_s0/s1/s2` look like blind comparators and cover
**1 prompt each**, not 968. Treating them as population arms would have produced a "blind baseline"
from three single-prompt artifacts. Only `generic` (k=4) and `genericpool16` (k=16) are blind with
full coverage, and `generic` IS `POOL[0:4]` (R788) -- so it sits inside the blind k=4 dose and serves
as a consistency check that the dose reproduces a real released arm.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DISCLOSURE -- what was already known when this was written
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
KNOWN and quoted: R795's matched dose (0.6403, 0.6944, 0.7362, 0.7691, 0.7801, 0.7850 at
k = 1, 2, 4, 8, 12, all), its poison dose flat at ~0.50, and the single neutral point 0.7886.
**NOT known: any point of the BLIND dose except its k=16 endpoint**, nor the matched-minus-blind gap
at any smaller k, nor whether it has a consistent sign.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE ARITHMETIC, DERIVED BEFORE MEASURING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D1  THE BLIND DOSE AT k=16 IS `vs genericpool16` EXACTLY, because the subset is all 16 criteria.
    **DERIVATION**, and it is this round's OBJECT check: the endpoint must equal R795's committed
    0.7886 to floating point, as the matched dose's endpoint equalled 0.7850.
D2  BOTH DOSES MUST RISE WITH k. More criteria summed means a target whose class is less sensitive
    to any single criterion, and R795 measured the matched dose rising monotonically. If the blind
    dose does NOT rise, the two curves are not comparable and the gap is not a size-matched contrast.
    **DERIVATION-adjacent**: the prediction is forced, the measurement is not.
D3  THE MATCHED CELL AT k=16 IS NOT PURE. `full` carries mean 15.48 criteria (min 4, max 39), so a
    request for 16 is capped per prompt at what exists. The k=16 matched cell is therefore a MIXTURE
    of sizes and must be labelled; the clean size-matched comparisons are k <= 12.
    **DERIVATION**, and it forbids reading the top cell as a matched pair.
D4  `generic` = `POOL[0:4]` (R788), so `coval_core vs generic` must lie inside the blind k=4 dose's
    draw distribution. **DERIVATION**, and it is a free consistency check on the whole construction.

STRONGEST CONFOUND, written BEFORE the run: the two doses draw from pools of different sizes -- `full`
has mean 15.48 criteria to choose from and `genericpool16` has exactly 16 -- so at a given k the
matched subsets are drawn from a smaller, prompt-varying pool. A subset of 4 from a pool of 5 is more
constrained than 4 from 16. CONTROL IN THE SAME ITERATION: restrict the matched dose to prompts with
at least 16 criteria and re-run the gap on that subpopulation, so both pools are >= 16 and the draw
constraint matches. Report both populations.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ESTIMANDS (before method)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E1  ⭐ THE TWO DOSES: `coval_core vs (random k-subset)` for k in {1, 2, 4, 8, 12, 16}, from this
    prompt's `full` (MATCHED) and from `genericpool16` (BLIND), 20 draws each, with per-cell spread.
E2  ⭐ THE GAP AT EACH k, paired per prompt with a cluster-bootstrap CI: matched minus blind. This is
    what prompt-matching is worth with target size held fixed and the floor being ABSENCE.
E3  the whole population: for each of the 20 distinct objects, `vs full` minus `vs genericpool16`,
    with CIs and BH over the 20 -- does the released core stand out among arms?
E4  the pool-size confound control (see above), and D4's consistency check that
    `coval_core vs generic` lies inside the blind k=4 draw distribution.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IDENTIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E1-E4 exact given the two per-criterion tensors, both on disk.
⚠ PARTIALLY IDENTIFIED: "blind" means "these 16 criteria were not written for this prompt". It does
not mean "uninformative" -- a generic criterion can still be relevant. So a null gap says
prompt-specific WRITING adds nothing measurable, not that the target is meaningless.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
population  968 prompts; and the >=16-criteria subpopulation for the confound control
instrument  pairwise-sign agreement between `coval_core`'s class and the target's class
baseline    the BLIND dose -- absence, not misdirection
regime      first release, home judge, this tree_sha

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WORLDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A · PROMPT-MATCHING IS WORTH SOMETHING. The matched-minus-blind gap is positive and resolved at a
    majority of the clean cells (k <= 12). Then "preserves the rubric's verdicts" survives with the
    neutral floor and the clause keeps its meaning, at a size the round can state.
B · PROMPT-MATCHING IS WORTH NOTHING MEASURABLE. The gap is unresolved or negative at a majority of
    clean cells. Then what R794 called preserving the rubric is agreement with ANY competent target
    of that size, and the clause must be rewritten or dropped.
C · IT DEPENDS ON SIZE. The gap is resolved with one sign at small k and the opposite (or zero) at
    large k. Then the clause is size-conditional and neither A nor B may be stated flat.

PREDICTION MATRIX
  A -> gap positive and CI excludes 0 in >= 3 of the 5 clean cells (k = 1, 2, 4, 8, 12)
  B -> gap unresolved or negative in >= 3 of the 5 clean cells
  C -> resolved positive in some cells and resolved negative in others
  any other pattern -> report; CLAIM NO WORLD.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
KILL -- CONDITIONAL, gated on the controls
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
if OBJECT passes (D1: the blind k=16 cell equals R795's committed `vs genericpool16` = 0.7886 to
   1e-9, AND the matched all-criteria cell equals 0.7850 to 1e-9 -- exit 2 otherwise)
   and PLACEBO returns exactly 1.0 for `coval_core` against its own class
   and POSITIVE fires (D2: BOTH doses monotone non-decreasing in k; if the blind dose does not rise,
       the curves are not comparable and the round stops)
   and NEGATIVE fires (shuffling `coval_core`'s class across prompts collapses BOTH doses toward
       chance, establishing that either dose measures an arm-target correspondence at all):
        resolved with both signs across cells       -> WORLD C   (checked FIRST)
        positive and resolved in >= 3 of 5          -> WORLD A
        unresolved or negative in >= 3 of 5         -> WORLD B
        otherwise                                   -> report; claim no world.
else:
        UNVERIFIED. Never OVERTURNED, never CONFIRMED.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONTROLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OBJECT      D1's two endpoints, each against a committed number, to 1e-9. Exit 2, never 0.
PLACEBO     `coval_core` against its OWN class: exactly 1.0.
POSITIVE    D2's monotonicity on BOTH doses, with the band (k=1 to k=16) printed for each. A flat
            dose means target size does nothing and no size-matched contrast is admissible.
NEGATIVE    shuffle `coval_core`'s class across prompts and recompute both doses. World it excludes:
            "the doses measure a property of the TARGETS alone rather than a correspondence between
            this arm and its prompt's targets."
CONFOUND    the pool-size control: re-run the gap on prompts with >= 16 criteria, so both pools are
            at least 16 and the draw constraint matches. Registered BEFORE the run.
CONSISTENCY D4: `coval_core vs generic` must lie inside the blind k=4 draw distribution.
NOISE FLOOR MEASURED: the spread across the 20 subset draws at each cell.
UNIT        instrument unit = a (prompt, subset draw) TARGET; claim unit = a k CELL; E3's unit = an
            ARM. Three strings, required unequal.

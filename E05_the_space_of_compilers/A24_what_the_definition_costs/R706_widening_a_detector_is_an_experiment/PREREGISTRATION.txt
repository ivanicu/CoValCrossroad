ESTIMAND
  Three quantities about the WIDENED DETECTOR, before it is allowed to ship:
    (i)  LOSS      — NEXT paragraphs the old extractor found that the new one does not.
    (ii) GAIN      — commits newly visible, in full history and in the gate's own 400-commit window.
    (iii) FLAG RATE on the newly-visible population, against the already-visible one.

IDENTIFICATION
  Fully identified: both extractors are deterministic functions of the commit corpus on disk.
  ⚠ The FLAG RATE comparison is identified only up to the corpus being fixed; it is a property of
  this history, not of NEXT lines in general.

SCOPE
  population : 1270 commits (full history) and the gate's own last-400 window, both reported
  instrument : two regular expressions and the gate's existing `flagged()` predicate, unchanged
               instrument unit = A NEXT PARAGRAPH
               claim unit      = A COMMIT
               ⚠ NOT EQUAL — a commit may carry several; the counts are reported separately and
               the mismatch is what made check #308's own control meaningless.
  baseline   : the gate's documented commit-body flag rate, 37.2%
  regime     : this repository at HEAD

WORLDS
  A CLEAN WIDENING  loss 0, flag rate on the newly visible indistinguishable from the already
                    visible → the 65 are the same kind of object and may be frozen with a reason
  B DIFFERENT OBJECT flag rate differs beyond the null → the newly visible are NOT the same
                    population and freezing them wholesale would launder them
  C NOT A WIDENING  loss > 0 → the new rule drops paragraphs the old one caught, and must not ship

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THIS ROUND EXISTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
R788's NEXT proposed a FORMULATION change: clause ② should compare on A2 against a stated cut
rather than on `q_resolved`, because R787 showed `q` is exactly A2's percentile and R788 showed
`q_resolved` adds a variance term worth −0.2352 that rewards prompt-BLINDNESS. Before adopting
that formulation, the axis it rests on has to be priced. A cut is only as good as the axis's
resolution.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE ARITHMETIC, DERIVED BEFORE MEASURING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D1  THE ADMITTED SET `{arm : A2 > c}` CHANGES EXACTLY AT EACH DISTINCT A2 VALUE, so a cut sweeping
    ℝ produces exactly (#distinct A2) + 1 admitted sets. **DERIVATION.** With R787's 19 distinct
    values that is 20, and it is not a finding — it is what a step function does.
D2  A CUT'S PLATEAU IS THE GAP IT SITS IN. The interval of cut values giving one admitted set has
    width equal to the A2 gap between the two arms bounding it. So "the cut is robustly placed"
    ⟺ "the gap is wide", and **a gap below that pair's own paired MDE is not a distinction this
    design can make.** DERIVATION, and it is what turns D1's count into a measurement.
D3  `var(a − b) = var(a) + var(b) − 2·cov(a, b)` on the same prompts (R768's D2), so the paired
    MDE cannot be predicted from the marginals in either direction. The pairing must be DESTROYED
    to price what it buys. DERIVATION.
D4  #LEVELS ≤ #DISTINCT A2 VALUES, with equality iff every adjacent gap resolves; and #LEVELS ≥ 1.
    **DERIVATION.** The measurement is where in [1, 20] it lands.
    ⚠ AND THE COMPARISON TO `q`'s 4 IS THE POINT. If the ladder is ≈4, then R787's headline
    *"q destroys information, 19 → 4"* is a statement about POINT ESTIMATES and not about
    resolvable distinctions, and **must be downgraded by this round.** The round is designed so
    that its most likely embarrassing outcome falls on my own most recent published claim.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ESTIMANDS (before method)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E1  the pairwise matrix over all C(27,2) = 351 arm pairs: eff = mean per-prompt A2 difference ·
    percentile bootstrap CI over the 968 prompt clusters · paired MDE = ZEFF·sd(diff)/√P ·
    the three-valued verdict of `corebench/report.py`. Cells tested BESIDE cells surviving, with
    the non-survivors counted.
E2  ⭐ THE LADDER: the number of levels the A2 axis resolves, under TWO constructions, both
    reported — (a) ADJACENT: sort by A2, count resolved adjacent gaps, levels = that + 1;
    (b) GREEDY: walk up the sorted arms and open a new level when the arm is resolved against the
    current level's LOWEST member.
E3  ⭐ THE DECISIVE PAIR: `coval_core` − `generic`, with eff, CI, MDE and verdict. This is the
    minimum a cut-based clause ② must deliver: the released core resolvedly above the blind
    baseline. If it is UNRESOLVED, the formulation R788 proposed cannot do its job on this site.
E4  the plateaus: the widest cut interval whose admitted set is stable, and whether any cut
    admitting `coval_core` while excluding `generic` has a plateau wider than that pair's MDE.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IDENTIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E1–E4 are exact functions of the per-prompt A2 vectors, which are on disk.
⚠ PARTIAL, and named because it is the resolution question §4's poison row is about: a bootstrap
over PROMPTS does not resample ANNOTATORS, so its CIs are conditional on the observed annotator
sets. **This round does not declare that impossible — it builds the hierarchical bootstrap as a
specification cell** (resample prompts, then resample each drawn prompt's annotators with
replacement) and reports the ladder under both. The annotator split-half NOISE FLOOR is measured
separately as a floor the prompt-only bootstrap does not contain.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WORLDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A · THE AXIS IS FINER THAN `q`. The ladder has ≥ 8 levels. Then `q`'s collapse to 4 destroys
    distinctions the data supports, R787's headline stands as more than a point-estimate
    statement, and R788's "A2 against a stated cut" is the right formulation because it recovers
    them.
B · THE AXIS IS NO FINER THAN `q`. The ladder has ≤ 5 levels. Then the coarseness is the DATA's,
    not the statistic's; **R787's E2 must be downgraded**; and swapping `q` for a cut buys
    nothing on this axis — it only removes R788's variance term, which is a different reason.
C · THE CORE IS NOT RESOLVEDLY ABOVE THE BLIND BASELINE. `coval_core` − `generic` is UNRESOLVED
    or BELOW RESOLUTION. Then no cut-based clause ② separates the released core from a baseline
    that never reads the prompt, and that is the report whatever the ladder says.

PREDICTION MATRIX
  A -> ladder_adjacent >= 8  AND  coval_core > generic RESOLVED
  B -> ladder_adjacent <= 5  AND  coval_core > generic RESOLVED
  C -> coval_core vs generic NOT resolved (CI contains 0, or |eff| < MDE)
  ladder_adjacent in {6, 7} with the pair resolved -> report; CLAIM NO WORLD. Registered as a gap
  rather than folded into A or B, because a threshold chosen after seeing 6 is a narrative.

KILL -- CONDITIONAL, gated on the controls (a kill that can fire on a broken instrument is an
        automated way to publish an artifact)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
if OBJECT passes (27 arms rebuilt, P == 968, every rebuilt A2 equal to R782's published value to
   1e-9 -- exit 2 otherwise)
   and PLACEBO returns exactly 0.000000 with CI exactly [0, 0] and verdict UNRESOLVED
   and POSITIVE has a NON-DEGENERATE band (at delta = 0 the verdict is NOT resolved -- the floor
       must fail -- and at delta = 0.05 it IS resolved -- the ceiling)
   and NEGATIVE fires (destroying the prompt pairing inflates the MDE by a ratio > 1):
        coval_core vs generic not resolved     -> WORLD C          (checked FIRST; it dominates)
        ladder_adjacent >= 8                   -> WORLD A
        ladder_adjacent <= 5                   -> WORLD B
        otherwise                              -> report; claim no world.

        UNVERIFIED. Never OVERTURNED, never CONFIRMED.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONTROLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OBJECT      27 arms rebuilt from sat files; P printed; each arm's A2 checked against R782's
            published number to 1e-9. Empty population or mismatch -> exit 2, never 0.
PLACEBO     an arm against itself: eff exactly 0.000000, bootstrap CI exactly [0, 0], and the
            verdict must be UNRESOLVED. A contrast where no effect can exist.
POSITIVE    BAND COMPUTED AT BOTH ENDS. Plant a shift `delta` on a copy of an arm and sweep
            delta in {0, 0.002, 0.005, 0.01, 0.02, 0.05}. Require the FLOOR (delta = 0) to be
            UNRESOLVED and the CEILING (delta = 0.05) to be RESOLVED; floor verdict == ceiling
            verdict is DEGENERATE and fails the control. The sweep also reads the empirical MDE.
            ⚠ And a second plant, same mean but with zero-mean per-prompt noise added, so the
            control cannot pass merely by detecting a constant.
NEGATIVE    DESTROY THE PAIRING: permute one arm's prompt order, preserving both marginals.
            THE WORLD IT EXCLUDES, named: "the paired CIs are narrow because of the pairing, not
            because the two arms track prompt by prompt." BUILT SYNTHETICALLY TOO: two arms with
            the same marginals and independent per-prompt draws, where the paired and unpaired
            MDEs must coincide. Inflation ratio reported against R768's 2.25 [2.18, 2.32].
SHAM        the same operation MINUS the ingredient under study, where the ingredient is THE
            SECOND ARM: compare each arm against a FIXED SCALAR cut (the reference class's median
            A2), which has no per-prompt vector and therefore no pairing to exploit.
NEUTRAL     ⚠ §4's poison row, applied in advance: a sham can be a poison rather than a placebo,
            so the NEUTRAL comparison is built too — each arm against the MEDIAN REFERENCE's own
            per-prompt vector, i.e. a second vector that is not a scored arm. The sham gap bounds
            benefit + harm; only the neutral gap isolates what the pairing with a real arm buys.
NOISE FLOOR MEASURED, not assumed: 20 random split-halves of each prompt's annotators; the sd of
            the half-to-half A2 difference is a floor the PROMPT bootstrap does not contain, and
            any arm gap below it is not a distinction.
UNIT        instrument unit = an (arm, arm, prompt) paired difference; claim unit = an ARM PAIR,
            and for E2 the LEVEL. Named as separate strings and required unequal, per §4.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MULTIPLICITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
351 unordered pairs (antisymmetric, so 351 and not 702). BH at q = 0.05 over ALL 351 on the
two-sided bootstrap p, reported BESIDE the verdict-rule count, which adds the MDE floor. Cells
tested, cells surviving each rule, and the non-survivors are all printed. BH's threshold at rank
k is q·k/C -- the largest is q itself; `q/C` would be Bonferroni (R725/§1).

R750 · when a sentence cites several rounds, how many of them actually hold the number?
WRITTEN BEFORE ANY CODE FOR THIS ROUND. 2026-08-05.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THIS ROUND EXISTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
R749 could not resolve claim row 8's object count because the row cites THREE rounds and the
resolver reads whichever appears earliest -- which happens to be the one holding no identity
relation, while another citation in the same group is the round that built the partition. Rotating
which citation is read moved 2 of 5 assignments.

That is a general defect wearing a specific costume. R590 asked whether A cited round holds the
number a sentence states, and found 15 of 19 grounded. It never asked what happens when SEVERAL
rounds are cited together: whether the group jointly supports the number, or whether one member
carries it and the rest are decoration a reader can follow into a round that never computed it.

SEVERITY CHECK, RUN BEFORE COMMITTING TO THE ROUND: the page holds 1,389 sentences, 65 citing >=2
distinct rounds, of which 17 carry a number. Citation-group sizes run 2, 3, 4, 5 and 8. That is a
real population, so the question is measurable here. Had it been n=1 -- which is what the
object-count-only version would have given -- this round would not have been worth running and the
honest move would have been to say so.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ESTIMAND
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For each sentence on STATEMENT.md citing >= 2 distinct rounds and stating a number, the SUPPORT
DEPTH: how many of the cited rounds' own artifacts contain that number.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IDENTIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IDENTIFIED given a matching rule, and the matching rule IS the instrument, so three are swept.

⚠ R590'S DOCUMENTED REPAIR IS REUSED RATHER THAN REDISCOVERED. Its first matcher required the
printed value to be a PREFIX of a stored float, so every value the document rounded UP failed: it
reported 13 orphans of which 9 were its own bug, and rounded matching cut them to 4. R590 has no
run.py -- it is one of the codeless rounds -- so the relation is re-implemented, but the repair is
carried forward and the prefix rule is included ONLY as the loose end of the specification curve,
to show what it costs.

⚠ AND THE UNIT PROBLEM, NAMED BEFORE THE CONTROL IS DESIGNED. Instrument unit = a (sentence,
number, cited round) triple. Claim unit = a SENTENCE a reader acts on. They are not equal: one
sentence can state several numbers. Support depth is therefore computed PER NUMBER and the
per-sentence figure is the maximum over its numbers, with the count of multi-number sentences
reported separately rather than silently averaged.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
population  the 17 sentences on STATEMENT.md citing >=2 rounds and stating a number
instrument  three matchers -- prefix (R590's broken one), rounded (its repair), tolerance
baseline    the SHAM: single-citation numeric sentences, which is R590's own population
regime      the page and the committed artifacts at this tree_sha

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WORLDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A · SHARED SUPPORT. In most groups >= 2 cited rounds hold the number. The group is a genuine
    multi-round grounding and a reader following any member lands somewhere that computed it.
B · DILUTED SUPPORT. In most groups exactly one member holds it, or none. The extra citations are
    decoration; which member a reader follows decides what they find, and R749's row-8 failure is
    an instance of a general property rather than one bad row.

PREDICTION MATRIX -- median support depth over the 17
    A -> >= 2            B -> <= 1

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
P1  BLIND   median support depth, ROUNDED matcher                registered 1    [0, 3]
P2  BLIND   share of groups with support >= 2                    registered 0.35 [0.00, 1.00]
P3  ⚠ PRIOR-ART INFORMED, DECLARED: the SHAM's base rate -- single-citation numeric sentences whose
    one cited round holds the number. R590 measured 15 of 19 = 0.789 on an overlapping population,
    so this is not a blind guess and is not scored as one.
                                                                 registered 0.79 [0.40, 1.00]
P4  BLIND   groups with support EXACTLY 0                        registered 2    [0, 17]

DIRECTIONAL, separately failable
D   support depth does NOT increase with citation-group size: a sentence citing 8 rounds is no
    better grounded than one citing 2. If it DOES increase, large groups are genuine consensus and
    the dilution reading is wrong.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
⛔ THE ARITHMETIC, DERIVED BEFORE MEASURING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FORCED, labelled derivations, not evidence:
  - support <= group size, always. "Support is less than the number of citations" is not a finding;
    only the DISTRIBUTION is.
  - prefix_matches <= rounded_matches is NOT forced in general -- a prefix rule can match a value
    the rounded rule rejects when the stored float has more digits. So P5 could come out either
    sign, and that is why it is registered rather than derived.
  - the SHAM's population and the main population are DISJOINT by construction (one citation versus
    two or more), so no arm shares a sentence with the other and the comparison is between-groups.
    Stated because a shared sentence would make the base rate partly its own comparator.

STRONGEST CONFOUND, written BEFORE the run: a cited round may hold the number in its README rather
than in its artifact, which would read as unsupported while being perfectly documented. CONTROL IN
THE SAME ITERATION: run the identical check against each round's README and report the two
supports side by side, never merged.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
KILL -- CONDITIONAL, gated on the controls
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
if POSITIVE recovers a known-supported synthetic group, and g=0 returns 0 for a number in no
artifact, and NEGATIVE changes the distribution under a rotated citation map, and PLACEBO is
exactly 0:
        median support >= 2  -> WORLD B KILLED. Citation groups are genuine joint grounding and
                                row 8 is one bad row rather than an instance.
        median support <= 1  -> WORLD A KILLED. The group does not ground the number; the page must
                                name WHICH citation computed each figure.
else:
        UNVERIFIED. Never OVERTURNED, never CONFIRMED.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONTROLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
POSITIVE   SYNTHETIC, and built so the answer is known without this instrument: take a value that
           two rounds' artifacts both provably contain (found by direct search, not by the
           matcher), and a fabricated sentence citing exactly those two. Support must be 2.
           BAND, COMPUTED: floor = a matcher that never matches (support 0 on a known-supported
           group), ceiling = the group size, 2. Threshold 2, strictly above the floor, asserted.
g=0        a number present in NO artifact must return support 0 AND be reported, never skipped.
           A skipped zero would raise the median by removing the worst cases.
NEGATIVE   rotate the sentence -> citation-group map. The support distribution must change. World
           excluded: "support is a property of the NUMBER, not of the cited rounds".
SHAM       the same operation with the INGREDIENT ABSENT, not inverted. Ingredient = being cited
           alongside others. Run the identical check on SINGLE-citation numeric sentences. That is
           R590's population and it supplies the base rate; without it a low support depth could
           simply mean the artifacts do not store printed values at all.
PLACEBO    the same sentence scored twice -> exactly 0 difference, reported as 0 of N.
NOISE      no rng. The variance is the MATCHER, and three are swept: prefix / rounded / tolerance.
UNIT       instrument unit = (sentence, number, round) triple; claim unit = sentence. Support is
           computed per NUMBER, the per-sentence value is the MAXIMUM over its numbers, and the
           count of multi-number sentences is printed rather than averaged away.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MULTIPLICITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3 matchers x 2 populations (multi-cited, single-cited) x {median support, share >=2, share ==0}
= 18 reported quantities, plus the README-vs-artifact confound split. All reported.
